## 2. DA Charge Non-Execution — Root Cause Analysis (7+ Consecutive Cycles)

**Confirmed pattern (Aug 27 – Sep 2)**: DA charge at recommended HE10-11 (bid cap ≤$35/MWh) never executed despite DA prices clearing at $23-24/MWh — well below the cap. Actual charging: HE04 DA (-30 MWh at $24.30) and HE05 RT (-49.4 MWh at $25.63/MWh) in Sep 2 actuals.

**Root cause assessment (updated from Sep 3 review)**:

The bid price is not the issue — $23-24/MWh is below any reasonable cap. Three possible root causes:

1. **Pre-loaded alternate schedule conflict (most likely)**: Tenaska dispatch system has a resource plan with a fixed charge block at HE04-05. This schedule was not updated when the optimizer shifted charge to HE10-11. The HE04-05 schedule executes as the primary and the HE10-11 DA bid is either rejected or not submitted to SCED.

2. **HSL constraint mismatch**: The optimizer models HSL=100 MW flat. If the actual Tenaska HSL parameter for charge mode has a different effective ceiling or ramp constraint, the dispatch system may reject the HE10-11 charge bid as infeasible given post-HE04-05 SoC state.

3. **Bid submission gap**: HE10-11 DA charge bids are being submitted correctly to the operations team but not forwarded to DAM. The Sep 3 report shift to HE02-03 with an explicit ≤$26 cap is a test of whether the charge window matters or whether the submission process itself is broken.

**Status of Sep 3 fix (charge shift to HE02-03)**: Unconfirmed. Sep 4 actuals will arrive Sep 6. This is the most important data point to check when Tenaska PnL becomes available.

**If HE02-03 still fails**: The non-execution is a bid submission process failure (bids not reaching DAM), not a charge window or price issue. Requires Tenaska operations to provide confirmation of submitted DA schedule for each problem day.

## 3. AS+Energy Overlap Failure Pattern

**Pattern summary**: HE19 has shown simultaneous DA energy discharge (50 MW) and NonSpin (85 MW) in Sep 2 actuals (and Aug 31 catastrophically at -$25,691 RT loss). The optimizer zeroes AS MW explicitly for any energy-active hour. The overlap originates in the bid submission step, not the optimization.

**Root cause (confirmed)**: The trading desk submits AS offers from a separate template covering HE13-20 as a block. This template is not zeroed at energy hours before DAM submission. The optimizer's zero-AS instruction for HE19 is not being applied at the submission layer.

**Sep 2 impact**: -$2,227 RT imbalance (BESS physically unable to serve both 50 MW DA discharge and 85 MW NS simultaneously; RT buyback at $43.79/MWh). This is the third confirmed occurrence.

**Fix required**: A pre-submission validation script must flag any HE where `energy_MW != 0 AND AS_MW != 0` and block submission until resolved. This is a one-time build that eliminates a recurring $2,000-26,000 per-cycle loss. The Sep 3 recommendation explicitly noted this requirement; no implementation confirmation exists.

**Escalation status**: Formal escalation filed Sep 2 (Action 2 per `learnings/2026-09-02.md`). No response confirmed as of Sep 5.
