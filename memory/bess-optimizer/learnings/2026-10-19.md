- Assessment: The logic was high-conviction and directionally correct. HE12 DA energy at $17.96 is genuinely the cheapest charge point when the duck is EXTREME. The DA lock-in at $83.58 was clearly the right venue given the large spread.

The strategy was the right structure; the revenue figure is directionally meaningful but not precisely verified.
