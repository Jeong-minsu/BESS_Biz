## 4. DA-RT Spread Call (2026-09-17 Actuals as Reference)

**Convention reminder**: spread = DA − RT; positive = DA expensive = short DA / long RT signal.

### Energy DA-RT Call for Sep 18

My recommendation called DA-commitment-heavy at discharge (HE19-20) based on two signals:
1. Sep 16 showed RT >> DA at HE19-21 ($103-225/MWh RT vs ~$61 DA) — negative spread (RT expensive) → DA short was wrong in HE19-21 for Sep 16
2. Sep 17 confirmed RT spike at HE18-19: RTSPP HE18 $83.39, HE19 $92.53 — negative spread again
3. Correct response: commit to DA at HE19-20 to lock in price and avoid RT imbalance, regardless of DA-RT direction

The DA-commit strategy is directionally correct regardless of whether DA > RT or RT > DA, because it eliminates imbalance risk. The key insight incorporated from Sep 17: any physical dispatch in RT that is not hedged with a matching DA position creates a large imbalance cost when RT spikes. The recommendation explicitly stated "set DA bid = physical capability eliminates imbalance risk entirely."
