## 3. SoC Management Assessment

**Aug 24 schedule**: Charge HE09 (−100 MW), HE10 (−50 MW), partial HE24 (−50 MW). Discharge HE20 (+100 MW), HE21 (+100 MW).

**Issue identified**: Two consecutive 100 MW discharge hours (HE20 + HE21) with 200 MWh starting SoC would deliver 100 + 100 = 200 MWh to grid, but actual usable energy at 92.2% one-way efficiency = 200 × 0.922 = 184 MWh. If SoC at start of discharge = 200 MWh, then:
- HE20: −108.5 MWh used → SoC = 91.5 MWh remaining
- HE21: at 100 MW → −108.5 MWh needed, but only 91.5 MWh available → **SoC violation**

HE21 should have been capped at 84 MW to respect the SoC limit. The Aug 24 recommendation did not include this correction — it assumed full 100 MW at HE21. This is a SoC accounting error in the recommendation. Applied correction in Aug 25 recommendation (HE21 = +84 MW).

**Action item**: Always verify SoC feasibility for discharge hours: HE21 max MW = remaining SoC × η_d = (200 − 108.5) × 0.922 = 84 MW.
