## 3. Largest Prediction Misses

**Miss 1 — DA Energy sell did not execute (largest miss: -$6,930 on this line)**  
The recommendation assumed 200 MWh of DA energy sold at HE20-21 ($41.08/$44.65). In actuality, GKS had zero DA sell activity; all discharge settled in RT. This is a continuation of the same venue mismatch documented in prior self-reviews (2026-06-05 learnings, plans/2026-22-da-rt-venue-follow-up.md). The DA sell framework materially overstates expected DA energy revenue in every cycle where GKS dispatches in RT. The revenue is recovered via RT, but the DA-RT revenue accounting line gets reshuffled. Net impact was partially offset by RT energy actual (+$4,113 received vs $0 modeled).

**Miss 2 — RRS not offered, but $648 was available (cost: -$648)**  
The recommendation explicitly excluded RRS in all hours, based on the logic that NonSpin prices dominate RRS prices at peak hours. However, HE20-24 RRS cleared at 90 MW and generated $648 — during the same hours GKS was apparently not discharging (RT injection was concentrated earlier). RRS during overnight hours when SOC is not being consumed represents an incremental revenue layer we passed on. The logic "NonSpin dominates RRS" assumed RRS and NonSpin compete for the same capacity, but if GKS can offer both simultaneously from idle SOC, missing this layer is an avoidable loss.

**Miss 3 — NonSpin revenue underestimated (+$1,621 surprise)**  
We projected $486 in NonSpin revenue (50 MW × 17 hours at $0.45-0.97/MWh avg). Actual was $2,107. Two factors explain the gap: (a) GKS cleared 90 MW NonSpin (we offered 50 MW — actual MW was 80% higher), and (b) 19 of 24 hours cleared vs our 17-hour expectation. The higher MW clearing likely reflects Tenaska submitting at the full HSL (90 MW) rather than our conservative 50 MW assumption.
