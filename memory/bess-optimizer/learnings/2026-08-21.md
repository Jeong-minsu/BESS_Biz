## 3. Root Cause Analysis

**Primary failure: RT spike did NOT materialize at HE20-21.**

The recommendation's core thesis was: NL 70,636 MW at HE20 → ORDC activation → RT >> DA by $15-30+/MWh → DA offer ≥$150/MWh ensures non-clearing → full RT revenue at $100/MWh P50.

**What actually happened:**
- DASPP HE20: $41.06/MWh
- RTSPP HE20: $41.27/MWh → spread = -$0.21/MWh (DA ≈ RT, essentially flat)
- DASPP HE21: $36.72/MWh
- RTSPP HE21: $36.79/MWh → spread = -$0.07/MWh (flat again)
- 24h average DA-RT spread: +$0.44/MWh (nearly flat all day)

The RT spike failed because:
a) **Evening wind recovery was likely strong**: Aug 21 (Friday WDPEAK) saw wind ramping in the evening — the congestion analyst's data for Aug 21 implied GR_WEST HE13 min was 1,755 MW but then recovered. Strong evening wind provides the supply buffer that prevents RT scarcity.
b) **Heat wave Day 3 demand response**: By Day 3, ERCOT demand response (DRRS) programs may have trimmed effective peak load, reducing scarcity probability below ORDC trigger.
c) **The RT >> DA assumption is conditional**: The recommendation's Aug 16 anchor (confirmed RT > DA) was based on NL 67,918 MW (series record at that time) with weaker wind. Aug 21 had LOWER effective scarcity because wind recovered earlier in the evening.

**Secondary finding: Ops team ran a fundamentally different strategy.**

The actual stack was:
- NonSpin 90 MW HE01-07 (NOT 15 MW as recommended — ops had higher actual SoC)
- DA sales HE12-18 (partial discharge HE12-18, not in recommendation)
- RT charging HE10-12 (not DA charging HE09-10 as recommended)
- DA load + RT discharge at HE20-22 (partial DA commitment = hybrid, not pure RT-long)
- ECRS HE20-24 ($1,781 — not in recommendation)

This is consistent with the Aug 22 recommendation style (ECRS co-optimization), not the Aug 20 recommendation style (pure RT-long). Ops correctly identified ECRS as the primary revenue driver and avoided the RT gamble.

**Third factor: DA charge execution gap (recurring).**

The recommendation called for DA_CHARGE HE09-10. The actual data shows RT charging at HE10-12 (RTSPP $21-23/MWh) instead of DA charging (DA $18.95-20.92/MWh at those hours). RT charging at $21-23 vs DA at $19-21 = ~$2/MWh more expensive charge, AND the charge happened one hour later (HE10-12 vs HE09-10). This recurring execution gap (confirmed in 4+ prior cycles) cost approximately:
- Extra charge cost: 100 MW × 2h × $2/MWh = $400
- Lower estimated impact: modest (compare to $10,265 total miss)

The execution gap contributed ~$400 but was not the primary cause of the shortfall.

**Revised insight for spread modeling**:
- RT > DA at peak hours is conditional on BOTH high net load AND low evening wind
- When wind recovery is strong (> ~8k MW at HE20-22), RT stays ≈ DA even at high NL
- Future spread calls must assess both NL AND wind recovery at HE20-22 before committing to RT-long
