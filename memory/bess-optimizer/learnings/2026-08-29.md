## 3. Root Cause Analysis

### Finding 1: Day 10 RT Prices Were POSITIVE at HE10-11 (CT), Not Deeply Negative

**Critical insight**: RT_Energy_Amt = -$1,524 at HE10 (CT). If the unit was charging 100MW in RT, the cost was $15.24/MWh RT price. This means RT prices at HE10 on Day 10 (NL = 28,394 MW) were approximately $12-16/MWh POSITIVE — NOT negative as expected from the duck curve.

**Cross-check**: Aug 30 market view comparison table states Day 10 (Aug 28) DA HE10 estimate was "$-60 to $-15/MWh." However actual RT was approximately +$15/MWh, implying:
- DA-RT spread at HE10 = DA - RT ≈ -$30 - (+$15) = -$45/MWh (RT far more expensive than DA)
- The AVERAGE daily spread of +$5.66 masks extreme intra-day variation

**The pattern**: DA was deeply negative at HE10 (around -$20 to -$40/MWh per the Aug 30 comparison table), but RT was POSITIVE ($12-16/MWh) because:
(a) At 28,394 MW net load, Day 10 is still above ERCOT's practical negative-clearing floor in RT (RT SCED clears more frequently and can stay positive even when DA goes negative in a trough this shallow vs the Day 11 expected 19,918 MW)
(b) RT re-dispatch and intra-hour imbalances at 28 GW NL are not as extreme as they would be at 20 GW NL

**Implication for Aug 28 strategy**: The unit charged in RT at HE09-12 at POSITIVE prices (+$4-15/MWh) while DA was likely negative (-$15 to -$40/MWh). This is backwards — the unit should have had a DA charge schedule and NOT charged in RT.

**Learning 1 — DA vs RT Charge Venue (CRITICAL)**: For duck curve days, always secure charge in DA (not RT). RT charging at midday is not free just because DA is negative — RT can clear positive even when DA clears negative, especially at NL troughs above 25 GW. For NL trough 19,918 MW (Day 11), RT is more likely to match or exceed DA negative depth; but the lesson holds: secure DA charge commitment first.

### Finding 2: Charge Cost Exceeded Discharge Revenue — Round-Trip Efficiency Loss at Low Spreads

**Charge cost** (HE09-12): $395 + $1,524 + $1,408 + $418 = **$3,745**
**Discharge revenue** (HE13, HE24): $2,067 + $809 = **$2,876**

Net energy (charge+discharge): $2,876 - $3,745 = **-$869** (energy net loss)

Average charge price paid (RT): ~($3,745 / 400MWh) = $9.36/MWh
Average discharge price received (RT): ~($2,876 / 200MWh) = $14.38/MWh

Gross spread before RTE: $14.38 - $9.36 = $5.02/MWh
After RTE (85%): Effective output = 200MWh × 0.85 = 170MWh; adjusted spread = ($2,876 - $3,745×0.85) = $2,876 - $3,183 = -$307 net (energy loss on arbitrage cycle alone before ECRS)

**Learning 2 — Minimum Spread Threshold**: At HE10-11 RT prices of +$15/MWh and HE13-24 discharge at $15-20/MWh, the round-trip spread ($5/MWh gross) is insufficient to cover 15% RTE efficiency loss. The minimum gross spread required for break-even at 85% RTE = approximately ($charge_cost / discharge_MWh) = requires discharge price to be at least 1/(0.85) × charge price = 1.18x charge price. At $15 charge → need $17.6 discharge minimum. Day 10 achieved $14.38 discharge avg → below breakeven.

**For Day 11 (Aug 30)**: Charge at expected -$80 to -$150/MWh (price RECEIVED for charging, not paid), discharge at +$45-80/MWh. The spread is vastly larger and highly favorable. No break-even concern.

## 5. DA-RT Spread Call Review

**Day 10 spread**: Average +$5.66/MWh (DA expensive). However, the sign was misleading:
- DA was negative at midday (HE10 est. -$20 to -$40) while RT was positive (~+$15)
- This created an intraday spread of DA-RT ≈ -$50 at HE10 (DA much cheaper than RT)
- But evening hours had DA > RT (standard summer pattern), which dominated the daily average positive

**Learning 4 — Intraday Spread Asymmetry**: A daily average DA-RT spread of +$5.66/MWh does NOT mean midday spread is also positive. Duck curve days show INVERTED intraday spread: DA very negative at midday (DA cheap) while RT may clear positive → spread = DA - RT very negative at midday. This creates a strong "charge in DA, discharge in RT" signal at midday — exactly opposite of what occurred (the unit charged in RT, not DA). ALWAYS treat midday duck curve hours as: DA bid (not RT charging) for the charge position.
