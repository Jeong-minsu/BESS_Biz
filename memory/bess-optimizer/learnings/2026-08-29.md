## 3. Root Cause Analysis

### Finding 1 (CRITICAL): DA Discharge Executed at HE14/HE17 Instead of Recommended HE20-21

**Evidence**: Settlement shows DA_Energy = +$3,848 at HE14 and +$4,015 at HE17. Recommended DA discharge was HE20 (est. $55-95/MWh) and HE21 (est. $25-55/MWh). Actual prices at HE14 = $38.48/MWh and HE17 = $40.15/MWh — within recommendation ranges for those hours but NOT the target hours.

**Revenue impact**: Had discharge occurred at HE20-21 via DA:
- HE20 RT actual = +$1,580 → RT price ≈ $15.80/MWh only. The evening RT prices were moderate (Day 10 NL 65,377 MW, wind 12,478 MW provided buffer). DA at $55-95/MWh midpoint $75 would have yielded +$7,500 vs the actual RT $1,580 → +$5,920 more at HE20.
- HE21 similarly: +$7,500 vs +$1,576 → +$5,924 more.
- Total incremental from timing correction alone: ~+$11,844 vs actual.

The bidding agent or execution layer appears to have submitted DA discharge offers at HE14/HE17 rather than HE20-21. This is the single most consequential failure of the heat wave series.

**Action**: Ensure DA gen offer is filed ONLY at HE20-21 for Aug 31. Add explicit "do not discharge HE14-18" note to tomorrow's schedule.

### Finding 2: RT Charging at HE08-09 Instead of DA Charge at HE10-11

**Evidence**: RT_Energy_Amt = −$163 at HE08 and −$231 at HE09. If the unit charged 100 MW at these hours, implied RT prices were +$1.63 and +$2.31/MWh. The recommendation was to charge in DA at HE10-11 at expected −$60 to −$15/MWh. No DA charge at HE10-11 is reflected in the settlement.

**Revenue impact**: Missed DA charge credit at HE10-11 (est. $1,500-3,500). Paid a small positive price for charging at HE08-09 instead of receiving a negative price.

**Pattern vs Aug 28**: Exactly analogous to Aug 28's finding (RT charging at positive prices when DA negative). The fix required — DA charge bid at HE10-11 — has not been implemented as of Aug 29.

### Finding 3: Large RT Imbalance at HE14/HE17 Suggests Counter-Dispatch

**Evidence**: HE14 RT = −$2,344 and HE17 RT = −$2,703 while having 100 MW DA discharge scheduled. Possible explanations:
(a) Unit physically could not discharge 100 MW (SoC insufficient) → had to charge in RT to cover DA position (settlement imbalance).
(b) ERCOT SCED dispatched unit to charge (AS deployment of RegDown or automatic down-regulation).
(c) Unit operator manually counter-dispatched.

Net result: For each hour, the battery received DA revenue ($3,848 + $4,015) but simultaneously paid RT charging cost ($2,344 + $2,703). Net DA+RT energy revenue for these two hours: ($3,848 + $4,015) − ($2,344 + $2,703) = +$2,816. Vs. if unit had simply delivered 100 MW DA at both hours with no imbalance: +$7,863.

**Implication**: The starting SoC assumption may have been wrong. If battery entered HE14 with SoC insufficient to discharge 100 MWh (less than 100 MWh), the counter-dispatch is forced. This is consistent with no DA charge having been executed at HE10-11 (battery stayed at ~50 MWh starting SoC through midday).

### Finding 4: RT Revenue at HE19-21 Partially Salvaged the Day

The actual RT revenue at HE19-21 totaling $3,954 shows the battery was generating in real-time (without a DA schedule) during those hours. This is the correct behavioral response to the evening price spike — the unit was physically dispatching into high-price RT. However:
- RT clearing at HE20 = $1,580 / 100 MW ≈ $15.80/MWh. This is far below the DA estimate ($55-95/MWh), indicating the HE20 evening spike on Day 10 was MODERATE, not extreme. Wind 12,478 MW at HE20 (vs Day 12 forecast 16,101 MW) provided adequate buffer.
- The RT-only strategy captured only $3,954 vs a full DA strategy at HE20-21 that would have captured $7,500+ at DA midpoint.

### Finding 5: ECRS/RegUp/RRS Clearing Rates Very Low on Day 10 (Saturday)

Total ECRS = $599 for 850 MW-hrs (avg $0.705/MW-hr). Saturday (Day 10 = Day of week discount) with lower AS demand explains the unusually low clearing. RegUp, Reg Down, RRS all cleared at $0.00 — the products either weren't bid or bids were above market.

**Implication for Monday Aug 31**: Monday WDPEAK should have meaningfully higher AS clearing rates (2-4x Saturday baseline). The $599 Saturday benchmark is the floor; realistic Monday expectation is $1,500-4,000+ for ECRS alone at elevated contingency rates.
