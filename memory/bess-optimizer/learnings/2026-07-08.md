## 4. Root Cause Analysis

### 4a. Rule 9 Not Applied — NS/RRS Structure Gap

The 7/8 recommendation used 100 MW NS flat (old pattern) instead of 70 MW NS + 70 MW RRS switch (Rule 9). Impact:
- Recommendation effectively had $0 RRS and 100 MW NS for 17 hours
- If Smartbidder did switch to RRS at HE20-24 (per Rule 9 structural observation), the NS shortfall at HE20-24 is already baked in. But the recommendation's revenue table overstated NS by 30 MW × 4h × ~$1.50 = $180 (minor).
- More importantly: no RRS was included in expected revenue. If RRS cleared HE20-24 at ~$1.76/MWh (7/6 precedent), missed $620 from recommendation.

### 4b. SoC Terminal = 0 MWh (Below Minimum 20 MWh)

The 7/8 SoC math: "100 + 100 (HE10) - 117.6 (HE21) - 82.4 (HE22) = 0 MWh terminal." Operating range per standing rules is 20-190 MWh (10-95% DoD). Terminal SoC of 0 MWh violates the 20 MWh minimum. The recommendation should have limited HE22 discharge to leave 20 MWh minimum, reducing HE22 from 70 MW to approximately 59 MW (80 MWh remaining SoC - 20 min = 60 MWh × 0.933 = 56 MW, or with rounding 59 MW).

At full 200 MWh SoC (100 + 100 MW charge): 
- HE21 discharge 100 MW → depletes 100/0.933 = 107.2 MWh → SoC = 200 - 107.2 = 92.8 MWh
- HE22 available (above 20 min): 92.8 - 20 = 72.8 MWh → grid inject = 72.8 × 0.933 = 67.9 ≈ 68 MW (not 70 MW)
The 70 MW choice would deplete to 200 - 107.2 - 75 = 17.8 MWh — just under the 20 MWh minimum. Minor but should be corrected.

### 4c. Unplanned RT Charging — Structural Smartbidder Behavior

Daytime unplanned RT charging (-$2,049 vs benchmark energy) is now confirmed on 7/4, 7/6, and 7/8. Pattern: Smartbidder RT SCED layer independently charges during solar trough hours (HE09-16) when RT prices are cheapest, regardless of DA charge schedule. This is a systematic behavior that the bess-optimizer cannot control directly. The recommendation's "DA Energy: $0 RT" assumption structurally underestimates RT imbalance costs.

**Correction**: Add explicit risk line in every report: "RT charging during HE09-16 solar trough is a structural Smartbidder behavior (confirmed 3 cycles: 7/4, 7/6, 7/8). Expected additional RT charge cost: -$300 to -$800 vs DA plan. Build this into conservative estimate."

### 4d. No ECRS Offered Despite Summer Scarcity Rule (Rule 1)

The 7/8 recommendation had $0 ECRS despite Rule 1 (ECRS mandatory HE20-24 during summer). The 7/8 ECRS DA signal was lower than 7/9's $2.81, but the rule requires inclusion unless SoC insufficient. This is a Rule 1 violation — ECRS should have been offered at 15-20 MW alongside energy bids.
