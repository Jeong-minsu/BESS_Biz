## 5. Key Learnings

### Learning 1 — Process failure WITH available data is worse than process failure WITHOUT data

The Oct 1-5 gap had an arguable excuse: all major data sources (Yes Energy, Smartbidder, Tenaska) were unavailable. The Oct 7 gap has no excuse: Yes Energy was successfully fetched on Oct 7 (confirmed by the Oct 8 market view header). The orchestration workflow did not trigger despite at least one primary input being available. This means there is a workflow logic deficiency beyond the "DEGRADED fallback" fix — the cycle must trigger unconditionally on the daily timer, not conditionally on data availability checks.

**Corrective action**: The orchestration workflow must be redesigned so that the bess-optimizer cycle fires unconditionally at 07:30 CT. Input status (LIVE / DEGRADED / ABSENT) affects output quality flags, not whether the cycle executes. A structural heuristic recommendation under any combination of DEGRADED inputs is superior to no recommendation.

### Learning 2 — Oct 7 structural conditions were likely SEVERE duck, consistent with Oct 8

The multi-day atmospheric persistence argument (solar clarity + wind drought + stable outage pattern) suggests Oct 7 had a SEVERE duck profile closely matching Oct 8. The Oct 6 DEGRADED brief (which estimated MODERATE conditions for Oct 6) would have been significantly more accurate for Oct 7 had it used live Yes Energy data. The gap in market view generation for Oct 7 compounded the recommendation gap — even if the optimizer had fired, it would have had no fresh market view to work with.

**Corrective action**: Market view generation must also be fail-open. If Yes Energy is available, generate the market view regardless of other source status. If No sources are available, generate a structural market view from the most recent live anchor (Oct 8 becomes the anchor for forward degraded cycles now).
