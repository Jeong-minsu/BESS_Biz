2. **ERCOT demand response auto-dispatch**: Less likely given the pattern (repeated HE09-11 solar hours) — ERCOT DR would be less predictable in hour selection.
3. **Settlement artifact**: Least likely — Tenaska data quality is PRODUCTION confirmed.

### Impact assessment:

- HE15-17 RT: -$2,079 (expected settlement offset for DA sales — not a loss per se)
