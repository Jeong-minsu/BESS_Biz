## 5. Key Delta Observations

### Delta 1 — Single-Source Forecast Underestimated Duck Curve Depth Range

The Aug 29 forecast was filed with Enverus ABSENT (single-source only). The YE NL trough of 28,394 MW at HE10 was used as the sole anchor. The preceding day (Aug 30) had Enverus available and showed a cross-source gap of ~4,944 MW (extreme case — Sunday WEPEAK SEVERE-to-EXTREME scenario). For Aug 29 Saturday, this information was not available at filing time.

In retrospect, had Enverus been available for the Aug 28 cycle, the cross-source gap could have been assessed. Given the Saturday WEPEAK day type (typically shallower duck curve than Sunday due to higher afternoon load), the single-source YE reading of 28,394 MW likely carried ±1,200-2,000 MW uncertainty (standard single-source range). The -$1.0 to -$2.5/MWh E[GKS MCC] range correctly acknowledges this breadth.

**Learning**: When Enverus is absent and YE NL trough is 28,000-30,000 MW (upper MODERATE zone), the P50/P90 range should explicitly widen to span -$0.5 to -$3.5/MWh rather than a centered -$1.0 to -$2.5/MWh to reflect the full single-source uncertainty cone.

### Delta 2 — GR_WEST Recovery Trajectory Validation

The Aug 28 forecast (for Aug 29) stated: "overnight Aug 28-29 wind may recover toward LOW-MEDIUM territory."

Actual Aug 29: HE01 GR_WEST = 7,608 MW (SCI 0.254 — LOW zone). HE23-24: 11,532-12,573 MW (SCI 0.384-0.419 — MEDIUM zone).

**Assessment**: Early overnight (HE01) recovery was lower than the "LOW-MEDIUM" label might imply (SCI 0.254 is solidly LOW, not approaching MEDIUM). The MEDIUM zone was reached only in HE22-24, not HE01-06. This repeats the pattern identified in the Aug 27 self-review (learnings/2026-08-27.md, Delta 3): early overnight rebound after multi-day suppression arrives ~one diurnal cycle later than late-night momentum suggests.

For Aug 29 specifically: the GR_WEST rebound timing was correctly captured in the HE22-24 window (as the Aug 28 forward watch stated "overnight Aug 29 wind may recover"). The magnitude at HE24 = 12,573 MW was within the stated forecast range (10,000-14,000 MW in the Aug 28 forward watch).

**Learning**: Confirmed — Aug 27 learnings/Delta 3 is now validated by a second independent cycle (Aug 28 HE22-24 leads to Aug 29 HE22-24 MEDIUM, not HE01-06). The pattern holds with two data points.

### Delta 3 — WEST_TO_NORTH Window Timing: Late-Night vs Overnight

The Aug 29 forecast called WEST_TO_NORTH HE22-24 as the primary binding window. This is correct. However, the Aug 30 outlook filed the very next day shows WEST_TO_NORTH HE01-08 as the dominant overnight signal (8-hour MEDIUM window, P 12-20%). The transition from Aug 29 HE22-24 MEDIUM to Aug 30 HE01-08 MEDIUM represents a continuation of the same wind surge — it does not restart.

**Learning**: When HE22-24 shows GR_WEST in MEDIUM zone (SCI > 0.35) and wind is directionally building into HE24, the D+1 forecast for the next cycle should treat HE01-06 as HIGH probability to also see MEDIUM-zone SCI, not as an independent calculation. The late-night-to-early-overnight transition is physically continuous. The Aug 29 → Aug 30 handoff demonstrates this: Aug 29 HE24 SCI 0.419 → Aug 30 HE01 SCI 0.370. The continuity is directionally maintained even though the magnitude dropped slightly.

**Action**: When WEST_TO_NORTH HE24 is in MEDIUM zone (SCI > 0.35), set HE01-03 of D+1 P(binding) to at least LOW-MEDIUM (equal to or only 3-5 ppt below HE24), not independently derived from zero-base.

### Delta 4 — HOUSTON_SOUTH_MIDDAY Tight-Margin Risk Flag (Not Acted On)

Cap-out at 9,419 MW was 581 MW below the 10,000 MW dual-trigger. This was the smallest cap-out gap in the Aug 2026 Stage 0 series. The forecast correctly noted this as the "smallest Aug 2026 gap" but did not escalate P(binding) above LOW 6-9%.

In the Aug 27 self-review (learnings/2026-08-27.md, Delta 1), a similar tight-margin issue was noted for NL ramp — a recommendation was made to upgrade from NEGLIGIBLE to VERY LOW when margin < 800 MW. By analogy, the cap-out margin of 581 MW on Aug 29 should have triggered a similar upgrade protocol.

**Learning**: Extend the tight-margin protocol from NL ramp to cap-out: when cap-out gap to 10,000 MW dual-trigger is below 700 MW (single-source), upgrade HOUSTON_SOUTH_MIDDAY from LOW to LOW 8-12% with explicit tail note.
