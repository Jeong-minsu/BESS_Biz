If all three confirm binding at T+2 settlement, this will establish a new calibration anchor: a **persistent-binding heat wave week** is one where every WDPEAK NL ramp exceeds the floor, regardless of day-to-day variation. This argues for a future feature: "heat wave day count" (consecutive WDPEAK days above binding floor) as a prior-probability uplift signal.

### 4b. SEVERE Duck Curve on a Thursday: A New Calibration Point

The Jul 31 NL trough of 23,784 MW is the deepest Thursday WDPEAK trough in Stage 0 history. Prior SEVERE events were:
- Jul 9 (Wednesday, 18,030 MW — EXTREME tier; series record)
- Jul 27 (Monday, 21,706 MW — EXTREME borderline)
- Jul 4 (Saturday, ~23,278 MW — SEVERE)
- **Jul 31 (Thursday, 23,784 MW — SEVERE; this forecast)**

Thursday WDPEAK days have historically had the highest loads of the week (load peaks ~2,000-4,000 MW above Wednesday midweek), which counteracts the solar-driven duck curve. The Jul 31 result — SEVERE duck curve ON the highest-load day of the week — demonstrates that when July solar exceeds 30 GW and load peaks at 92 GW, even peak-load Thursday days produce SEVERE NL troughs. Stage 1 feature set should include day-of-week × peak-solar interaction.
