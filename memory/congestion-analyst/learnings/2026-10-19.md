## 4. Three Actionable Insights

### Insight 1: Tyba DA/RT spread polarity is a binding-regime classifier, not just a corroborator — formalize as a Stage 1 feature

**Observation**: Oct 18 (Sunday WEPEAK, DUCK EXTREME dominant) showed large DA >> RT in the evening (DA $83-84 at HE19, spread +$20+). Oct 20 (Tuesday WDPEAK, SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT dominant) showed persistent RT > DA across HE17-21 (RT above DA by $5-9, P(RT>DA) 0.54-0.58). These are two structurally opposite Tyba signal patterns on days with comparable solar magnitude.

The key insight: **DA >> RT in the high-demand window suggests the constraint cleared in DAM at a premium (DAM congestion); RT > DA suggests the constraint is clearing in RTM (RTM congestion or supply tightness not priced in DAM).** Both can be associated with binding constraints, but the affected markets (DA vs RT dispatch) differ, which drives different BESS optimal strategies.

**Stage 1 action**: Add `tyba_evening_spread_sign` (binary: DA>RT or RT>DA in HE17-21) and `tyba_p_rtgt_da_max_evening` (max P(RT>DA) in HE17-21) as features in the Stage 1 LightGBM binding classifier. Hypothesis: DA >> RT signals DAM binding (positive shadow price visible in DAM); RT > DA signals RTM binding (or near-miss cleared late). Validate once settlement data recovers.
