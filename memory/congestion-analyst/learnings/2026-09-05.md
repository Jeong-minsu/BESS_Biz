## 2. SCI-Proxy Method — Identified Limitations

### Limitation 1: Single-Day Bridge Method Vulnerable to Regime Transitions

The Sep 5 overnight wind prediction used Sep 4 HE24 (21,398 MW) as a one-day bridge to Sep 5 HE01. This method implicitly assumes overnight wind regime continuity. The Sep 6 data (8,963 MW HE01 — a 58% drop from Sep 4 HE24) shows the method fails when a regime transition occurs around the forecast day. The 5-day Series pattern (Sep 1-4 all MEDIUM-SCI overnight) created a recency bias toward continued high overnight wind.

**Improvement rule**: When the ZERO-SOURCE cycle is used, weight the bridge assumption by a 2-day rolling average of HE01 readings rather than a single prior-day HE24. If only one anchor is available, widen the SCI range by ±0.05 (vs the standard ±0.02-0.03).

### Limitation 2: Zone Ratio Staleness Accelerates During Low-Wind Transitions

The 55% GR_WEST ratio was calibrated from Sep 3 (YES_ENERGY single source). The Sep 2026 high-overnight-wind period (Sep 1-4) was likely favorable to a 55% West zone concentration. As total ERCOT overnight wind drops from 21,000 MW to 8,963 MW level, the zone ratio itself may shift materially — lower total wind may involve different geographical distribution. This introduces compounding uncertainty on SCI estimates during wind-down transitions.

**Improvement rule**: When total ERCOT overnight wind is estimated below 12,000 MW (vs the Sep 1-4 range of 20,000-22,500 MW), note that the 55% zone ratio has no calibration support at those levels and expand the zone ratio range to 45-65%.

### Limitation 3: ZERO-SOURCE Cycle NL Trough Uncertainty Documented but Under-Applied
