## 5. CONGESTION_PROJECT Stage 0 — Improvement Points from This Cycle

**Finding 1: Solar taper onset timing error (actionable at Stage 1)**

The evening constraint trigger was modeled as activating at HE19-22 CT when net load ramps above 9,000 MW (HE18→HE21 ramp per forecast). But the realized dispatch shows GKS dispatching at RT premium in HE14-15 CT, when solar starts to collapse rapidly. The net load ramp model anchors to the wrong reference hour. The actual trigger appears to be: when cap_out (solar-adjusted thermal headroom) starts declining AND solar MW drops more than X% from its daytime peak. Need to track the solar collapse rate as a trigger variable, not just the net load level.

**Finding 2: Overnight NS pattern is a proxy constraint-binding signal (actionable now)**

The $440 DA_NS_Amt at HE22 CT (UTC 02:00Z) and $300 at HE23 CT is anomalous for overnight hours (RTSPP ~$25-27). This pattern — high NS settlement at low RTSPP — is a proxy indicator that ERCOT priced NS scarcity due to a constraint binding that created locational stress. Going forward, prior-day overnight NS settlement profile (DA_NS clearing by hour) should be cross-referenced against WEST_TO_NORTH and PANHANDLE signals. This could be a leading indicator even before hub-pair LMP data is available.

**Finding 3: HE21 CT (UTC 01:00Z) as a distinct constraint window**

The HE21 RT_Energy spike of +$1,907 (60.9 MW discharge at $31.32 RTSPP on a Saturday overnight) was not explicitly forecast. The WEST_TO_NORTH window was called as HE22-24 CT, but the actual RT discharge happened at HE21 (one hour earlier). The SCI-based trigger at HE24 (SCI 0.426) did not capture the HE21-onset dynamic. Future West-to-North calls should extend the binding window to HE20-24 on high-SCI days rather than HE22-24.

**Finding 4: DA_Energy_Amt source discrepancy note**

The task brief stated DA_Energy_Amt = -$5,124.11 but the raw JSON sums to -$4,124.11 (a $1,000 difference). This discrepancy was noted in the data quality flags. The raw file value (-$4,124.11) is used throughout this review. Source of the $1,000 discrepancy should be clarified with pnl-manager in the next cycle.

**Finding 5: W3 item 0.10 urgency reinforced**

This cycle is the 31st consecutive cycle without hub-pair LMP backfill. The timing error identified in Finding 1 (solar taper onset 4-5 hours early) cannot be calibrated without actual hub-pair price data. The $1,907 overnight RT spike also cannot be linked to a specific constraint without hub-pair West vs Houston price separation. Priority remains: unblock W3 item 0.10 (hub/zone 15-min LMP). It would have directly resolved three of the four assessment ambiguities in section 1.
