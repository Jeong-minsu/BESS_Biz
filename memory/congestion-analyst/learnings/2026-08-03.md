## 4. Learning Points

### L1. Day-over-day West wind step-change (Aug 3→Aug 4): +4,374 MW overnight surge

GR_WEST HE02: Aug 3 = 10,189 MW (SCI 0.345) → Aug 4 = 14,563 MW (SCI 0.492). A +4,374 MW step-change in 24 hours. This level of intra-week variability (+43%) is not captured by any current Stage 0 feature — the daily forecast depends entirely on the YE bidclose GR_WEST values. Stage 1 implication: overnight GR_WEST trajectory (D-2 to D-1 trend) would be a useful feature to flag potential step-changes. Wind model ensemble spread (Enverus would help here if available) should be the highest-priority single data addition for Stage 1.

### L2. Duck curve escalation pattern (Aug 2 NEGLIGIBLE → Aug 3 MODERATE → Aug 4 SEVERE)

Over three consecutive days, NL trough escalated: 32,360 MW (Aug 2, NEGLIGIBLE) → 26,395 MW (Aug 3, MODERATE) → 23,639 MW (Aug 4, SEVERE). The Aug 3 MODERATE was a middle step in a deepening trajectory. This pattern — progressive intensification mid-week in August — may reflect consistent cloud cover clearing through the week or a weather pattern that deepens solar yield. Stage 1 implication: including a "prior-day NL trough" feature would help detect multi-day deepening trajectories and update probability estimates accordingly.

### L3. SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT ramp escalation pattern matches solar cliff deepening

Aug 3 ramp +18,665 MW → Aug 4 ramp +21,473 MW (+2,808 MW, +15%). This escalation is primarily driven by the lower NL(HE18) on Aug 4 (43,269 vs Aug 3's 44,963 MW) and higher NL(HE21) on Aug 4 (64,742 vs Aug 3's 63,629 MW). The Enverus gap uncertainty applies consistently to both days. The calibration framework (floor/anchor system) correctly scales the probability (LOW-MEDIUM 15-25% for Aug 3 vs MEDIUM 28-42% for Aug 4). The gap between Aug 3 and Aug 4 probability calls (+13-17 ppt upgrade) is proportional to the ramp differential (+2,808 MW, crossing from "narrow floor margin" to "near anchor").

### L4. Enverus gap creates systematically conservative SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT calls

The YE-Enverus NL gap (3,741-4,626 MW at HE21) consistently pulls the adjusted ramp to or below the non-binding floor, even when the YE base ramp is strong. On Aug 3 (+18,665 MW), the Enverus-adjusted range was 14,039-14,924 MW (near/below floor). On Aug 4 (+21,473 MW), the Enverus-adjusted range is 16,847-17,732 MW (below floor). This means that without Enverus validation, the effective P(binding|Enverus applies) remains low even on strong YE signal days. The August 1 RT-only binding event (called LOW-MEDIUM 18-30%) may reflect this systematic conservatism — the RTM does not have the same Enverus gap exposure because it responds to actual physical conditions, not the YE bidclose NL estimate. Until Enverus data is restored, all SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT calls should carry a note that P(RT binding) > P(DA binding) by potentially 10-15 ppt.
