## 2. D+2 Carry-in Failure — Detailed Analysis

**This is the highest-priority learning from the Oct 14 → Oct 15 cycle.**

### What was predicted
Oct 14 learnings/2026-10-13.md §10 documented the D+2 processing methodology:
- Trigger condition: HE24 SCI 0.373 > 0.350 → ACTIVATED
- Decay model: Sep 18 HE24 SCI 0.383 → Sep 19 HE01 SCI 0.366 (delta = -0.017)
- Projected Oct 15 HE01: SCI 0.373 - 0.017 = 0.356 (range 0.356-0.385 with ±1 SD)
- Projected GR_WEST: 10,618-11,486 MW → MEDIUM tier

### What actually happened
- Oct 15 HE01 actual: GR_WEST = 9,774 MW (SCI = 0.328)
- Actual overnight decay: 0.373 − 0.328 = 0.045 (vs projected 0.017)
- Error magnitude: 2.6x faster decay than the Sep analog suggested
- Miss direction: always downward (actual < projected) — systematic underprediction of decay rate

### Root cause hypotheses
1. **Meteorological: different wind regime type.** Sep 18 was an evening buildup chain (overnight SCI stayed relatively flat). Oct 14's HE24 SCI 0.373 was the peak of an INCREASING trend at end of day. Oct 15 overnight showed rapid decay as the diurnal cycle reset. The evening-buildup → overnight-collapse pattern may be a distinct October regime vs September's overnight-sustain pattern.

2. **Single-analog model inadequacy.** The decay rate was estimated from one Sep analog (Sep 18→Sep 19, -0.017). A proper regression over all Aug-Sep 2026 overnight sequences would provide a distribution of decay rates. The Sep analog may be unrepresentative of October diurnal wind behavior.

3. **October solar-wind coupling.** October solar peak hours (HE10-14) generate dramatic GR_WEST suppression (today's HE14 minimum = 2,090 MW, lowest in Oct 2026 series). The Oct 15 overnight GR_WEST (9,774 MW) is already in a "next-day decay" pattern that precedes the Oct 15 midday solar-driven GR_WEST collapse to 2,090 MW. The chain dynamics may be influenced by the anticipated next-day solar suppression.

### Calibration implications for Stage 1
1. D+2 carry-in probability should be modeled as P(HE01 SCI > threshold) rather than binary trigger + point estimate.
2. The decay rate distribution should be fit empirically across all Stage 0 overnight sequences (not just one Sep analog).
3. The model should include a flag for "evening-buildup trigger" vs "sustained-overnight trigger" — these may have different decay rate distributions.
4. The D+3 carry-in from Oct 15 HE24 SCI 0.614 is kept as STRONGLY ACTIVATED but with widened CI (±12-15 ppt vs standard ±8-12 ppt) to reflect demonstrated variability.

### Impact on downstream agents
- bess-optimizer Oct 15: overnight charge window was flagged as MEDIUM conviction. Actual: no overnight charge opportunity (GR_WEST 6,483-9,774 MW; below MEDIUM all HE01-06). The congestion signal was NOT realized overnight. The real charge opportunity shifted to duck curve (MILD, HE10-12) and the late-evening surge (HE22-24, post-discharge window). Net impact: bess-optimizer received a misleading overnight charge signal from Oct 14's D+2 flag. Applying LOW decision weight (Stage 0 policy) appropriately discounted this.
