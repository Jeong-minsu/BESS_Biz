## 2. Thread B — Key Findings from Aug 11 D+1 Analysis

### Finding 13 — Two-Hour EXTREME Duck Curve Pattern (New)

**Discovery**: Aug 11 YE bidclose data shows NL at HE10 = 19,981 MW and HE11 = 21,375 MW — both below the 22,000 MW EXTREME boundary. This is the FIRST occurrence in Stage 0 Phase 2 of two consecutive EXTREME hours on a WDPEAK day.

**Structural mechanism**: The TWO-HOUR EXTREME window arises from a specific driver combination unique to Aug 11:
1. Very high solar generation persisting from HE10 into HE11 (solar drops from 27,027 MW at HE10 to 30,346 MW at HE11 — solar actually increases from HE10 to HE11 before peaking at HE12-13 at 31,744-31,980 MW)
2. GR_WEST remains MEDIUM (SCI 0.394-0.406) throughout HE09-12 — unusually sustained West wind during the duck curve window compared to prior events
3. Load rises from 67,599 (HE10) to 72,295 (HE11) but not fast enough to overcome the solar increase + sustained West wind

**Provisional rule (Finding 13)**: When GR_WEST during HE09-12 exceeds 10,500 MW (SCI > 0.355) AND solar is rising through its pre-peak ramp (HE10-11 solar trajectory still ascending toward peak), the duck curve maintains EXTREME depth across multiple consecutive hours even as load rises. The single-hour EXTREME events in prior Phase 2 history (Jul 27: 21,706 MW) occurred when GR_WEST during duck curve was LOW (SCI < 0.300). When West wind sustains MEDIUM levels during the duck curve ramp, the EXTREME tier can persist for 2+ hours.

**Calibration implication**: The EXTREME tier calibration (E[GKS MCC] = -$5.0 to -$6.0+/MWh) was established for single-hour EXTREME events. For a two-hour EXTREME window, the GKS dispatch opportunity is approximately doubled — the battery can charge at the highest-value MCC for two full consecutive hours rather than one. The total charge energy capture at EXTREME MCC levels for Aug 11 (HE10+HE11 combined) is the highest in Phase 2 history outside of Jul 9 series records.

### Finding 14 — Evening West Wind SCI Escalation Pattern (New)

**Discovery**: Aug 11 HE22-24 GR_WEST SCI values (0.468/0.506/0.526) set new Phase 2 evening records. The prior Phase 2 evening SCI record was Aug 10's HE24 at 0.466. Aug 11 exceeds this by 0.040-0.060 in the same hours.

**Three-day progression pattern observed (Aug 9→10→11 late evening)**:
- Aug 9 (Sunday): HE23 SCI 0.388, HE24 SCI 0.420 (LOW-MEDIUM)
- Aug 10 (Monday): HE23 SCI 0.438, HE24 SCI 0.466 (MEDIUM → approaching MEDIUM-HIGH)
- Aug 11 (Tuesday): HE23 SCI 0.506, HE24 SCI 0.526 (MEDIUM-HIGH — Phase 2 record)

**Provisional rule (Finding 14)**: The Monday-Tuesday West wind evening escalation pattern in August 2026 follows a consistent acceleration: Sunday overnight shows lowest SCI (Finding 11), Monday evening SCI is intermediate (MEDIUM-approaching-MEDIUM-HIGH), Tuesday evening SCI reaches new weekly peaks. This 3-day ramp pattern may continue into mid-week (Wednesday Aug 12) before reverting. Monitor Aug 12 bidclose data for whether the peak is Tuesday or if Wednesday extends the escalation.

**PANHANDLE implication**: The evening West escalation is making the PANHANDLE constraint increasingly relevant post-W25. Aug 11 HE24 is the first post-W25 hourly breach (15,571 MW > 15,000 MW threshold). If Wednesday Aug 12 continues the evening escalation, HE23-24 may both exceed threshold, raising PANHANDLE to LOW-MEDIUM territory. This would be the first multi-hour PANHANDLE window since Jun 30.

### Finding 15 — 12-Hour Continuous MEDIUM SCI Span (New)

**Discovery**: Aug 11 GR_WEST exceeds the MEDIUM SCI threshold (0.350) for 12 consecutive hours (HE01-12) and then again for 6 more hours (HE19-24), yielding 18 of 24 hours at or above MEDIUM. The 12-consecutive-hour MEDIUM span (HE01-12) is a Phase 2 record for sustained MEDIUM SCI without interruption.

**Prior Phase 2 records**:
- Aug 10: 7-hour MEDIUM span (HE01-07) — previous Phase 2 record for overnight
- Aug 6: 6-hour MEDIUM overnight + 3-hour evening HE22-24
- Aug 11: 12-hour continuous span (HE01-12), plus 6-hour evening = 18/24 total

**Provisional rule (Finding 15)**: When GR_WEST maintains MEDIUM SCI through both the overnight period AND the duck curve window without dropping below 0.350, the result is compounded congestion — the overnight West constraint creates price suppression AND the duck curve West constraint creates additional price suppression during the same day. The overlay of overnight West flow + duck curve West surplus is the defining characteristic of EXTREME-intensity WDPEAK days. Prior EXTREME events (Jul 9, Jul 27) may have had similar patterns — worth verifying once W3 hub-pair LMP data is available.
