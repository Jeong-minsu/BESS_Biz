## 5. Stage 0 Model Improvement Points

1. **Analog delta needs more than one observation**: The Tue-to-Wed delta of -2,671 MW is derived from the Jul 22 week only. Stage 1 feature design should build a rolling distribution of WDPEAK day-to-day NL ramp deltas (Mon→Tue, Tue→Wed, Wed→Thu, Thu→Fri) using the full Stage 0 series. A single-week delta has ±2,000-3,000 MW uncertainty — larger than the Enverus wind gap correction.

2. **Compound-degradation policy needed for consecutive days above binding floor**: When 3+ consecutive WDPEAK days show NL ramp above the binding floor, the analog fallback should DEFAULT to "above floor" rather than applying the Tue-to-Wed delta, because the structural heat wave conditions dominate the day-of-week pattern. In this week, Mon (+22,134), Tue (+21,985), and Thu (+20,431) were all above floor — suggesting Wed was also likely above floor regardless of the day-type delta applied.

3. **Wednesday WDPEAK shallowing pattern may not apply during extreme July heat waves**: The Jul 22 week Tue-to-Wed trough shallowing (+4,760 MW) occurred during a different heat regime. Jul 27-31 shows a sustained SEVERE-EXTREME trough regime for all five days. Stage 1 should include a "heat regime persistence" feature (e.g., 5-day average NL trough or heat index trajectory) to override the day-type delta in extreme weeks.
