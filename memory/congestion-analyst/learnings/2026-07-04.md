## 6. Key Learning Items

**L13 — Holiday thin liquidity can elevate RT prices without constraint binding**

The Jul 4 (Independence Day) RT discharge price proxy ($59.93/MWh) is elevated but holiday markets have documented thin liquidity (Risk 6 in the issued outlook). A $59.93/MWh RT price at GKS during HE21-22 on a holiday Saturday could reflect:
- (a) HOUSTON_IMPORT binding → elevated RT clearing for load-serving entities behind the constraint, or
- (b) Thin-market price spike without constraint binding → clearing artifacts from low DA/RT market participation

The issued outlook identified this risk explicitly (Risk 6: "thin markets can produce atypical price spikes; use wider confidence intervals"). When Jul 8 settlement confirms, this calibration point will help distinguish the two scenarios.

Action: After Jul 8 settlement, compare Jul 4 RT price proxy vs confirmed binding/non-binding status. If non-binding, add to "thin market spike" event library. If binding at LOW 12-18%, document as rare-event binding for calibration.

**L14 — Maximum-strength Finding 7 behavioral validation**

Jul 4 achieved the conditions for maximum-strength Finding 7 in the July series: solar peak 30.1 GW (new series high) + NL trough 22-23k MW (new series low). The GKS battery charged at HE09-11, confirming the negative MCC direction call for the third time in five July series days (confirmed also Jul 1, Jul 2 through behavioral proxies). The progression pattern is:
- Jul 2: solar 27.5 GW, NL trough ~29k MW → E[MCC] -$3.5
- Jul 3: solar 29.0 GW (YE) / 30.4 GW (Env), NL trough 24k MW → E[MCC] -$3.5 to -$4.0
- Jul 4: solar 30.1 GW (YE) / 30.3 GW (Env), NL trough 22-23k MW → E[MCC] -$4.0 to -$5.0

The monotonic relationship (more solar + deeper NL trough → more negative GKS MCC) is physically grounded and consistently directional across the series. Stage 1 priority: formalize the NL trough → MCC magnitude curve for Finding 7.

**L15 — AS stack dominates on low-congestion Saturday holiday (structural)**

On Jul 4, 78% of GKS total revenue ($3,309.20 of $4,260.84) came from ancillary services (ECRS + NonSpin), not energy. The congestion-driven component (midday negative MCC avoided via HE09-11 charge, evening positive MCC conditional) contributed directionally correct positioning, but the AS stack was the primary P&L driver on this day type. This is consistent with the heuristic that:
- Saturday + major holiday → reduced RT energy price volatility from congestion (thin market holiday discount applied to probability calls)
- NonSpin and ECRS are priced on system tightness (load/reserve margin), not constraint binding specifically

Implication for congestion-analyst role: on low-probability binding days (Saturday holidays), the congestion signal primarily informs the energy component of the stack (charge window timing, discharge window timing) rather than the AS positioning. The positive behavioral outcome (bess-optimizer charged HE09-11 per Finding 7 guidance) validates the directional call even when the absolute P&L is dominated by AS.
