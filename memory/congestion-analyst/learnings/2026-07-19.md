## 5. Improvement Points — Stage 0 Calibration Updates

### Update 1: Sunday Discount — Reduce for Extreme Heat (KDFW >95°F)

**Trigger**: SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT bound on Sunday at YE NL ramp +18,538 MW despite -5 to -7 ppt Sunday discount that produced a LOW-MEDIUM call (18-28%). Actual behavior resembled a weekday pattern.

**Evidence**: GKS RTSPP $32-34/MWh at HE21-22 on a Sunday is consistent with confirmed weekday binding cycles (Jul 1: $33-41/MWh HE20-22). The structural weekend load suppression does exist but is partially offset by residential cooling demand on extreme heat days.

**Calibration update**: When KDFW forecast peak >= 95°F AND day type = Sunday, reduce discount to -3 to -5 ppt (from -5 to -7 ppt). For KDFW >= 100°F AND Sunday, apply -2 to -3 ppt only. The Jul 19 cycle had KDFW 96.3°F at HE18 (per AG2), placing it at the lower end of the "extreme heat Sunday" regime. Apply immediately in next cycle with Sunday extreme-heat conditions.

### Update 2: Finding 7 MCC Magnitude — Reduce at NL Trough 27,000-28,000 MW

**Trigger**: NL trough 27,418 MW (Jul 19) produced MCC ~0/-$1.5 (inferred), while the forecast issued -$2.5/-$4.5. The Jul 18 trough was 25,662 MW (record deep) with MCC issued -$3/-$5. The Jul 19 trough was 1,756 MW shallower and the forecast reduced by only 20-30% to -$2.5/-$4.5 — insufficient.

**Evidence**: The magnitude reduction from 25,662 MW (Jul 18) to 27,418 MW (Jul 19) appears to require a much larger proportional magnitude reduction — perhaps 50-70% rather than 20-30%.

**Calibration update**: Establish a provisional NL trough → MCC magnitude mapping:
- NL trough <= 26,000 MW: -$3.0 to -$5.0/MWh (severe regime)
- NL trough 26,000-27,000 MW: -$2.0 to -$4.0/MWh (moderate-severe)
- NL trough 27,000-29,000 MW: -$0.5 to -$2.5/MWh (moderate regime — REVISION from prior -$2.5/-$4.5)
- NL trough 29,000-32,000 MW: -$0.5 to -$1.5/MWh (mild regime)
- NL trough > 32,000 MW: -$0 to -$0.5/MWh (negligible)

This is provisional and subject to revision as more settlement cycles confirm actual MCC magnitudes.

### Update 3: YE vs Enverus Source Priority When Divergence > 5,000 MW

**Trigger**: YE-Enverus NL ramp divergence was 6,402 MW on this cycle (new series record). YE implied binding (+18,538 MW ramp); Enverus implied non-binding (+12,136 MW). YE proved closer to actual market behavior.

**Evidence**: The driver was solar forecast divergence at HE18 (YE 23,942 MW vs Enverus ~17,783 MW = 6,159 MW gap). YE's higher solar estimate at HE18 means lower NL at HE18, producing a larger HE18→HE21 ramp — which is what actually drove binding. The constraint binding confirms YE's solar estimate was more accurate for this cycle.

**Calibration update**: When YE-Enverus solar at HE18 diverges by more than 4,000 MW, treat YE as PRIMARY source for NL ramp at HE18 and assign binding probability based primarily on YE ramp. Enverus is used as downside anchor only. If YE is ABSENT, the appropriate uncertainty adjustment is +5 to +10 ppt for SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT probability given the confirmed systematic Enverus undercount of solar at HE18.
