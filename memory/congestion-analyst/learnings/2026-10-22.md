### Insight 3: Tyba asymmetric tail interpretation (formalization)

- HE18-20: P(DA>RT) = 55.1-57.4% (most of time DA > RT)
- BUT E[RT] = $51.16-$58.42 > E[DA] = $46.76-$55.06 (expected RT > DA)
- Interpretation: ~55-57% probability the day looks "normal" (DA wins), but when binding occurs (~43-45% probability), the RT spike is large enough to dominate expected value

This structure (P(dominant event) < 50% but E[value] driven by tail) is diagnostic of SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT binding behavior. The binding event has fat right tail in RT-DA space. Using P(RT>DA) as a binary classifier (per Oct 19 Insight 1) has limits — the proper metric is E[RT]-E[DA] which can be positive even when P(RT>DA) < 0.5.

**Formalization for Stage 1**: binding regime classifier for SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT should use E[RT]-E[DA] (expected spread) from Tyba, not just P(RT>DA) sign.
