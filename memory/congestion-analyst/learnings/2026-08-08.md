## Key Learnings from Aug 9 D+1 Preparation

### Finding 10 — Sunday WEPEAK Distinct Duck Curve Regime

**Observation**: Aug 9 Sunday WEPEAK load peak = 84,969.9 MW (only 1,619 MW below Aug 7's Friday WDPEAK peak 86,589 MW). Despite the relatively small load difference, the NL trough on Aug 9 (27,151.2 MW) is 1,402 MW DEEPER than Aug 7 (28,553 MW). This indicates that weekend WEPEAK duck curves can be deeper than adjacent weekday MODERATE cycles because: (a) Sunday afternoon load is lower at solar peak hours even while load-weighted totals are similar; (b) composition shifts (C&I load lower, residential higher but less heat-sensitive on non-work days).

**Implication**: Do NOT assume WEPEAK Sunday = shallower duck curve than Friday WDPEAK. Sunday NL trough can be 1,000-2,000 MW deeper even at comparable peak load levels. The duck curve is driven by HE10 NL (when solar is at ~85% of peak), not by the WEPEAK/WDPEAK label.

**Action**: Add "Sunday WEPEAK duck curve adjustment: +500 to +1,500 MW deeper than same-week WDPEAK equivalent" to duck curve tier calibration notes. This is a provisional observation based on 1 cycle — monitor Aug 16 (Sunday) as the next confirmatory data point.

### Finding 11 — Overnight West Wind Weekly Structure

**Observation**: GR_WEST overnight SCI progression for the Aug week: Aug 4 (0.461-0.492) → Aug 5 (0.367-0.419) → Aug 6 (0.362-0.434) → Aug 7 (0.363-0.401) → Aug 9 (0.254-0.319). The sharp drop from Aug 7 to Aug 9 (-0.082 at max SCI) is the largest 2-day change in the Aug series. This suggests overnight West wind has a weekly structure where mid-week maxima (Aug 4-5 MEDIUM-HIGH) decay to weekend lows (Aug 9 all-LOW). This pattern may be related to offshore flow patterns that strengthen Mon-Thu and weaken Fri-Sun.

**Implication**: When forecasting Sunday overnight West wind from prior-Friday bidclose data, consider a downward adjustment of ~2,000-3,000 MW for GR_WEST overnight on Sunday. This would have changed Aug 9's provisional overnight call from "comparable to Aug 7" to "significantly weaker than Aug 7" earlier in the analysis cycle.

**Action**: Provisional adjustment rule — when flowday is Sunday or Monday, apply a GR_WEST overnight haircut of ~15-25% relative to prior-Friday bidclose overnight forecast. Requires multi-week validation before formalizing.
