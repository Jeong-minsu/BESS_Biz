## 4. Stage 0 Model Improvement Points

Three observations from this cycle that carry forward to Stage 1 feature engineering:

1. **WDPEAK day-type is not a reliable proxy for duck curve depth during extreme July heat**: Prior to July 2026, all WDPEAK troughs in the Stage 0 series ranged 30,000-35,000 MW. July 27's 21,706 MW is 8,264 MW deeper than any prior WDPEAK day. The 21,706 MW trough is driven by a combination of near-zero West wind at solar peak (GR_WEST 5,949-9,507 MW HE10-15) and high solar (31,263 MW peak). Stage 1 feature engineering must replace "is_wdpeak" binary with a continuous trough-depth estimate (NL_trough_MW or wind_at_solar_peak_MW), not a day-type categorical. Day-type categoricals should be a secondary feature only.

2. **Single-source cycle introduces ±10 ppt CI widening — emergency fallback design needed**: The Jul 27 forecast was issued with only YE data available; Enverus, Smartbidder, and AG2 all absent. The widened CI (±10 ppt probability, ±50% magnitude) is operationally acceptable for direction calls but unacceptable for magnitude. Stage 1 infrastructure should include: (a) a pre-fetch fallback priority ordering (YE primary → Enverus secondary → AG2 tertiary); (b) a "data richness score" input feature for the model that explicitly encodes single-source vs multi-source cycles; (c) a daily 09:00 CT re-fetch attempt before DAM cutoff if initial cycle is single-source, to recover any late-arriving Enverus or AG2 data.

3. **Dual-constraint extreme on WDPEAK Monday (first in Stage 0 series) suggests a July regime shift**: Finding 7 EXTREME and SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT MEDIUM-HIGH co-occurring on a WDPEAK Monday has not been observed in any prior month. This suggests July 2026's heat/solar/wind combination is creating a structural environment outside the calibration range of May-June. Stage 1 should include a seasonal regime feature (month, or lagged 7-day average NL trough) to capture this drift, rather than assuming stationarity across the full training window.
