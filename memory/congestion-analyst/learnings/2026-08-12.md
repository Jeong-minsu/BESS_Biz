## 2. Key Structural Learnings from Aug 12 Cycle

### A. Finding 16 First Occurrence — All-24-Hour MEDIUM Day

Aug 12 was the first Phase 2 day in which ALL 24 hours had GR_WEST SCI at MEDIUM or higher. This has now occurred twice consecutively (Aug 12 + Aug 13). Key implication: when a sustained Great Plains wind event places GR_WEST above ~10,600 MW throughout the entire 24-hour period, there is no "no-congestion" window. The afternoon "no-trade" zone still applies (MEDIUM SCI produces mild MCC suppression of -$0.5 to -$3/MWh), but there is no intraday period where GKS MCC approaches zero from the WEST_TO_NORTH driver.

Stage 1 LightGBM implication: overnight SCI minimum and daytime GR_WEST floor are new features that distinguish Finding 16 days from ordinary HIGH-SCI days.

### B. Finding 17 — First Overnight PANHANDLE Breach

Aug 12 HE01-04: PANHANDLE breach (margins +33 to +437 MW; 4 hours). This was the first time in Phase 2 that the PANHANDLE_EXPORT constraint activated during OFFPEAK overnight hours. Prior PANHANDLE observations were all W25-era or WDPEAK daytime. The overnight PANHANDLE co-activates with WEST_TO_NORTH overnight congestion and is a new dual-constraint overnight pattern. Aug 13 extends this to 7 hours and >3x margins.

### C. Finding 14 Peak and Reversal on Aug 13

Aug 12 established the peak of the Mon→Wed evening SCI escalation: HE24 = 0.567. Aug 13's HE24 = 0.525 represents a −0.042 reversal. This confirms that the evening escalation pattern has a natural ceiling and reverses on Thursday, while overnight escalation continues. The Mon→Wed escalation was: 0.420→0.466→0.526→0.567. The Thu reversal: 0.525.

Operational implication: Aug 12 evening (HE22-24) was the peak evening congestion day in Phase 2 history. Future forecasts should recognize that when the Mon→Wed pattern peaks (HE24 SCI > 0.560), the next day's evening is likely to step down.

Aug 12 NL drop HE07→HE10 = −19,970 MW (prior Phase 2 record; superseded by Aug 13's −20,118.5 MW). The daily series record has been set on back-to-back days. This is driven by expanding solar capacity and sustained high West wind — both forces are at Phase 2 peaks simultaneously.
