The Jul 22 forecast applied -$1.5 to -$2.5/MWh for a NL trough of 34,730 MW (YE) / 34,280 MW (Enverus). However, the Update 2 calibration table (confirmed in `learnings/2026-07-20.md`, published Jul 21 morning) explicitly defines:

**Root cause**: The Jul 22 forecast (produced Jul 21) was the first cycle where Update 2 was available (confirmed in learnings/2026-07-20.md). However, the analyst applied Update 2 to the lower NL trough band (29,000-32,000 MW) but did NOT carry the correction through to the > 32,000 MW negligible tier. The Finding 7 calibration table in the Jul 22 report shows Jul 6 (35,301 MW → -$1.5/-$2.5) and Jul 15 (37,081 MW → -$1.5/-$2.5) as reference anchors — but those prior-cycle estimates were themselves pre-Update 2 estimates, not confirmed settlement actuals. The analyst used pre-Update 2 historical estimates as calibration anchors, perpetuating the overcall in the > 32,000 MW regime.

**Contrast**: The Jul 23 D+1 report (produced Jul 22, AFTER the Jul 22 forecast was filed) correctly applied the > 32,000 MW negligible threshold for NL trough 33,791/33,899 MW, yielding -$0 to -$0.5/MWh. This confirms the model was corrected within the same day cycle.

**Magnitude overcall**: The issued estimate of -$1.5 to -$2.5/MWh is likely 3-5x the actual MCC magnitude for a trough > 34,000 MW. The actual MCC is expected to be -$0 to -$0.5/MWh (Update 2 correct range).

**Directional call**: CORRECT (negative midday MCC expected for any active Finding 7, even negligible-tier events). The direction guidance to bess-optimizer to charge at HE09-12 was operationally valid.

**Operational impact assessment**: GKS charging at HE09-12 would have been guided by a -$1.5 to -$2.5/MWh nodal discount that was likely 3-5x overstated. The actual charge economics were probably near-flat vs hub (MCC -$0 to -$0.5).
