**HE19 DEC near-miss (P adj ~49%):**

Sep 9's HE19 (NL 62,541 MW) clears the gate by +2,541 MW — the first time HE19 has fired the Scarcity Cliff gate in September 2026. However, solar at HE19 = 12,067 MW. HE21 is weaker than HE20 but stronger than Sep 8's HE21 on two key metrics: NL is 887 MW higher (66,992 vs 66,105 MW) and wind is 3,891 MW lower (11,082 vs 14,973 MW).
