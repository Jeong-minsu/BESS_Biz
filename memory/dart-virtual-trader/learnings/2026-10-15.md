#### Structural Argument for HE14-15 INC at HB_WEST

1. Oct 15 HE24 GR_WEST = 18,299 MW (all-time Stage 0 record, SCI 0.614). DA for Oct 16 closed 10:00 CT Oct 15 — the full Oct 15 surge (HE22-24) was visible and priced into Oct 16.

2. DA naturally extended a congestion discount into Oct 16 midday (HE09-15), assuming the wind system would persist through the morning transition. **Why this is structurally sound**: The mechanism is "DA congestion pricing carry-over into transition hours" — a complement to the established onset_early/onset_late discriminant.
