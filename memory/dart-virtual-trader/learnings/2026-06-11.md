## 3. Key Learnings

### Learning 1: Absence of Smartbidder probability data creates systematic downgrade uncertainty

All 4 positions on the 2026-06-12 cycle were issued without Smartbidder P(DA>RT) confirmation. This is the second consecutive cycle (2026-06-12 and 2026-06-11's HE15 HB_WEST) where Smartbidder data absence required falling back to structural model only.

**Rule reinforced**: When Smartbidder DA-RT probability is unavailable AND HOUSTON_IMPORT is at MEDIUM (not HIGH), size all positions at 25 MW (MED tier) regardless of structural model signal. This was correctly applied on 2026-06-12. Do not increase to 50 MW without Smartbidder confirmation.

**For 2026-06-13**: Smartbidder probability IS AVAILABLE (PRODUCTION) — this is a meaningful upgrade vs the prior cycle. Sizing can reflect the probability signal directly.

### Learning 2: Elevated DA ($40+) on MEDIUM HOUSTON_IMPORT day is an anomaly worth flagging

HE21 DA $40.23 on a MEDIUM conviction day is unexpected. Typical MEDIUM days in the June series have HE21 DA in the $30-35 range. The $40+ clearing suggests either:
(a) The DAM bid stack was thin on Friday (end-of-week), or
(b) A resource outage or localized scarcity event not captured in the Yes Energy/Enverus forecast caused the high clearing.

**Watch**: If Friday settlement confirms that RT was actually lower than $40 (SHORT DA won), this may indicate that Friday-thin DAM creates elevated DA clearing that systematically exceeds RT — a structural SHORT DA signal for end-of-week HIGH-priced hours. This would be a new alpha source worth tracking.

**For 2026-06-13 (Saturday)**: Saturday DA prices are NOT elevated in the same way — DA HE21 forecasted at only $31.88 vs Friday's $40.23. This supports the thesis that Saturday's lighter DAM liquidity pushes prices in the opposite direction from Friday (lower DA on Saturday, potentially closer to or below RT if scarcity materializes).

### Learning 3: HB_WEST elimination on the Friday cycle was structurally correct

Five consecutive cycles of HB_WEST inclusion ended correctly on 2026-06-12. GR_WEST at 3,129 MW is a fundamentally different wind regime than the prior 4 cycles (all GR_WEST > 11,000 MW). The elimination was a straightforward structural decision, not a discretionary one.

**For the Saturday 2026-06-13 cycle**: GR_WEST is still low (trough 9,832 MW, SCI peak 0.565) — far below the 11,500 MW floor trigger. HB_WEST is again structurally absent. No repeat of the multi-cycle HB_WEST pattern unless GR_WEST recovers above 11,500+ MW.

### Learning 4: Friday HE19 at 56% P(win) — reconsider inclusion criterion

HE19 on the 2026-06-12 cycle was the weakest position (56% structural model, no Smartbidder confirmation). This is the third consecutive cycle where HE19 was included at the boundary of the MED threshold.

**Rule under review**: When P(win) is 55-57% AND Smartbidder data is absent, the position is marginal. The 0.6x compression applied to the E[spread] ($4.0/MWh post-compression) is close to the $4 hard floor. Including it adds notional exposure for marginal expected value.

**Tentative conclusion**: HE19 should be excluded when Smartbidder is absent AND structural P(win) is below 57%. With Smartbidder present (as on 2026-06-13), the threshold check can use the Smartbidder probability directly — if P(DA<RT) or P(DA>RT) > 59% at HE19, include; otherwise pass.

**Applied to 2026-06-13**: P(DA<RT) = 0.6288 at HE19 — above the 59% inclusion threshold. HE19 is included on the Saturday cycle.

### Learning 5: 0.6x compression factor — sample too small to change, but directional evidence accumulating

Confirmed data:
- 2026-06-10 HE22: realized spread $5.87/MWh vs 0.6x-compressed estimate $4.5/MWh → realized = 0.78x raw
- 2026-06-06 (Saturday): realized spread approximately -$23/MWh (RT >> DA) → the compression factor was irrelevant; the direction was wrong entirely

The 0.6x factor was designed to account for the uncertainty in the Stage 0 heuristic MCC estimates. With two data points — one where the factor was too conservative (HE22 on a weekday) and one where the direction was reversed (Saturday scarcity event) — the factor cannot be revised yet. Continue at 0.6x until 3+ directionally correct confirmed settlements provide calibration material.
