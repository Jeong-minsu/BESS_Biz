For 7/30 (Wednesday), analog ramp estimated ~+19,300 MW (above binding floor) → SOUTH_HOUSTON binding MEDIUM-HIGH (32-48%). HE19-22 SKIP maintained in all-skip recommendation.

### Learning C — HE21 DEC threshold refinement warranted

P(DEC win) at HE21 = ~59% — correctly blocked at 60% threshold in DEGRADED mode. However, the structural case for DEC at HE21 with SOUTH_HOUSTON binding (ramp > 20,000 MW + P(binding) > 35%) is sound. A refined entry condition would allow small DEC positions (10 MW) at:

- Non-spin DA HE21 < $10/MWh (no AS scarcity spike risk).

This condition would have allowed 10 MW DEC entry at HE21 on 7/29. File in plans/ for v5 model design.

7/29 was SB-DEGRADED with YE PROD available (−3 ppt penalty, entries possible). 7/30 is dual-degraded (both absent, −5 ppt penalty, all-skip result).
