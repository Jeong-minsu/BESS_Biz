## 5. Actionable Insights

### Insight 1: DEGRADED Penalty — Introduce Spread-Magnitude Tiering for Large-Spread Hours

**Observation**: The flat −5 ppt DEGRADED penalty treats all signals equally regardless of spread magnitude. On Oct 20, four DEC hours (HE17-21) with |spread| $5.01-$8.79/MWh are blocked. These are large-spread signals where directional confidence from structural analysis is high independent of Tyba calibration quality. The raw P values (54-57.3%) are in the range where the 2026-09-21 threshold solver showed Tyba calibration uncertainty; however, the structural mechanism (NL ramp + wind trough) is unambiguous and the spread magnitude provides a larger margin for error.

**Action**: Introduce a two-tier DEGRADED rule:
- Tier 1 (thin spread, |spread| ≤ $3/MWh): Apply full −5 ppt penalty. Tyba calibration uncertainty is large relative to spread → conservative filter is appropriate.
- Tier 2 (large spread, |spread| > $5/MWh): Apply reduced −3 ppt penalty. Even with 2 ppt less penalty, floor remains adj P ≥ 55%. A raw P of 57.3% (HE21) → adj 54.3% under Tier 1 (blocked) vs adj 54.3% still blocked, but raw P 58% → adj 55% now qualifies (Medium). At |spread| > $5/MWh, the E[net] buffer is wide enough to absorb calibration error.

**Expected recovery**: Under Tier 2, HE20-21 (raw P 55.8-57.3%, |spread| $5.01-6.63/MWh) would qualify at Medium (15 MW each), recovering ~$45-47 advisory E[net] per similar day. This directly addresses the pattern where "Oct 20 blocked advisory E[net] (5-6x active advisory)" is a recurring issue on WDPEAK days with strong evening DEC structure.
