## Qualitative Observations (from model run on 2026-06-30 for 2026-07-01)

### Model v3 conflict-zone skip (new finding, filed here)

During the 2026-07-01 position run, a new model behavior emerged: Smartbidder's P(DA<RT) was 0.60-0.68 for HE01-11, but the Smartbidder price forecast simultaneously showed DA > RT (positive spread) for the same hours. This intra-vendor contradiction has not been documented in prior learnings files.

**Implication**: Smartbidder's probability model and its price model appear to use different simulation paths or aggregation methods. The probability reflects the median/mode of a distribution, while the DA Energy Price column may reflect the mean. When the distribution has a positive mean but a sub-50% probability of being positive, the distribution is right-skewed — many near-zero or slightly negative outcomes (RT > DA by $0-2) and a few large positive outcomes (DA >> RT during congestion events).

**For DART model**: This means the "Long DA / Short RT" signal from P(DA<RT) > 0.5 during overnight hours is real, but the magnitude (expected spread) is tiny. The combined model correctly skips these hours. The model v3 conflict-zone rule (skip when probability opposes spread AND |spread| < $1.50) is validated by this analysis.

**Action item**: When Tenaska data becomes available for 2026-06-29 and 2026-07-01, review HE01-11 actual DA-RT settlement prices to test whether the conflict zone hours would have been profitable as Long DA/Short RT or Short DA/Long RT. This will calibrate the $1.50 conflict-zone threshold and determine if overnight hours merit a separate model treatment.
