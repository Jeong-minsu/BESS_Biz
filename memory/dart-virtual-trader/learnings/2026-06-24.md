## 3. What Went Wrong — Root Cause Analysis

### 3.1 The DART Virtual P&L Attribution Problem

The Tenaska `dart_virtual_revenue` = −$3,964.90 is computed as `DA_Energy_Amt + RT_Energy_Amt` per the pnl-manager note: "Computed as DA_Energy_Amt + RT_Energy_Amt (net settlement). GKS was a net buyer in DA (negative DA energy revenue) and partially offset in RT. This does not represent virtual bid/offer positions — it reflects physical charging purchases at DA prices vs RT imbalance."

**Critical finding**: The −$3,964.90 DART Virtual Net in Tenaska does NOT isolate the virtual positions issued by dart-virtual-trader. It combines physical BESS DA charging/discharging with any virtual positions. GKS had large DA energy charges (−$4,005.31) that represent physical charging operations by the BESS, not financial virtual bids.

From the Tenaska hourly data in the pnl report:
- HE01-05: GKS DA discharge (positive DA_Energy_Amt = $1,223 to $1,820/h) against large RT consumption charges — these are physical BESS operations
- HE08-12: GKS DA charging (negative DA_Energy_Amt = −$916 to −$1,213/h) — physical purchases
- HE15-20: GKS DA charging again (−$916 to −$1,572/h)

These are NOT DART virtual financial positions. The −$4,005.31 DA energy total is dominated by physical BESS dispatch, not the 4 basis virtual positions recommended.

### 3.2 Did the Basis Positions Actually Execute and Settle?

The Smartbidder benchmark DART Virtual = +$661.75 for Jun 24 (from Tenaska pnl report). This represents: "a virtual position cleared at HE15 ($2,417.00 DA long offset by -$1,755.25 RT short) which GKS did not execute." This tells us two things:

1. Smartbidder executed a DIFFERENT virtual position than what dart-virtual-trader recommended (HE15 virtual long, not HE19-22 basis).
2. GKS "did not execute" the Smartbidder HE15 equivalent. This suggests virtual positions may NOT have been submitted for Jun 24 — or they cleared at different hubs/prices.

The 4 basis positions (Long HB_HOUSTON DA / Short HB_NORTH DA at HE19-22) that dart-virtual-trader recommended have no isolated settlement line in the Tenaska data. The recommendation may not have been executed, OR it was executed and settled without a dedicated line item in the Tenaska extract.

**Conclusion**: The −$3,964.90 DART Virtual Net is primarily a physical BESS P&L artifact (DA energy buying at high DA prices vs RT). The dart-virtual-trader's 4 basis position recommendations at HE19-22 cannot be confirmed or denied from this Tenaska data. The settlement infrastructure gap (W25 plan open item) remains the binding constraint.

The Tenaska DA_Energy_Amt + RT_Energy_Amt figure aggregates physical BESS energy settlements with any virtual settlements. On Jun 24, the −$4,005.31 DA Energy is overwhelmingly from BESS physical charging operations (GKS charging 50+ MWh DA at HE08-12 and HE15-20). Using this figure as a hit-rate measurement for virtual positions is incorrect.

**Action**: Escalate to pnl-manager to request a separate endpoint or row filter that isolates DART virtual financial settlements from physical BESS energy settlements. The current aggregated figure is not usable for dart-virtual-trader calibration.
