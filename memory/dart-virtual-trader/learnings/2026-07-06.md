## 4. 핵심 학습 사항

### 학습 1 (CRITICAL): Smartbidder DA HE21 forecast — 29% systematic overestimate

The model used DA ref $58.64 from Smartbidder forecast. Actual DA settled $41.74. Error = −$16.90 (−29%).
The RT side also erred: Smartbidder forecast $33.08, actual $38.94 (+$5.86, +18%).
Both errors compressed the spread: forecast +$25.56 → actual +$2.80 (89% smaller).

This is a scarcity premium miscalibration. On the first Monday after July 4 holiday, the solar cliff at HE21 was real but the DA market did NOT price in the premium that Smartbidder predicted. Possible explanations:
- Generation return after holiday (outage units back) suppressed DA premium
- Solar contribution at HE21 (Solar HE21 = 948 MW) was small but the transition was faster than expected
- DA bidders priced HE21 modestly given relaxed holiday ramp-up

**Action**: Apply a 20-25% haircut to Smartbidder DA HE21 forecast for post-holiday Mondays. Use adjusted DA ref = 0.75 × Smartbidder DA ref as the spread calculation input for sizing decisions. (Corrected: 0.75 × $58.64 = $43.98, closer to actual $41.74.)

### 학습 2 (HIGH): HE19 missed — $11.01/MWh spread, P~59-60%, floor fail

The model skipped HE19 because P(DA>RT) was just below 60% and the floor condition also failed. The actual spread was +$11.01/MWh — the second-largest spread of the day, after HE07 morning.

If INC had been taken at HE19 with 35 MW: +$385 profit.

The expected value of HE19: (2 × 0.595 − 1) × 35 × $11.01 ≈ 0.19 × 35 × $11.01 = **+$73** at ex-ante parameters. However the realized result would have been 35 × $11.01 = +$385.

This suggests the floor rule (0.6 × E[spread] ≥ $4) may be discarding high-magnitude opportunities when P is marginally below threshold. A "high-EV override" rule should be considered: if E[|spread|] > $8/MWh AND P > 55%, enter at quarter-size.

**Action**: Add high-EV override rule proposal to `plans/` for model v4.

### 학습 3 (HIGH): Morning cluster HE01-08 all INC — systematically missed

All morning off-peak hours (HE01-HE08, CT 00:00-08:00) had positive spreads ranging from +$1.52 to +$11.50. HE07 (06:00-07:00 CT, early morning solar ramp-up / wind inflection) was the largest spread of the day. These were all skipped as "P<60%, floor fail."

The RT spike veto (GR_WEST < 5,500 MW AND load > 80,000 MW) was active for HE15-18:

**Action**: Narrow the RT spike veto to HE15-17 only in summer when wind is critically low. HE18 represents the post-afternoon shoulder where RT tends to undershoot DA (solar contribution fading but demand also dropping). Consider releasing HE18 from the veto when forecast GR_WEST > 500 MW AND INC spread forecast > $5.
