## 3. Why Only HE22 Shows Settlement

The GKS DART Virtual settlement recorded only HE22. Three competing hypotheses:

**Hypothesis A — Virtual positions only partially submitted to ERCOT DAM**: GKS (via Smartbidder) may have only submitted virtual bids at HE22. The other 6 recommended positions (HE10-12 HB_WEST, HE19-21 HB_HOUSTON) may not have been entered into the DAM, either because Smartbidder's own book did not include them or the ERCOT submission cutoff was missed for those nodes/hours.

**Hypothesis B — Positions submitted but cleared only at HE22**: Smartbidder's virtual bids may have been submitted across all 7 hours, but only HE22 cleared in the DAM auction (other hours priced out or not awarded).

**Hypothesis C — Smartbidder's virtual book does not align with dart-virtual-trader recommendations**: Smartbidder runs its own virtual strategy independently. The $146.75 at HE22 is Smartbidder's own position, not the dart-virtual-trader recommended book. The "GKS DART Virtual" line reflects what Smartbidder actually executed, which may have no direct relationship to the recommendations issued here.

**Current best assessment**: Hypothesis C is most likely, given the 2026-06-11 learning note already flagging this ambiguity. The dart-virtual-trader recommendations are advisory inputs; Smartbidder (and GKS physical operations) execute their own positions independently. The +$146.75 at HE22 confirms that a SHORT DA position at HB_HOUSTON HE22 was in fact profitable — consistent with the direction recommended — but the scale ($146.75 vs $188 forecast) reflects Smartbidder's own position sizing, not the 25 MW recommendation from this agent.

## 4. Key Learnings (Updated with Actual Data)

**Learning 1: HE22 direction was correct; 0.6x compression slightly too conservative for this hour**
- Realized spread $5.87/MWh vs 0.6x-compressed estimate $4.5/MWh. The actual was 30% above the compressed estimate.
- Raw (pre-compression) E[spread] was $7.5/MWh; realized was 78% of that — closer to 0.8x than 0.6x for a late-evening MED hour on a Wednesday.
- Implication: the 0.6x blanket compression may understate HE22 evening spreads on mid-week high-load days. Do not change the factor yet (single data point), but flag for tracking.

**Learning 2: HE10-12 HB_WEST and HE19-21 HB_HOUSTON — zero confirmed settlement**
- The Smartbidder/GKS actual recorded $0.00 for all 6 positions other than HE22. This does NOT confirm those positions lost money — they may simply not have been submitted to ERCOT DAM by Smartbidder.
- Critical gap: until it is confirmed whether HB_WEST virtual positions at HE10-12 are ever submitted to ERCOT by the GKS/Smartbidder system, forecasting P&L for those positions is speculative. If Smartbidder never submits HB_WEST virtuals, the E[P&L] of $1,500 for HE10-12 is entirely notional.
- Action for operator: confirm with Smartbidder whether HB_WEST (non-hub) virtual positions are within the Smartbidder submission scope.

**Learning 3: Effective hit rate calculation requires knowing which positions were actually submitted**
- Current confirmed data: 1 submitted position (HE22, SHORT DA HB_HOUSTON, 25 MW) → WIN ($5.87/MWh realized).
- Cannot calculate a meaningful hit rate across the 7-position book when only 1 position has a confirmed settlement record.
- The trailing hit rate estimate of ~60-65% (proxy) is not invalidated by this data, but it also cannot be confirmed.

**Learning 4: Forecast-to-actual gap of 95% is a model recalibration signal — but causation matters**
- If Hypothesis C is correct (Smartbidder executes its own book, not the recommendations), the 95% gap is not a model forecast error — it is simply a mismatch between the advisory positions and the execution book.
- If Hypothesis A or B is correct (partial submission or partial clearing), the gap reflects real execution shortfall and the model is severely overestimating achievable P&L per cycle.
- Do NOT reduce position sizes or spread estimates based on this gap alone until the submission mechanism is understood.

**Learning 5: Non-Spin $9.19 at HE21 — half-size rule outcome still not confirmed**
- HE21 GKS DART Virtual = $0.00. Cannot determine whether the 25 MW (vs 50 MW) half-size saved money or left money on the table.
