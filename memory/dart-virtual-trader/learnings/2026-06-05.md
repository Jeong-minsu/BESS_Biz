### Finding 2: Friday vs Saturday Spread Compression Pattern Confirmed

Comparing Friday June 5 vs Saturday June 6:
- HE20 P(DA>RT): Friday 67.2% vs Saturday 63.4% (lower P on Saturday despite higher absolute spread)
- HE20 spread: Friday $6.0 vs Saturday $9.55 (higher spread on Saturday — unusual)
- HE21 spread: Friday $3.6 (SKIP) vs Saturday $10.19 (HIGH)

The Saturday 2026-06-06 spread profile is materially stronger than the Friday despite lower gross load. The explanation: the Saturday solar cliff (+21,865 MW peak → 592 MW at HE21) is identical in absolute magnitude but starts from a higher gross load fraction — the solar-to-gross-load ratio is larger on a 58 GW Saturday than on a 69 GW Friday, meaning the solar exit represents a larger proportional stress. Additionally, the NL ramp (+10,630 MW Saturday vs +4,662 MW Friday) is more than 2x larger.

**Model implication**: Gross load level alone is a poor predictor of DART spread. The net load ramp and solar cliff magnitude are superior drivers. The model should weight these over the day-type (weekday vs weekend) classification. Saturday 2026-06-06 is a better trading day than Friday 2026-06-05 by all spread and probability measures, despite Saturday having 11 GW less gross load.
