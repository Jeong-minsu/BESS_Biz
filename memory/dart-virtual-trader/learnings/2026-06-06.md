## 3. Key Findings

**Finding 1 (Critical): DART virtual positions ARE being submitted — -$5,266 confirms operational execution.**

The recurring open item from 14 prior proxy cycles — whether GKS DART virtual positions are actually submitted to ERCOT DAM — is now resolved. The Tenaska PTP settlement shows dart_virtual_revenue = -$5,266.40, a real negative settlement. Positions are being executed in production. Prior cycles showing dart_virtual_revenue = $0.00 in the GKS JSON were either settlement lag or a different data vintage. This is the most important operational resolution in the tracked cycle history.

**Finding 2: Saturday evening DA-RT direction was completely wrong — RT dominated DA in HE19-23.**

The model's SHORT DA thesis at HE19-22 assumed DA would be expensive relative to RT (spread > 0). The actual pattern inverted: RT was expensive at HE20-24 CT, generating large positive RT energy settlement for GKS's physical battery (e.g., HE22 +$1,571, HE23 +$2,534). The physical BESS was dispatching into expensive RT — meaning DA cleared BELOW RT in those hours (spread = DA − RT was negative), directly opposite the DART virtual position direction.

Root cause candidates: (a) DA market underpriced the Saturday evening scarcity, (b) ORDC or unscheduled outages in RT drove RT well above DA prices, (c) thin Saturday DAM liquidity caused DA to clear far below the actual RT scarcity price.

**Finding 3: The Non-Spin and NL ramp signals pointed to RT scarcity — which should have signaled LONG DA / SHORT RT (DEC), not SHORT DA / LONG RT (INC).**

The issuance thesis used Non-Spin $12.19/MWh at HE21 and NL ramp +10,630 MW as conviction signals for SHORT DA (INC offer — sell DA, buy RT). But extreme scarcity signals (high Non-Spin, extreme NL ramp) are also consistent with RT spiking far above DA — which would favor LONG DA / SHORT RT (DEC bid) instead. When Non-Spin > $10/MWh on a high-ramp day, the prior model assumption (DA prices in the scarcity, RT realigns lower) may be backwards: DAM may under-price relative to RT dispatch.

**Finding 4: Saturday thin DAM is a structural risk that was under-weighted.**

Saturday DAM volume is materially lower than weekday DAM. Thin liquidity means DA clearing can deviate significantly from the "efficient" level — either too high (model SHORT DA wins) or too low (RT spikes above DA, model SHORT DA loses). The prior learning noted Saturday thin DAM as a checklist item. This cycle confirms the risk is real: the loss of -$5,266 on a 225 MW Saturday position is directly attributable to DA clearing below RT, consistent with thin-DAM underpricing of scarcity.

**Finding 5: GKS physical battery profited substantially from the same RT price spike that caused the DART virtual loss.**

Total GKS revenue on 2026-06-06 was +$5,715. The RT energy profit (+$4,113) and RRS (+$648) in HE20-24 are directly explained by the RT price spike that caused the DART virtual -$5,266. This is a natural hedge: when the physical BESS dispatches into expensive RT, the DART virtual SHORT DA position loses (because DA was cheaper than RT). The two positions are partially offsetting by design in the GKS portfolio. The net day including DART virtual loss was still positive at +$5,715, but DART virtual subtracted $5,266 from what would have been ~$10,981 in physical P&L.

## 6. Learnings Applied to 2026-06-08 (Monday) Positions

**Learning A (HIGH priority): When Non-Spin DA > $10/MWh on an extreme NL ramp day, evaluate LONG DA / SHORT RT (DEC bid) in addition to SHORT DA.**

Extreme scarcity signals (Non-Spin > $10, NL ramp > +8,000 MW) do NOT unambiguously favor SHORT DA. If DA underprices scarcity (especially on thin-DAM Saturday), RT spikes above DA and SHORT DA loses. Rule proposal: when Non-Spin > $10/MWh, run a dual-direction scenario check — which direction wins if RT clears 20-30% above DA? Do not apply SHORT DA blindly if that scenario has >30% probability.

**Learning B (HIGH priority): Scale Saturday notional to 50% of weekday equivalent until DAM liquidity risk is better characterized.**

Saturday DAM thin liquidity can cause DA to deviate unpredictably from RT. Until a track record of confirmed Saturday settlements (at least 4-6 Saturdays) is available, cap Saturday active notional at 50% of the modeled size (e.g., 225 MW weekday conviction → 112 MW Saturday maximum). This limits the tail loss on a directional failure like 2026-06-06.
