## 2. HE13 Outperformance

HE13 was recommended at MED tier (25 MW, P=62%, E[spread]=+$9.0). Actual realized spread was **+$6.11** at the system level, but the nodal execution produced **+$1,101.50** — implying roughly $44/MWh realized at 25 MW (or SB executed larger size than recommended). This is a strong win, but the source is likely a combination of:

- Solar ramp collapse between HE12 and HE13 driving RT prices sharply lower (RT fell to $11.80 vs DA $17.91)
- Possible HB_WEST congestion working in favor (WEST_TO_NORTH constraint keeping RT low at WEST while DA was set system-wide)

This was a **partial surprise** in magnitude. Direction was correct and the recommendation captured it, but the $1,100+ hourly P&L was well above the $9.0 × 25 MW = $225 gross estimate. Either SB executed more MW than 25, or the nodal spread was far wider than the HB_WEST system proxy suggested. Calibration takeaway: midday solar-ramp hours at HB_WEST can have explosive RT collapses — the MED sizing may be systematically undersized on these hours.
