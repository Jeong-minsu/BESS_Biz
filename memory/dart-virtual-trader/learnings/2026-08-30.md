## 4. Error Analysis

### Error 1 — CRITICAL: EXTREME-tier INC direction was wrong in summer heat wave context

The recommendation used INC direction at EXTREME NL depth (< 22,000 MW), citing:
1. Aug 11 EXTREME precedent (EXTREME → INC)
2. Market-analyst INC directional call for HE09-13
3. Aug 29 daily avg spread +$5.66 (INC-consistent)

**Why INC was wrong:**

INC (Short DA / Long RT) wins when DA > RT, i.e., spread = DA - RT > 0.

At EXTREME NL depth in summer heat wave:
- DA clears very negative ($-80 to -$150) due to: massive solar surplus (33,396 MW peak), committed thermal must-run, GKS and other generators forced to dump energy at DA.
- RT does NOT go negative because: summer heat wave maintains substantial load (71,007 MW avg load on Aug 30 = significant thermal must-run; GKS rt_con_mwh data confirms RT clearing at $16+/MWh thermal floor).

**Result: DA << RT (both in magnitude and sign) → spread = DA - RT = very negative → DEC wins.**

The correct EXTREME-tier trade in a summer heat wave is DEC (Long DA / Short RT):
- Buy at DA (negative price → receive payment)
- Sell at RT (positive price → receive payment)
- Double profit: receive money on both legs.

### Error 2 — Wrong application of Aug 11 INC precedent

The Aug 11 EXTREME INC direction was the founding precedent. This was applied as-is to Aug 30. However, no validation of the Aug 11 INC direction exists (dart_virtual_revenue null for 40+ days). The Aug 11 scenario may have had different fundamental conditions (e.g., if thermal commitment was lower, RT could have gone negative too, making INC/DEC direction ambiguous). This precedent was unvalidated.

**Learning**: Never apply historical directional precedents without understanding the underlying mechanism. The mechanism check for Aug 30 would have been: "Will RT stay positive (thermal floor)?" Answer: Yes, because Aug 30 had 71,007 MW avg load → heavy thermal must-run → RT > $0. Therefore DEC wins even at EXTREME NL depth.

### Error 3 — Misapplication of Aug 29 +$5.66 daily avg signal

The Aug 29 daily avg spread was +$5.66/MWh (positive = INC-consistent). This was a MODERATE-tier duck floor day, not EXTREME. The physics at MODERATE is different: DA may clear at -$30 to -$60, and RT could also clear very low (but not necessarily at thermal floor), making the direction more ambiguous. The INC-consistent Aug 29 result does not carry forward to EXTREME-tier Aug 30.

**Learning**: Day-type tier boundaries matter critically for directional signal. EXTREME tier in summer heat wave behaves differently from MODERATE tier.
