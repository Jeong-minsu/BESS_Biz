### 학습 2 (CONFIRMED): Smartbidder HE21 DA forecast systematic overestimate persists

7/6 learnings identified a 29% DA overestimate (forecast $58.64 → actual $41.74). For 7/7, the model reference was DA $56.15 for HE21. Based on the 7/6 pattern and 7/7 overall DASPP avg of $28.07 (24h), the actual HE21 DA on 7/7 was likely $38-45/MWh — again ~25-30% below Smartbidder forecast.

The 20-25% haircut rule (0.75 × Smartbidder DA ref) proposed in 7/6 learnings is now validated across two data points. **This haircut should be applied in model v4 going forward, particularly for high-spread INC hours where DA scarcity premium is being modeled.**

For 7/9: HE21 DA ref = $36.72. Applying 0.75× = $27.54 (too aggressive — reduces spread dramatically). BUT: 7/9 uses a lower DA forecast to begin with ($36.72 vs $56.15), so the haircut less applicable. The overestimate pattern was specific to very high DA forecasts (>$50). For moderate DA forecasts ($30-40), the haircut may overcorrect. **Revised rule: apply 0.80× haircut only when Smartbidder HE21 DA forecast > $45/MWh.**

### 학습 3 (NEW): P(win) vs E[spread] disconnect — high P, low spread

7/7 HE22 had P(DA>RT) = 67.4% from Smartbidder but E[spread] = +$11.35/MWh (model estimate). The 7/9 CSV shows HE22 P(DA>RT) = 70.7% but market-analyst E[spread] = +$3.82/MWh. This pattern confirms: Smartbidder's probability classifiers can be high (signaling direction confidence) while the market-analyst's expected spread magnitude is low.

Under the current model, the floor rule ($4.00/MWh) appropriately filters out hours where P is high but the expected payoff per MW is insufficient. HE22 on 7/9 ($2.29/MWh floor value) is correctly skipped even though P=70.7%.

**Action**: No model change needed. The floor rule correctly handles this case. However, flag it as evidence that P and E[spread] are semi-independent — do not use P alone as sizing input.

### 학습 4 (MEDIUM): Late-evening DEC direction risk on 7/7

The 7/7 settlement shows overall RT > DA by $1.82/MWh, with heavy RT charging costs at HE01-02, HE06-07. The evening hours (HE20-23) showed mixed signals: HE20 very high RT, HE21-23 with scarcity-priced nonspin. This is DIFFERENT from 7/6 (post-holiday Monday wind collapse → DEC won late) because on 7/7 (Tuesday), GR_WEST was recovering but the physical scarcity from midday outages persisted.

The −7 ppt correction for HE22-24 proposed in 7/6 learnings applies specifically to post-holiday Mondays with wind collapse. On 7/9 (Thursday with strong GR_WEST 10,000+ MW → 15,700 MW overnight), the correction does NOT apply. Wind recovery suppresses RT → INC (DA > RT) more likely in late-evening.

**Action**: The summer late-evening Smartbidder correction should be conditional: apply −7 ppt only when GR_WEST < 5,000 MW at HE22-24. For 7/9 with GR_WEST 13,500-15,700 MW, no correction applied.
