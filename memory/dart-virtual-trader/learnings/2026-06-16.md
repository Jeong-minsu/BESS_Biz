Consider: for hours where DA > $65 AND Non-Spin > $8 (scarcity modifier applies), the realized spread has been larger than forecast, suggesting the scarcity premium is being over-absorbed by the DA market (DA set high but RT does not follow). This is the inverse of the 2026-06-06 and 2026-06-14 failures where RT spiked above DA in scarcity.

**Two scarcity regimes** now observed:
- Regime A (2026-06-14, 2026-06-06): Non-Spin scarcity → RT spike → RT > DA → INC LOSES
- Regime B (2026-06-15): Non-Spin scarcity → DA over-prices scarcity → RT stays low → DA >> RT → INC WINS

**Distinguishing factor hypothesis**: In Regime A, the scarcity was primarily supply-side (wind bust, outage). In Regime B, the scarcity was primarily price-discovery (DAM over-anticipated) and supply was adequate (wind/solar above forecast at time of settlement). Need more confirmed cycles to formalize. Current rule (Non-Spin > $8 → half-size; > $20 → skip) is risk-balanced but misses Regime B upside.
