## 5. Learnings Reflected in 2026-06-18 Positioning

The 2026-06-18 position report (`reports/daily/dart-virtual-trader/2026-06-18.md`) was already issued (2026-06-17 07:30 CT) before this self-review. The following connects this cycle's observations to that day's decisions:

### Learning 1 — DEC direction without Smartbidder carries high bid-fill uncertainty
At HE20, the DEC bid was set at $70 (upper end of $65-80 structural DA estimate). If DA cleared at $65 or below, the DEC bid may not have filled, producing zero exposure rather than a loss or win. The 2026-06-18 report avoided this problem by shifting to INC direction (Short DA / Long RT), where INC offer prices are set at or below expected DA, making fills more likely. The INC structure (offer at expected DA) is mechanically more robust than DEC bids when DA price estimates are uncertain.

### Learning 2 — Speculative tail positions (sub-55% P) should not be included when settlement is uncertain
The HE22 HB_WEST position generated no information value given DEGRADED settlement. The 2026-06-18 report correctly omits HB_WEST positions (WEST_TO_NORTH NEGLIGIBLE 2-4%) and applies the correct threshold. However, the 2026-06-18 report includes a new HB_HOUSTON / HB_NORTH basis leg at HE19-22 which is also Stage 0 heuristic with P(binding) 35-50%. This is analogous in uncertainty profile to the 2026-06-16 HB_WEST tail — the difference is that HB_HOUSTON/HB_NORTH basis positions use the HOUSTON_IMPORT_345 constraint (P HIGH, not LOW) which is structurally more established.

### Learning 3 — Three consecutive Smartbidder-absent cycles (2026-06-12, 2026-06-14, 2026-06-16) indicate a systemic fetch problem
This is the third cycle in five flowdays where Smartbidder data was unavailable. This pattern suggests the fetch script or credentials require diagnosis — not a one-off failure. The 2026-06-18 position report also notes Smartbidder as NOT AVAILABLE (fourth consecutive cycle in this stretch). This is a systemic issue requiring user action, not an agent-side fix.

### Learning 4 — DEC vs INC regime for evening hours on Tuesday
The 2026-06-16 cycle used DEC (Long DA / Short RT) for HE19-21, while the prior confirmed cycle on 2026-06-15 used INC (Short DA / Long RT) for HE21-22 and produced +$945.92. Both directions have structural justification on different evenings — DEC on high-NL-ramp evenings (RT expected to spike), INC on afternoons where DA is set high and RT stays low (Regime B from 2026-06-16 morning learning). The 2026-06-16 DEC thesis was driven by the largest Tuesday NL ramp in the tracked series (+8,770 MW). This structural rationale for DEC remains sound — but the outcome is unknown until settlement is recovered.
