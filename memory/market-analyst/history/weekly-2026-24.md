## 4. State / Federal Policy

### Texas 2025 Legislative Session (89th Legislature) — Final Enacted Laws Operative

The 89th Texas Legislature's regular session ended June 2025. Key enacted laws now operative or approaching implementation:

**Senate Bill 6 (signed June 2025)**: Empowers ERCOT to remotely disconnect data centers and other large loads during grid emergencies. Establishes new requirements governing planning, interconnection, and operation of large electrical loads. [Live fetch — JW.com "Senate Bill 6 & Upcoming Changes to Texas Electric Regulations"; Baker Botts July 2025 analysis] ERCOT is currently implementing remote disconnect protocols — this is part of the rationale for the Batch Zero attestation/readiness requirements (large loads must demonstrate they can respond to dispatch instructions and emergency curtailment).

**SB 2627 and HB 1500**: Established dispatchable generation completion bonus grants — up to $120,000/MW for new dispatchable generation interconnected by June 1, 2026, and $80,000/MW if interconnected before June 1, 2029. Total program cap: 10,000 MW. [Live fetch — Vinson & Elkins "Texas Passes SB2627 and HB1500"] The June 1, 2026 deadline for the higher bonus tier has now passed, shifting eligible new projects to the $80,000/MW tier.

No new Texas special session has been announced. The 90th Legislature would convene in regular session January 2027. No state energy legislation in the W24 (June 8–14) window was identified.

### Federal Policy — No New W24 Developments

## 7. Key Takeaways for Trading

- **PGRR145 Batch Zero is law (effective July 10)**: The 25-day window to developer submission deadline (July 15) is the most consequential near-term policy event. Market participants watching ERCOT market notices for Batch Zero attestation volume will get the first signal on how much of the 100 GW candidate queue actually progresses. A high attestation rate (>50 GW) signals a step-change in forward net load trajectory — bullish for long-run scarcity and AS prices. A low attestation rate (<20 GW) suggests the queue is more speculative than operational.

- **BESS capacity crossing 16 GW compresses AS margins**: June's record commissioning activity pushes total ERCOT BESS capacity to approximately 16 GW — a 14% jump from the 14 GW at start of 2026. Each GW of new BESS competing for the same Non-Spin, RRS, and ECRS procurement volumes reduces average hourly AS prices. GKS's revenue stack will need to rely proportionally more on energy arbitrage as summer progresses. The increasing midday solar-crash RT risk (HE11–14 on high-solar days, empirically confirmed June 6) must be managed via curtailment or repositioning to the charging window.

- **June record demand probable this week or next**: The heat wave signal ("ERCOT expects Texas power demand to break June record") combined with above-normal temperature forecasts through late July means the DA/RT spread structure should support elevated HE16–21 discharge revenue in the near term. The prior June record of 67,887 MW (June 1) is within range on any hot weekday in June 8–14. Watch for ERCOT Watch or Conservation Request notices if load exceeds 80 GW with low reserve margin.

- **DRRS status is the hidden binary**: If NPRR1309 received Board Priority designation at the June 1–2 meeting (unconfirmed), PUCT action at the June 18 meeting could set a concrete commercial launch date for DRRS in H2 2026. DRRS at even $5/MWh would add ~$4.3M/year to a 100 MW asset running 24/7. GKS's 2-hour duration is at the eligibility margin; Raven BESS sizing decision should factor in whether 4-hour duration (DRRS-qualifying) or 2-hour duration (DRRS-marginal) offers better risk-adjusted NPV.

- **West Texas export constraint remains structural through at least 2028**: The 765-kV STEP project legal friction (landowner opposition, ~400 miles of route additions contested by 1,400+ property owners) does not change the near-term CRR opportunity in the WEST-to-Hub direction. GR_WEST wind dispatch pattern (daytime trough / overnight peak) continues to produce the primary ERCOT zonal price divergence structure. No structural transmission relief before 2028 under any scenario.
