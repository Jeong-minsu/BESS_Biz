## 3. Root Cause Analysis — Negative Price Narrative Failure

The negative-price narrative (DA < $0 at HE10 when net load < ~20 GW) was embedded in the briefings from 08/10 through 08/14 and has now been definitively falsified by the 08/12 P&L. The root cause:

**[RCA-1] Missing ERCOT Market Mechanism Knowledge — Thermal Minimum Generation**

The briefings assumed that net load < thermal minimum capacity → curtailment → negative prices. The actual mechanism is different: ERCOT thermal generators submit economic minimum-generation bids (~$8-15/MWh); when net load is low, all thermal stays at minimum but the marginal unit sets price at its minimum-gen bid (positive). Only if VRE output exceeds system load MINUS thermal minimum generation (a much lower threshold) do prices go negative. At 17,060 MW net load with 9,042 MW cap-out, the required thermal dispatch ≈ 17 GW — still above most thermal minimum-generation floors — keeping prices at ~$8-15/MWh.

The negative-price threshold for ERCOT DA appears to require net load well below 15,000 MW (when thermal min-gen constraints cannot absorb all available VRE) rather than the 17,000-22,000 MW range where the briefings were predicting negatives.

**[RCA-2] No Historical Calibration During High-VRE Summer Events**

The pre-dome P&L anchors (08/04: $20.10/MWh; 08/08: $27.45/MWh) were the only real price data available. These were at 53,000+ MW net load avg — far from the 17,000-22,000 MW duck floor levels seen in the post-dome period. Without data points below 30,000 MW net load, the model extrapolated to negative prices incorrectly. The 08/12 P&L provides the first sub-36,000 MW net load calibration point ($18.84/MWh; HE10 = $12.62 at 17,060 MW).

**[RCA-3] Single-Source, No Smartbidder, No ERCOT DAM Price Signal**

Smartbidder provides DA clearing price distributions (P(price>0) at each HE). Without it, the briefings had no market-derived signal that the DAM expected positive clearing. If Smartbidder had been available, it would have likely shown P(DA>$0 at HE10) near 90%+ even with 17,060 MW net load, contradicting the negative-price narrative.

**Corrective action for future briefings**: The duck floor DA price at 15,000-25,000 MW net load in ERCOT should be modeled as **positive, anchored near thermal minimum-gen marginal cost ($8-18/MWh)**, not as negative. Negative prices only occur when net load approaches or is below ERCOT's thermal minimum generation floor (~12,000-15,000 MW estimated, though the exact level requires further calibration). The 08/12 anchor ($12.62/MWh at 17,060 MW) is now embedded as a positive reference point.

## 6. Key Learning Summary

1. **ERCOT DA market does not produce negative prices at 17,000-22,000 MW net load**: The 08/12 P&L confirms DA cleared positive at the duck floor (+$12.62/MWh at HE10 = 17,060 MW). The negative-price narrative embedded in briefings since 08/10 was incorrect. The threshold for negative DA clearing in ERCOT appears to require net load well below 15,000 MW — not the levels seen during the post-dome event. All future duck floor price estimates use positive anchors.

2. **08/12 DA avg actual: $18.84/MWh — moderate, not depressed**: Despite the extreme duck floor depth and the highly bearish supply picture, the DA average was close to the pre-dome reference ($20.10/MWh on 08/04). The market efficiently managed the intraday price curve: low-but-positive duck floor prices (HE09-12: $9-16/MWh) combined with moderate evening prices (HE20-21: $22-23/MWh DA). The extreme VRE events did not drive the DA average below $15/MWh even on the deepest duck floor days.

3. **GKS BESS charged at positive RT prices during the duck floor (HE09-10)**: The P&L shows RT Energy charges at HE09 ($14.02/MWh) and HE10 ($12.26/MWh) — the battery was paying positive prices to charge, not receiving negative prices. The charging strategy was correct in identifying the lowest-price hours, but the expected negative-price bonus was absent. This reduces the expected revenue from the duck-floor charge strategy materially vs the briefing assumptions.

4. **Evening commitment risk at HE19-21 was the real opportunity, not HE10 negative pricing**: The 08/12 RT premium at HE19 ($28.82, spread -$11.68), HE20 ($31.69, spread -$9.62), and HE21 ($29.50, spread -$6.30) represented the actual high-value period. GKS BESS captured significant Non-spin AS revenue in HE19-23 ($300-409/MWh-hr range). The DA under-commitment risk at the solar cliff (HE19-21) was the correct structural call; the duck floor pricing was miscalibrated.

5. **Post-dome wind reversal is accelerating**: Three consecutive days of GR_WEST daytime trough decline (11,306 → 10,648 → 9,531 → 7,925 MW from 08/12 to 08/15) confirm the synoptic westerly flow is dissipating at an accelerating rate. The market is returning toward conventional August pricing regime with positive duck floors and moderate evening peaks.

6. **P&L data lag creates calibration gap**: The 08/12 P&L was not available when the 08/12, 08/13, and 08/14 learnings were written, leading to three consecutive days of uncalibrated price estimates. Earlier availability of Tenaska P&L data (target: T+1 vs current T+2) would allow same-day or next-day price recalibration. Escalate to pnl-manager agent.
