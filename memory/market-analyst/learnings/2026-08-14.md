## 3. Root Cause Analysis — Dead-Calm Wind Regime Transition

### [RCA-1] Dead-Calm Synoptic Regime vs. Gradual Relaxation

The 08/15 briefing (and all prior post-dome briefings) modeled the wind reversal as a gradual relaxation of the post-dome westerly flow toward the August climatological baseline. The actual 08/16 BIDCLOSE reveals that GR_WEST collapsed by -4,099 MW in a single day (08/15 → 08/16), reaching 3,826 MW at the trough. This is not gradual relaxation — it is an abrupt transition to dead-calm conditions.

The distinction matters because:
- **Gradual relaxation** (prior assumption): each day GR_WEST trough declines by ~600-1,600 MW; reaches August baseline (~7-9 GW trough) over 3-5 days; prices rise gradually.
- **Dead-calm abrupt transition** (actual 08/16): GR_WEST trough reaches 3,826 MW in one day from 7,925 MW (-4,099 MW); the August baseline is not 7-9 GW but rather the thermal minimum (~2-4 GW) during a stagnant high-pressure system; prices escalate non-linearly as net load jumps +8,817 MW at HE21 in a single day.

The abrupt transition profile is the highest-consequence scenario for evening supply adequacy and was not explicitly modeled as a tail risk. The 08/15 briefing's flag that "below 7,000 MW confirms the reversal" implicitly assumed a gradual approach to 7,000 MW rather than a single-day skip from 7,925 to sub-4,000 MW.

### [RCA-2] Evening GR_WEST Night Recovery Assumption

Prior post-dome days showed GR_WEST maintaining 13-16 GW overnight even when daytime troughs were falling (e.g., 08/14 GR_WEST overnight = 14-16 GW despite HE13 daytime trough = 9,531 MW). The 08/15 briefing's HE21 net load estimate (59,101 MW) implicitly assumed GR_WEST at HE21 ≈ 20,054 MW (from the BIDCLOSE for 08/15). On 08/16, GR_WEST HE21 = 6,902 MW — a -13,152 MW collapse relative to what was embedded in the 08/15 estimate. This is the single largest contributor to the HE21 net load escalation (+8,817 MW).

The 08/15 briefing could not have known the 08/16 GR_WEST evening would be this low, as it was writing for 08/15. But the lesson is: when the dead-calm regime arrives, the overnight and evening wind recovery is suppressed in a way that is qualitatively different from a "transitional" day. The evening recovery that stabilizes GR_WEST above 13 GW in the prior series may not occur at all.

## 4. Methodology Gaps and Improvements

### [GAP-A] Dead-Calm Regime Tail Risk Not Modeled

The briefing protocol models the wind as a gradual relaxation toward a climatological baseline. The dead-calm tail (sub-5 GW GR_WEST daytime trough; sub-7 GW evening) was not included as a bear scenario despite three consecutive days of accelerating decline. For future briefings: when GR_WEST daytime trough falls below 8,000 MW, explicitly model a dead-calm bear scenario with GR_WEST trough at 3,000-5,000 MW and compute the resulting HE20-21 net loads and prices. A trough at 3,826 MW on the BIDCLOSE is the first realization of this tail; it will not be the last given the August climatology of West Texas.

### [GAP-B] Night Recovery Assumption Invalidated in Dead-Calm Conditions

Prior briefings relied on GR_WEST recovering to 13-16 GW overnight as a stabilizing signal for evening net loads. The 08/16 BIDCLOSE (HE01 = 13,961 MW declining to 3,826 MW by HE14, with evening at 6,902 MW) shows the overnight is higher but the daytime collapse is so severe that the evening is also very suppressed. Going forward: when the daytime trough is below 5,000 MW, assume the evening wind at HE21 will be in the 5,000-9,000 MW range rather than the 13-16 GW seen in the transitional days.

### [GAP-C] HE21 Price Anchor Above All References

The 08/15 briefing estimated HE21 at $25-33/MWh, anchored to 08/12's HE21 = $23.20/MWh at 54,947 MW and 08/04's HE21 = $32.16/MWh at 64,742 MW. For 08/16, HE21 = 67,918 MW exceeds all references. The protocol needs a mechanism to extrapolate beyond the highest calibration point. Proposed approach: use the 08/04 anchor ($32.16/MWh at 64,742 MW) and assume price escalation of approximately $3-5/MWh per 1,000 MW of additional net load above the anchor. At +3,176 MW: $32.16 + (3,176/1,000) × $4 = $32.16 + $12.70 = ~$44.86/MWh central estimate, with wide bands of ±$15/MWh at this unanchored extrapolation. This is more systematic than the $40-60+/MWh qualitative range used in the 08/16 briefing.

## 6. Key Learning Summary

1. **Dead-calm synoptic regime can produce single-day GR_WEST collapses of 4,000+ MW**: The 08/15 → 08/16 GR_WEST trough decline of -4,099 MW (7,925 → 3,826 MW) in one day demonstrates that the dead-calm summer anticyclone transition is abrupt, not gradual. Future briefings must include a dead-calm tail scenario (GR_WEST < 5,000 MW) when the post-dome wind reversal is confirmed.

2. **Evening wind (HE21) collapses with daytime in dead-calm conditions**: Prior post-dome days maintained 13-16 GW GR_WEST overnight even when daytime troughs were falling. In the dead-calm regime (08/16), HE21 GR_WEST = 6,902 MW — the evening recovery is suppressed. This produces dramatically higher evening net loads (+8,817 MW at HE21 on 08/16 vs 08/15).

3. **HE21 net load above all price calibration anchors on 08/16 (67,918 MW)**: The 08/04 reference (64,742 MW at $32.16/MWh DA) is the highest calibrated point. When net load exceeds the calibration range, price estimates require explicit extrapolation methodology and very wide confidence intervals.

4. **HE24 cap-out predictor continues to fail systematically**: Six consecutive days (08/10-08/16) of overnight cap-out exceeding the prior day's HE24 reading by 700-1,363 MW. The predictor appears systematically biased upward in the negative-to-low-price environment: generators file HE24 returns but then elect to extend maintenance overnight when price forecasts are below their cost threshold. This behavior will likely persist until the morning prices are high enough to make maintenance returns clearly optimal.

5. **08/15 positive duck floor framework validated structurally; price confirmation pending**: The 08/12 P&L recalibration (positive HE10 at $12.62/MWh) was correctly applied in the 08/15 briefing. The 08/15 price estimates ($12-18/MWh at HE10) are directionally correct based on the framework. Full numerical validation awaits 08/17 P&L delivery.

6. **Smartbidder DEGRADED (Day 21) — Opportunity Cost Now Maximum**: The 08/16 evening net load at 67,918 MW is the most consequential pricing uncertainty of the post-dome period. Smartbidder's DA clearing price distribution at HE20-21 on 08/16 would be the most valuable data point available. The absence of Smartbidder is most costly precisely when market conditions are most extreme and the price model has the widest confidence intervals. Escalate Smartbidder restoration urgency.
