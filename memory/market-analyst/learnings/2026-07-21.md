## 3. Root Cause Analysis

Three simultaneous failures on 7/21:

1. **DA HE20 underforecast (+$33.20)**: The briefing placed the DA spike at HE21 ($86.97). The actual DA auction cleared scarcity at HE20 ($98.95), one hour earlier. Smartbidder's ramp timing model placed peak stress at HE21; the actual DA market anticipated it at HE20.

2. **RT HE19-24 spike completely missed**: RT averaged $36.78 (full day) but HE19-24 range was $47-96. These extreme RT prices suggest a real-time resource shortage — likely wind came in below forecast during the critical 18:00-24:00 CT window. The 7/21 briefing had wind avg at 7,584 MW (COPHSL) but if afternoon wind was materially below (GR_WEST HE14: 654 MW forecast), real-time could have been even lower.

3. **Scarcity credibility gate misapplied**: The W29 protocol (Item 8 in 7/20 learnings) was designed to downgrade HE21 DA spike credibility. On 7/21, the gate logic was partially applied (treated $87 as "credible"). But the gate should have ALSO flagged HE20 as a separate high-scarcity hour, and should have included explicit RT spike scenarios for the full HE19-24 window.
