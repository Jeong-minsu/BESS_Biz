## 4. 2026-07-20 Carry-Forward Insights from 7/19 Pattern

1. **AG2 load beat is larger on hotter days**: 7/19 AG2 peak ~85.5 GW (vs YE 82 GW) was already unusual. 7/20 AG2 peaks at 90.8 GW (vs YE 86.2 GW, a +4.6 GW gap). This scales with the heat differential (100.4°F on 7/20 vs 96°F on 7/19). The upward bias in AG2 on extreme heat days requires updating the prior "+1.5–2.5 GW" rule to "+3–5 GW when temps exceed 99°F."

2. **Monday weekday post-hot-Sunday pattern is more dangerous than Sunday alone**: The Monday 2026-07-20 profile stacks a +5.2% load increase on top of a hot-day baseline AND a sharper duck curve. The DA $53 HE21 spike is structurally motivated — it reflects real thermal dispatch economics, not a model artifact.

3. **Duck curve ramp timing**: Sunday HE22 was net load peak; Monday HE21 is peak. This 1-hour shift means BESS discharge timing should be adjusted accordingly — prioritize HE21 over HE22 for DA energy bid on 7/20.
