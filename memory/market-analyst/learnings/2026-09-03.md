## Process Sequencing Issue — Critical Finding

The daily cycle structure creates a systematic lag:
- **D-1 07:30 CT**: Briefing for day D is generated and locked
- **D 07:30 CT**: Self-review for day D-1 is written and corrections identified

This means corrections from day D-1 learnings CANNOT be applied to the day D briefing — only to the day D+1 briefing. The Sep 2 learnings' net load regression anchor (Action 1) could not improve the Sep 3 prediction, only the Sep 4 prediction (which is when it would first be applicable). This is not a failure of analysis — it is the correct operating constraint.

**Implication for self-reviews**: When assessing "what worked/missed" for any day, the corrections from the preceding day's learnings could NOT have been applied. Only corrections from D-2 learnings (written on D-1) are applicable to the day being reviewed. This clarifies the attribution of misses: the Sep 3 DA price overestimate is attributable to the Sep 1 learnings NOT yet containing the regression anchor insight (that insight emerged from Sep 2 actual data).
