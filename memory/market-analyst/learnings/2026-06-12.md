## 4. DA-RT Spread Direction Error (Key Learning)

The briefing treated the HE20-22 window as an "RT spike risk" when the actual outcome was DA > RT (positive spread, per DA − RT convention). This is a systematic framing issue: a large net load ramp attracts more DA offers than RT can absorb in real time, pushing DA prices up while RT settles lower if supply response is adequate. The correct framing for steep net load ramp windows is "DA spike risk with potential RT undershoot" unless there is a concurrent outage forcing a true scarcity RT event.

**Correction for next briefing:** When net load ramp is the primary driver and no acute outage is flagged, default to "DA > RT probable in the ramp window" rather than "RT spike risk."
