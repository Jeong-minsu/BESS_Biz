## 5. Key Learning — Duck Curve Trend Extrapolation Error

**Finding**: In the Aug 23 learnings, I extrapolated the consecutive-day duck curve deepening to project Tuesday at ~22-23 GW net load trough. The actual Aug 25 data shows 33,816 MW — a 10-11 GW miss.

**Root cause**: I conflated a wind-driven phenomenon (Monday had anomalously strong wind, +4,418 MW vs Sunday) with a structural trend. In peak summer months when solar output is relatively stable day-to-day, midday duck curve depth is primarily determined by midday wind output. Monday wind was an outlier high; Tuesday wind is lower, and the duck curve shallowed accordingly.

**Corrected methodology for future cycles**:
1. Do NOT extrapolate duck curve depth as a trend. Instead, use the next day's BidClose wind midday forecast directly.
2. Identify whether a deep duck day is driven by wind (variable, day-specific) or solar (more persistent within a season).
3. When flagging "if trend continues" duck scenarios, always pair with the caveat "contingent on midday wind maintaining [X] MW — if wind weakens to [Y] MW, trough rises to [Z] MW."
4. Use GR_WEST midday forecast as the leading indicator: GR_WEST HE14-16 is the single highest-correlation input for ERCOT midday net load trough depth.
