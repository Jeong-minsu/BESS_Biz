## 5. Key Learning — Duck Curve Trend Extrapolation Error

**Finding**: The Aug 23 learnings projected Tuesday's net load trough at ~22–23 GW via linear extrapolation of consecutive-day deepening. Actual Aug 25 trough: 33,816 MW. Error: ~10–11 GW.

**Root cause**: Duck curve depth in summer is primarily a function of midday wind output, not a persistent day-over-day structural trend. Solar output is relatively stable within peak summer; wind drives day-to-day variance. Treating a wind-driven phenomenon as a mechanistic trend produced the largest directional error in the August heat wave series.

**Corrected methodology (implement immediately)**:
1. Do NOT extrapolate duck curve depth across days. Use next-day BidClose wind midday forecast directly.
2. Identify whether a deep-duck day is wind-driven (variable) or solar-driven (more persistent).
3. When citing day-over-day duck trends, always state: "contingent on GR_WEST midday floor maintaining [X] MW — if wind weakens to [Y] MW, trough rises to [Z] MW."
4. Use GR_WEST HE14–16 as the primary leading indicator for ERCOT midday net load trough depth.
