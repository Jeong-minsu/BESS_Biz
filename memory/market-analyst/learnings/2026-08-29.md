## 4. Root Cause Analysis

**Root cause #1: DA average was severely underestimated ($5-18 vs actual ~$30-45/MWh)**
- The 24h DA average was calculated by simple net-load-weighted mental model, overweighting the hours of deep negative net load (HE09-13).
- In ERCOT, thermal units that must commit for the HE19-20 ramp cannot back off at midday without incurring start-up cost recovery bids. These commitment costs floor DA prices across the full committed window even at hours of deep negative net load.
- Result: DA midday may clear slightly negative but the commitment cost floor prevents the $-60/MWh scenario from appearing broadly in DA. DA is anchored by marginal commitment economics; RT is where the true depth occurs.
- **Lesson**: During duck-curve heat wave events, DA average pricing is anchored HIGHER than net-load-weighted estimates suggest because thermal commitment economics dominate. RT midday can be deeply negative; DA midday tends to be less so.

**Root cause #2: Midday negative price timing was off (HE08-09, not HE10-11)**
- Yes Energy BidClose showed solar starting at HE08 with 2,015 MW. The BESS experienced large negative RT at HE08 (−$162.77) and HE09 (−$231.38). This suggests the critical net supply surplus began earlier than the net load trough.
- Why: At HE08, solar is ramping steeply (+16,195 MW from HE08 → HE09) while thermal units cannot reduce output quickly enough. The mismatch creates a brief but severe RT negative spike in the ramp period, before the trough settles into a sustained moderate negative at HE10-11.
- **Lesson**: The deepest RT negative may occur during the SOLAR RAMP HOUR (HE08-09) rather than the solar PEAK HOUR (HE10-11). The BESS charge strategy should prioritize the HE08-09 solar ramp window for RT charge capture, not just the midday plateau.

**Root cause #3: No Smartbidder + no AG2 for 34+ consecutive days**
- The price view quality has degraded materially without probabilistic DA/RT forecasts. The qualitative approach underestimates DA commitment cost floors and overestimates midday negative depth.
- Action: Smartbidder MSAL renewal is overdue at Day 36. GKS revenue was $8,222 vs what could have been captured with better midday timing. This is a direct revenue miss.
