## 1. Supply-Demand Structural Accuracy

The 08/12 briefing's load, wind, solar, and net load projections were the Yes Energy BIDCLOSE values themselves — these ARE the 08/12 actuals for the model-input layer. The structural review therefore focuses on whether the key narrative calls (duck floor depth, wind trend, cap-out behavior) were directionally accurate and what the 08/13 BIDCLOSE reveals about their resolution.

### [CORRECT-A] Duck Floor at 17,060 MW — Structural Accuracy

The 08/12 briefing identified the HE10 net load floor at 17,060 MW as the deepest of the event series and predicted negative DA prices in the -$15 to -$35/MWh range. The 08/13 BIDCLOSE subsequently showed HE10 = 16,701 MW (-359 MW, further deepening), confirming that the structural direction (continuous duck floor deepening through Day 4) was correct. The 08/12 briefing did not predict the additional 359 MW deepening on 08/13, but the directional call was right.

### [CORRECT-B] Cap-Out Spike at Duck Floor Hour — Pattern Called Correctly

The 08/12 briefing explicitly identified the HE10-12 cap-out spike to 9,042-9,056 MW as a structural anomaly (generators taking maintenance outages during anticipated negative-price windows). This call was directionally confirmed by the 08/13 BIDCLOSE, which showed continued strategic overnight maintenance scheduling (08/13 opened at 8,045 MW despite 08/12's HE24 = 7,348 MW, and 08/14 opened at 8,929 MW — the pattern persisted two additional days). The 08/12 briefing's identification of "strategic maintenance scheduling at negative-price hours" as the mechanism was accurate and embedded in the cap-out analysis for subsequent days.

### [CORRECT-C] GR_COASTAL Fourth Consecutive Event Record — Called Correctly

The 08/12 briefing projected GR_COASTAL would set a "new event record" on 08/13. The 08/13 BIDCLOSE confirmed HE18 = 4,270 MW (+166 MW above 08/12's HE18 = 4,104 MW) — the fourth consecutive record, as predicted. The coastal sea breeze intensification narrative was correct.

### [CORRECT-D] HE24 Predictor Unreliability — Identified in Real Time

The 08/12 briefing explicitly called out that the HE24 = 7,348 MW predictor should be treated as a "weak signal with ±1 GW uncertainty" after the HE24 failure pattern on 08/12 (08/11 HE24 = 6,827 MW → 08/12 morning = 8,777 MW). This skepticism was validated: 08/12's HE24 = 7,348 MW → 08/13 morning cap-out = 8,045 MW again failed the predictor (opening 697 MW above HE24), and 08/13's HE24 = 7,686 MW → 08/14 morning = 8,929 MW also failed (+1,243 MW overshoot).

### [MISS-A] GR_WEST Daytime Recovery Rate — Overstated

The 08/12 briefing projected continued GR_WEST daytime strengthening, noting "each day the daytime trough recovers by +3-4 GW." The 08/13 BIDCLOSE showed the first reversal: GR_WEST daytime trough declined from 11,306 MW on 08/12 to 10,648 MW on 08/13 (-658 MW). The 08/14 BIDCLOSE extended the reversal further to 9,531 MW (-1,117 MW additional). The 08/12 briefing did not anticipate the possibility of a GR_WEST daytime reversal at all. The narrative embedded a structural assumption of monotonic post-dome wind recovery that was too strong.

**Quantified miss**: projecting continued +1-2 GW/day GR_WEST improvement vs actual -658 MW decline on 08/13 and -1,117 MW on 08/14 = approximately 2-3 GW directional error in the most consequential wind variable for the duck floor calculation.

**Root cause**: Single-source (Yes Energy STWPF only) with no AG2 Windcast IQ or Enverus STPF cross-validation to detect the synoptic-flow peak. The 08/12 briefing had no information to distinguish between continued wind strengthening and the beginning of a synoptic relaxation.

### [MISS-B] GR_NORTH Recovery Rate (+500 MW/day) — Overestimated for 08/14+

The 08/12 briefing projected "at the post-dome recovery pace, 08/14 GR_NORTH avg may approach 2,500-2,750 MW." The 08/14 BIDCLOSE shows 1,924 MW — -576 to -826 MW below that projection, and actually LOWER than 08/13's 2,249 MW (a reversal). The +500 MW/day GR_NORTH trend was not sustained. This miss compounds the GR_WEST miss: both major wind regions recovered at lower rates than extrapolated from the 08/09-08/12 synoptic pattern.

### [MISS-C] Post-Dome Wind Recovery Duration — Implicitly Too Optimistic

Both MISS-A and MISS-B reflect a broader structural error: the 08/12 briefing (and the preceding days' briefings) implicitly treated the post-dome wind recovery as having further headroom for strengthening. The 08/10-08/12 trajectory (continuous improvement in all wind zones) was extrapolated forward. In reality, the synoptic peak appears to have occurred around 08/12-08/13, with reversal beginning on 08/13 and accelerating on 08/14. Without AG2 or Enverus synoptic meteorological analysis, the briefing had no mechanism to anticipate the synoptic peak.

**Improvement action**: Add a "Post-peak synoptic risk" flag to any briefing where two or more wind zones are simultaneously at multi-year-high levels and day-over-day gains are decelerating. Once the rate of improvement slows (as it did on 08/13: GR_WEST +88 MW avg vs 08/12's +2,043 MW avg), the probability of a reversal in the next 1-2 days is elevated.

## 3. Methodology Gaps and Improvements

### [GAP-A] Synoptic Peak Detection — Missing Signal

The 08/12 briefing had no mechanism to detect that the post-dome wind supply boost was approaching its peak. The 08/13 briefing (written 08/12) did flag the GR_WEST daytime reversal as a structural risk ("if 08/14 BIDCLOSE shows trough below 9,500 MW, this signals the reversal"), and the 08/14 BIDCLOSE at 9,531 MW essentially confirmed the signal.

**Recommended protocol**: When three or more consecutive days show the same wind metric at multi-year highs (e.g., GR_WEST daytime trough > 10 GW for 3+ days, which is far above the ~8 GW August baseline), explicitly add a "Synoptic deceleration watch" item to the Risks section. Flag when the day-over-day improvement rate decelerates more than 50% (e.g., 08/13's +88 MW avg vs prior days' +2,000+ MW avg). This deceleration flag would have appeared in the 08/13 briefing and provided an explicit 08/14 watch item.

### [GAP-B] Friday Demand Structure Not Explicitly Modeled in 08/12 Briefing

The 08/12 briefing was written for Wednesday and did not include any forward-looking Friday structure analysis. The 08/14 briefing needs to account for the standard Friday demand pullback (-1,200 to -1,500 MW avg vs Thursday). The impact: lower gross load on Friday partially moderates the tighter net load from weakening wind, but does not fully offset it. This interaction should be flagged in Thursday (D-1 for Friday) briefings explicitly.

**Improvement**: Add "Weekend transition note" to Thursday briefings (explicitly flag: Friday gross load -5-8% below Thursday avg; evening demand drops faster post-HE18; DA-RT inversion risk at HE19-21 from Friday demand underrun vs DAM commitment — see 08/08 learning CRITICAL-2).

### [GAP-C] GR_NORTH Recovery Rate Assumed Linear — Incorrect

The 08/12 briefing extrapolated the +500 MW/day GR_NORTH recovery linearly. The actual trajectory: 08/10 = 1,288 → 08/11 = 1,619 → 08/12 = 2,038 → 08/13 = 2,249 → 08/14 = 1,924 MW. The linear extrapolation broke precisely at the synoptic peak. GR_NORTH recovery appears non-linear and bounded — the ceiling appears to be around 2,000-2,500 MW daytime trough (not the 3,000-5,000+ MW nameplate potential) in the current synoptic regime.

**Improvement**: Cap GR_NORTH trend extrapolation at a "synoptic regime ceiling" rather than allowing unbounded linear projection. In the post-dome environment with a weakening westerly flow, the GR_NORTH recovery may be limited to 1,500-2,500 MW avg until the Panhandle receives a distinct strengthening event.

## 6. Key Learning Summary

1. **Post-dome wind recovery is non-monotonic** — the +3-4 GW/day improvement pattern broke on Day 4 (08/13 GR_WEST trough -658 MW) and accelerated on Day 5 (08/14 -1,117 MW). Linear extrapolation of a rapid recovery trend will systematically overstate wind supply as the synoptic flow approaches its peak and begins to relax.

2. **Duck floor reversal can be large (+5 GW in one day)** — the 08/13→08/14 shift from 16,701 to 21,796 MW net load floor is the largest single-day duck floor reversal of the event series. The previous expectation of gradual changes (±500 MW/day) was wrong; when the synoptic flow relaxes, the morning GR_WEST profile can drop 2+ GW in one day.

3. **The HE24 predictor has failed 4 consecutive days in the negative-price environment** — it should be treated as noise until confirmed by Smartbidder or AG2 cross-validation. The only reliable indicator of next-day overnight cap-out is a structural assessment of whether the market expects positive or negative midday prices (which determines the maintenance scheduling incentive).

4. **Net load avg can RISE despite lower gross load** if wind weakens faster than demand — the 08/14 vs 08/13 case (+1,983 MW higher net load despite -1,248 MW lower gross load) illustrates the risk of using gross load alone as the market tightness signal. Net load (which accounts for VRE supply) is the correct first-order metric.

5. **19th consecutive day without Smartbidder or cross-validation** — the identification of the synoptic peak reversal on 08/14 is entirely dependent on Yes Energy BIDCLOSE as a single source. Had GR_WEST trough been understated by 2+ GW in the STWPF (actual being much weaker), the price implications would be materially different and undetectable.
