## 4. Qualitative Assessment — Concerns and Weaknesses

**Concern 1 — DA peak hour labeling ambiguity (action item)**

The briefing simultaneously states "HE20 = $44.65 DA (top)" in the Headline and "$41.08 HE20 / $44.65 HE21" in the Price View table. The downstream agents (bess-optimizer, dart-virtual-trader) received this as HE20 = $41.08 in the daily report table (section 2 of 2026-06-06.md). **Rule update**: When Enverus early-solar-exit rule and Smartbidder table disagree on peak hour, state explicitly: "Smartbidder table peak = HE[X] = $Y. Enverus rule adjustment: peak shifted to HE[X-1]. Using [one value] as base case." Do not allow conflicting numbers to propagate across different sections of the same report.

**Concern 2 — Early RT spike risk for HE16-18 not flagged in Risks section**

The 2026-06-05 actuals showed the largest RT events at HE16-18, not HE19-21. The briefing's Risks section focused on HE20 outage/load surprise and Saturday DAM liquidity, but did not explicitly flag HE16-18 as early-spike candidates (despite the established rule from the 2026-06-04 learning). On days when the Enverus net-load ramp starting HE17-18 exceeds 2,000 MW/hour, the early-spike risk must appear in the Risks section, not only in the Key Drivers section (where it was implicit in point 2 about "HE19-20 solar exit").

**Rule update (reinforcement)**: The "RT spike precedes DA peak by 1-3 hours" rule must appear explicitly in the Risks section with a specific HE range (e.g., "HE17-19 early RT spike risk: monitor SCED dispatch patterns, especially if thermal ramp-up lags"). This is a carry-forward from the 2026-06-04 learning that was not properly operationalized into the Risks section format.

**Concern 3 — Overnight HE24 RT tail risk not discussed**

The 2026-06-05 actuals showed HE24 RT = $64.96 (vs DA = $33.07, spread = -$31.89). The briefing discussed P(DA<RT) for HE01-04 but did not separately flag HE24 as a potential overnight tail hour. The briefing should note that GR_WEST ramp overnight reduces but does not eliminate RT tail risk.
