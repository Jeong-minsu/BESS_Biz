## 2. Key Misses and Root Cause

**Miss 1 — Midday RT spike (HE8-11).** Actual RT at HE8-11 ranged $19-31/MWh against a forecast floor of $14-16/MWh. Root cause: the briefing assumed midday RT would track DA downward with solar output. Instead, BESS and other resources were charging heavily (GKS charged 57-58 MWh in HE11-12 alone), and RT prices reflected intra-hour dispatch tightness not captured in Smartbidder's 5-min model. The $111.68 RT revenue at HE08 and the HE09-10 RT spike were not anticipated.

**Miss 2 — Afternoon DA-RT spread magnitude (HE13-16).** Actual DA $20-25 / RT $8-12 in HE13-16 — a DA premium of $11-18/MWh. The briefing called this window as "solar-driven, DA cheap" (DA-RT negative in HE11-12). In fact, DA cleared high relative to RT because the DA market over-procured for anticipated evening ramp needs, while RT was suppressed by continued solar generation. This is a structural pattern: on high-solar weekdays, the afternoon DA-RT spread is often large and positive (DA expensive), not negative.

**Miss 3 — DART virtual timing.** The briefing flagged the Non-Spin / AS opportunity at HE20-22. Actual DART virtual revenue concentrated at HE12-13 ($610 + $1,102), not the evening ramp. The DA-RT spread inversion in the midday solar window was the actual DART alpha source — the reverse of what the briefing anticipated for that window.
