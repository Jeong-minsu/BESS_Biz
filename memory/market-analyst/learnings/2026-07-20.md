## 4. Root Cause: HE21 DA Phantom Spike (Second Consecutive Cycle)

### Pattern

| Date | HE21 DA Forecast | HE21 DA Actual | Miss |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026-07-19 | $34.21 | $15.60 | -$18.61 |
| 2026-07-20 | $53.12 | $25.33 | -$27.79 |

Smartbidder is generating escalating HE21 DA scarcity spikes that the DA auction is not clearing. The actual cleared price is converging around $25-30, not $35-53.

### Contributing Factor: Wind Gap at Threshold

On 7/20, the AG2 vs YE wind gap was 1,901 MW (+19.4%) — just below the 2 GW formal trigger defined in the W29 protocol. The briefing acknowledged this with a qualitative $3-5/MWh downside note but did not apply the formal -$5 to -$15/MWh DA evening adjustment.

In practice the required correction at HE21 was -$27.79/MWh. Even the top of the protocol range (-$15/MWh) would have left a $12.79 residual error. This implies the protocol range itself may be too narrow for the HE21 hours, which are the most non-linear.

### Structural Hypothesis (Needs More Cycles to Confirm)

The DA market participants may be using a combined wind + storage supply model that suppresses HE21 clearing prices even when net load appears tight. ERCOT's increasing BESS fleet (both DA virtual and physical capacity) may be bidding HE21 as a charging hour (DA virtual load), adding supply-side volume and depressing clearing prices below what Smartbidder's generation-only supply curve predicts.
