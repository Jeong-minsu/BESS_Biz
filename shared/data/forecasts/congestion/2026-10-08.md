## Constraint Analysis

### 1. WEST_SOLAR_EXPORT / Duck Curve (HE10-13) — SEVERE

**Net load trough**: 25,380 MW at HE11, 25,785 MW at HE10.
**Solar**: 30,256 MW (HE11) — 30,472 MW (HE12). Full October solar peak.
**Wind**: GR_WEST 236-517 MW. Near-zero during peak solar window.
**NL tier**: SEVERE (22,000-26,000 MW range per Stage 0 calibration).

Stage 0 NL-tier P(binding) calibration:
- MILD (NL > 32,000 MW): 6-15%
- MODERATE (NL 26,000-32,000 MW): 20-35%
- SEVERE (NL 22,000-26,000 MW): 50-75%

Oct 8 HE10-11 sits solidly in SEVERE tier. This is materially worse than the Oct 6 expectation (MODERATE-MILD, NL trough 28,000-36,000 MW estimated without live data). Live bidclose data confirms SEVERE.

**Primary mechanism**: ~30 GW of West and South Texas solar generation must be exported northward to NORTH and HOUSTON load zones. Transmission thermal limits on West-to-North paths are the binding constraint. Low wind (especially West) means no generation "displacement" that would reduce the export pressure.

**GKS MCC direction (HE10-13)**: Strongly negative. GK settlement point, being a South/West delivery node, receives the negative congestion price when solar floods. Directional signal: SELL congestion, BUY at NORTH.

**Cap-out amplifier**: ~20,000 MW of thermal capacity offline means less thermal generation available in the North/Houston zone to locally serve load — imports from West solar must flow harder, pressing constraints.

### 2. SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT / Evening Ramp (HE19-21) — ELEVATED

**NL ramp**: +21,515 MW from HE17 (40,841 MW) to HE20 (62,356 MW) — 3 hours.
**Solar cliff**: -28,000 MW from HE17 (29,135 MW solar) to HE20 (107 MW solar).
**NL at HE20**: 62,356 MW — highest net load of the day.
**Wind recovery**: ERCOT total wind rises from 2,936 MW (HE17) to 5,457 MW (HE20), but this offsets only ~2,500 MW of the 28,000 MW solar loss.
**Cap-out**: ~19,746-19,971 MW thermal unavailable during this window.

Stage 0 calibration reference (from May-Sep 2026 series):
- Non-binding floor: +14,861 MW NL ramp
- Binding floor: +17,935 MW NL ramp

Oct 8 HE17→HE20 ramp of +21,515 MW exceeds both reference thresholds, placing this in an elevated binding probability regime. Note: these thresholds are rough calibration anchors derived from the Stage 0 historical series — they are not hard physical limits.

**Primary mechanism**: As solar sets and load remains high (73,195 MW at HE18), the ERCOT South zone must import power from surrounding zones to serve Houston/South demand. Transmission import capacity into Houston is the binding constraint. Wind recovery in West/South partially helps but not sufficient to offset the solar cliff.

**GKS MCC direction (HE19-21)**: Positive or near-neutral. Houston/South import premium during evening ramp. GK, as a delivery node in the South, may benefit from this premium.
