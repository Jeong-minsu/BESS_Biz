## GKS BESS Implication (HB_NORTH / North settlement node)

GKS is a North-zone settlement node. Under Stage 0 heuristic PTDF assumptions:

- When HOUSTON_IMPORT binds, North zone nodes typically receive a positive MCC (Houston premium flows partially to North). GKS E[MCC] = +$6 to +$12 (unconditional); +$18 to +$45 conditional on binding.
- DA vs RT spread: If HOUSTON_IMPORT binds in RTM but was not cleared in DAM, DA prices will be below RT at GKS. This is a DART short signal for HE19-22 at GKS.
- BESS charging opportunity: Evening ramp (HE18-19 transition) as solar ramps off and NL ramps up presents an arbitrage window. Low DA prices at HE10 (NL = 9,783 MW, solar max) and high prices at HE21 (NL = 46,521 MW).

**Midday window (HE09-17) — MEDIUM HOUSTON_SOUTH_MIDDAY_LOCAL**:
- Cap-out binding suppresses West zone but can elevate Houston and partially North. GKS E[MCC] = +$3 to +$6 midday.
- June 21 = summer solstice. Solar generation is at annual peak (28,824 MW Yes Energy at HE15). Net load trough of 9,783 MW at HE10 — expect near-zero or negative DA prices in some hours.
- BESS charging at HE09-11 (cheap solar period) followed by discharge in evening ramp is the highest-value stack.

**Overnight window (HE22-24) — MEDIUM WEST_TO_NORTH**:
- When WEST_TO_NORTH binds, North zone receives West surplus via the constrained path — typically North prices fall (or become negative in extreme cases). GKS MCC = -$2 to -$5 in this window.
- If GKS BESS has remaining capacity from the evening session, avoid further discharge in HE22-24; overnight negative MCC compresses realized price.

**Net GKS day view**: The day is asymmetric. Afternoon/evening (HE13-22) is significantly positive MCC expected; overnight/early morning (HE22-07) is negative MCC expected. Optimal BESS cycle: charge deep in the midday solar trough (HE09-12), discharge in the evening ramp (HE18-22).
