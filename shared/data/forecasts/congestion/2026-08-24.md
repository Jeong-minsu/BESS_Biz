3. Duck curve deeper than forecast: Clear-sky actuals could push NL trough below Aug 5 record (25,190 MW). E[GKS MCC] would extend toward −$5.0 to −$6.5/MWh.

**Downside (binding prob lower than forecast):**

4. GR_WEST overnight bust lower: If GR_WEST is 1,000 MW below forecast, SCI falls to 0.419–0.465 → still above MEDIUM threshold but materially lower binding risk. WEST_TO_NORTH overnight probability falls to LOW 5–12%.
