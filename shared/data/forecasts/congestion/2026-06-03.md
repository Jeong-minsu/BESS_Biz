SOUTH_TO_HOUSTON / HOUSTON_IMPORT P(binding) heuristic: **22-30%** (MEDIUM to low-end MEDIUM-HIGH). Window shifted per 2026-06-01 self-review methodology: anchor on solar cliff endpoint, so HE20-22 is primary window (not HE17-20).

3. **Overnight wind plateau (HE22-24)**: AG2 ERCOT Aggregate 10,687-10,778 MW through HE22-23. AG2 West 5,534-5,550 MW HE22-24. Enverus wind 11,445-11,775 MW overnight. ## Risks

### Risk 1: West Wind Underperformance — SCI Upgrade
Trigger: If AG2 West realized < 1,500 MW at HE10-11 (vs 1,910-1,931 MW forecast), the SCI crosses 0.80+ and approaches the 0.85 LOW-MEDIUM boundary. Probability of this scenario: ~20%.

### Risk 2: Evening NL Above YE Forecast — HOUSTON_IMPORT Escalation
Trigger: If realized NL at HE21 exceeds 58,000 MW (vs 56,867 MW forecast) due to load upside + RT wind below AG2 5,541 MW, and cap-out declines faster (< 10,500 MW), HOUSTON_IMPORT P(binding) escalates from 22-30% to 30-42%. GKS discharge HE20-22 becomes a high-conviction window; DART long-RT HOUSTON signal strengthens.
Probability of this scenario: ~18%.

### Risk 3: Solar Underperformance — Midday Signal Fades
Trigger: If realized solar is 15-20% below Enverus STPPF (e.g., 22,000-23,000 MW peak vs 27,002 MW), midday SCI falls below 0.70 and WEST_TO_NORTH risk is minimal. This would reduce the West basis compression and make the GKS charge window less advantageous. RT West prices at HE10-12 stay moderate rather than suppressed.
Probability of this scenario: ~25% (solar forecast uncertainty is high for 48h+ horizon).

### Risk 4: Cap-Out Cascade at HE18-22
Trigger: Cap-out has been declining through the day (13,970 MW HE9-12 → 10,892 MW HE24). If a forced thermal outage occurs between HE15-18, cap-out could reach < 10,000 MW by HE19-20 simultaneously with the 56,867 MW NL peak. This would materially increase ORDC scarcity probability and push HOUSTON_IMPORT from MEDIUM to MEDIUM-HIGH or HIGH. Total evening energy revenue opportunity for GKS could be $3,000-$5,000+.
Probability of this scenario: ~12%.
