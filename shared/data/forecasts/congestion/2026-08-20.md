This Finding 23 signature (GR_WEST Extreme Daytime Drought) means West Texas is exporting almost nothing during midday — which somewhat offsets duck curve congestion risk (less WEST_TO_NORTH pressure), but increases SOUTH_HOUSTON import pressure during evening ramp.
