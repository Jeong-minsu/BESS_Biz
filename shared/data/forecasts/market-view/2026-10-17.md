## Key Drivers

- **Duck curve severity**: 30+ GW solar coinciding with a multi-GW wind trough at sunset is textbook ERCOT fall pattern but extreme in magnitude. Net load goes from ~2,565 MW (HE11) to ~43,517 MW (HE20) — a 41 GW swing over nine hours. Gas thermal units must accommodate nearly the entire swing.
- **Mild weather suppresses load upside**: DFW temperatures 48-65F on 10/17 per AG2 weather (high only 65F, below normal 77F). Zero cooling or heating load extremes. This is a favorable condition for supply adequacy but keeps demand moderate, limiting how high peak prices can go — consistent with Tyba DA HE19 at $82/MWh rather than extreme scarcity levels.
- **Wind diurnal: overnight flush, evening scarcity**: AG2 windcast shows ~16.7 GW at midnight declining to 2.7 GW by 7 PM. This pattern is a structural ERCOT fall characteristic — south-to-north wind flows at night, coastal and West Texas wind dissipates mid-afternoon. The daytime wind weakness amplifies the solar-dependent net load curve.
- **REGDN premium at HE17-18**: As solar ramps down, gas units increasing output need downward regulation capacity — hence the $4.65-$6.79/MWh REGDN premium. BESS positioned to provide REGDN during this window captures value without energy dispatch.
