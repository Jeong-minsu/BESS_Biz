## Key Drivers

1. **Holiday-After Saturday calendar effect**: Independence Day+1 Saturday suppresses commercial and industrial load by an estimated 3–5% vs a comparable weekday, pulling the morning trough to only 53 GW (vs ~60+ GW on a weekday at similar temps). Weather-driven residential AC dominates the peak; the shape is more symmetric than a workday.

2. **Duck curve depth**: Solar peaks at ~29.6–30.1 GW midday while load is already high (~71–80 GW), pushing net load to a 28 GW trough at HE10 — the deepest duck curve this week per data. The resulting afternoon ramp (28 GW net-load to 65+ GW over 11 hours) requires ~37 GW of thermal + battery dispatch to come online. This is the structural driver of the HE21 scarcity pricing.

3. **GR_WEST daytime wind collapse**: West Texas wind drops to 1.7–2.0 GW from HE13–15 (vs 12 GW overnight). This is the most consequential renewable gap during the critical load-rising afternoon hours. Any underperformance vs forecast here accelerates the duck curve ramp.

4. **5.7 GW thermal outage background**: ~5.7 GW of capacity offline throughout the day. On a Saturday with moderate (not extreme) load, reserves remain adequate during most hours but narrow significantly at HE21 when net load peaks near 65–68 GW. Additional unplanned outages during this extended holiday weekend represent the primary upside price tail risk.

## Risks / Watch

- **Upside price risk (HE20–22)**: If actual wind comes in near Enverus (8.5 GW avg) rather than Yes Energy (10.1 GW), evening net load peak rises by ~2 GW toward 67–68 GW, potentially driving RT prices well above the Smartbidder $28.34 RT forecast for HE21. ORDC activation probability elevated.

- **Downside price risk (midday)**: Enverus solar at 13.1 GW avg (vs YE 12.2 GW) could push HE10–11 DA prices below $16. On a Saturday with suppressed industrial load, negative prices during HE9–11 are plausible if solar outperforms and committed thermal is slow to ramp down.

- **Overnight RT tail (HE1–5)**: Smartbidder P(DA<RT) = 68–70% overnight suggests nontrivial probability of RT spikes. Source could be wind ramp-down risk or DA clearing underpricing the tight overnight supply stack.

- **Unplanned outages**: Holiday weekend maintenance windows can produce unannounced capacity returns or extensions. Monitor ERCOT Outage Scheduler and Hotline for any 1–2 GW additions to the 5.7 GW base outage level.

- **Wind forecast convergence**: The 2.7 GW spread between AG2 and Enverus on wind is unusually wide for a D+1 forecast. Neither source has clear systematic advantage at this lead time; the truth likely lies between them. Watch GR_WEST actuals from HE15 onward as the leading indicator for evening price direction.
