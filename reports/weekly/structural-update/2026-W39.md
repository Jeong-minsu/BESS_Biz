## 1. Regulatory / Policy Updates ★★★

### 1-A. GOVERNOR ABBOTT SEP 21: TCEQ ENVIRONMENTAL PERMITTING FREEZE — WHOLE-OF-GOVERNMENT PAUSE ★★★★

**[출처: Texas Tribune Sep 21, 2026; CNBC Sep 21, 2026; Foley & Lardner Sep 2026; DLA Piper Sep 2026; Sidley Sep 23, 2026; Troutman Sep 2026]**

On **September 21, 2026** — the first day of W39 — Governor Greg Abbott issued a directive to the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) to immediately halt **all environmental permit approvals for data center projects** pending statewide audit results. This completes a multi-agency whole-of-government freeze initiated over the prior six weeks:

| Date | Action | Agency Affected |
|---|---|---|
| Jun 10, 2026 | Directive: data centers must fund own electric infrastructure costs | PUCT / ERCOT |
| Aug 3, 2026 | Directive: audit all data centers in ERCOT interconnection queue | PUCT / ERCOT |
| Sep 14, 2026 | Directive: partner with TWDB to audit water consumption and efficiency | TWDB |
| **Sep 21, 2026** | **Directive: halt ALL TCEQ environmental permits for data centers** | **TCEQ** |

The Sep 21 TCEQ directive represents the most sweeping escalation to date. Key details:

- **Scope**: Applies to **all** Texas data center projects, not just those in the ERCOT interconnection queue — expanding coverage from ~700 Batch Zero entities to any data center seeking new environmental permits in Texas.
- **TCEQ deadline**: Submit formal progress and compliance report to the Governor's office by **October 19, 2026**.
- **Interconnection pause**: Still in effect (since Aug 3). No new large load interconnection approvals are proceeding while Batch Zero audit runs.
- **Effective**: All three major bottlenecks — grid interconnection, water permits, environmental permits — are now frozen simultaneously.

**BESS / Market Impact**:
- The three-layer freeze substantially reduces near-term new large-load entry risk. Any new data center load must navigate all three freezes before receiving service, with December 10 ERCOT audit report as the earliest credible milestone for unlocking the process.
- Near-term demand growth is constrained to facilities already in-service or approved before Aug 3. The data center baseload floor (~7.6 GW) is real; incremental additions are blocked for Q4 2026.
- Long-term demand uncertainty increases significantly. If audit results (December 2026) show widespread ghost demand, ERCOT's 111 GW 2032 peak forecast would require downward revision.

### 1-B. 16 TAC §25.194 ADOPTED — EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 8, 2026 ★★★

**[출처: DLA Piper March 2026 + Sep 2026; National Law Review Sep 2026; Hyperscape Digital Sep 2026; White & Case Sep 2026 (via PUCT Project 58481)]**

The PUCT filed the **Order Adopting 16 TAC §25.194** on **September 18, 2026** (PUCT Project 58481). Rule effective date: **October 8, 2026** (Texas Register publication acknowledgement).

Key requirements:
- **Financial security**: $50,000 per MW of requested capacity required **prior to interconnection studies** for any new load ≥ 75 MW.
- **Implementing statute**: PURA §37.0561, as enacted by Senate Bill 6 (signed Jun 20, 2025).
- **Applicability**: All new and expanding large electric loads of 75 MW or greater at a single site in the ERCOT region.
- **Scope**: Standardizes interconnection requirements, capital contributions, and financial security obligations.

**Impact**:
- Creates a meaningful financial filter on new large-load interconnection applications. A 500 MW data center must post $25 million before studies begin.
- Combined with the Aug 3 / Sep 21 freezes, 16 TAC §25.194 is the permanent legal framework that will govern all large-load interconnections post-audit.
- Reduces speculative queue entries — projects without committed capital will not absorb study slots.
