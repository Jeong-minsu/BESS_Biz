### Texas SB 6 / PUCT Project 58481 — Large Load Interconnection Standards

**Status: Active — no final rule in W29.** [WebSearch — PUCT 58481 filings; Greenberg Traurig; Foley & Lardner]

SB 6 (enacted June 2025) requires PUCT to establish formal interconnection standards for loads above 75 MW at a single site. Key features of the March 2026 Proposal for Publication still under deliberation:
- $50,000/MW non-refundable interconnection fee (significantly higher than prior requirements)
- Financial security deposit on a $/MW basis
- Broadened site control requirements
- Two new programs: voluntary demand response + mandatory curtailment during peak events
- Transmission cost methodology review (four coincident peak method under examination; must be completed by end of 2026)

A finalized Project 58481 rule — expected later in 2026 — will set the definitive economics for all future large load interconnection in ERCOT and determine the financial viability of Batch Zero projects. No final rule issued in W29.
