## 1. Regulatory / Policy Updates

### 1-A. BATCH ZERO — BOTH RFI DEADLINES NOW PASSED; GHOST-DEMAND QUANTIFICATION PENDING

**[Sources: ERCOT.com Batch Zero Update Sep 11; Baker Botts Aug 2026; BNEF via Utility Dive "Texas data center pause puts 20% of US pipeline at risk"; Power Mag "Texas Audit Could Delay 49.8 GW"]**

As of W40 close (Oct 4), both Batch Zero Verification RFI deadlines have passed:

| Deadline | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Primary verification RFI deadline | Sep 23, 2026 | PASSED |
| Extension deadline | Sep 30, 2026 | PASSED |
| Community Impact RFI deadline | Oct 12, 2026 | UPCOMING |
| ERCOT audit completion (PUCT report) | Dec 10, 2026 | TARGET |

The response rate to the Sep 23/30 deadlines — the first quantitative signal of how many of the 231 large-load Batch Zero projects have viable backing — has not yet been published to ERCOT's dashboard as of W40 close. This is the single most important pending data point for the market's long-term demand outlook.

**BNEF warning (confirmed W40)**: BloombergNEF estimates Governor Abbott's interconnection freeze could delay **49.8 GW** of new data center electricity demand — approximately 20% of the U.S. development pipeline. BNEF's financial impact estimate: **$8 billion cumulative by Q1 2027** (60% AI-compute mix scenario), rising to **$15 billion** in a full-delay 100% AI-compute scenario. The Sep 21 TCEQ permit freeze "pushed that timeline into genuine uncertainty" past December 2026, per BNEF.

**Structure implication**: The W40 Batch Zero RFI response collection is complete. The December 10, 2026 PUCT Batch Zero Eligibility Verification Report is the single most important structural market event of Q4 2026. Market participants should model both scenarios.

### 1-B. 16 TAC §25.194 — EFFECTIVE OCT 8 (WEEK AFTER W40)

**[Sources: DLA Piper Sep 2026; National Law Review Sep 2026; PUCT Project 58481]**

The PUCT Order Adopting 16 TAC §25.194 (financial security for new large loads ≥ 75 MW) takes effect **October 8, 2026** — three business days after W40 ends. Key requirements:
- $50,000/MW financial security required **prior to interconnection studies** for any new load ≥ 75 MW
- A 500 MW data center must post $25 million before studies begin
- Applies to all new and expanding large loads in ERCOT under PURA §37.0561

**Market impact**: The combination of the Batch Zero audit (interconnection study freeze) + 16 TAC §25.194 (financial filter) creates a two-layer barrier on new speculative large-load entry. Near-term incremental large load additions are effectively blocked through at least Q1 2027. This is structurally favorable for BESS revenue given the existing 7.6 GW data center baseload floor creating sustained elevated overnight load.

### 1-C. NPRR1330 — CONFIRMED ADOPTED (EFFECTIVE JULY 16, 2026)

**[Source: ERCOT NPRR1330 issue page; W40 PUCT Oct 1 verification]**

NPRR1330 (Mitigated Offer Caps for RMR Units) was approved by the PUCT on **July 9, 2026**, with an effective date of **July 16, 2026**. The W39 report's designation of this item as "unconfirmed" was in error — the rule has been in effect for over 11 weeks as of W40.

**Operational implication for BESS**: RMR unit offer caps are now in place, providing a ceiling on thermal dispatch costs during scarcity periods. This marginally reduces the probability of extreme scarcity pricing events but does not structurally alter the BESS evening arbitrage opportunity.
