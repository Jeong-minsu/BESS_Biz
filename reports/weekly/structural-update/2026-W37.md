## 1. Market Rule Changes (NPRR / MOTION)

### 1-A. NPRR1330 — Mitigated Offer Caps for RMR Units ★ (New Status)

**[출처: ERCOT.com/mktrules/issues/NPRR1330; TAC Report May 2026; PUCT Report July 9, 2026]**

NPRR1330 addresses mitigated offer caps for Reliability Must-Run (RMR) units — a significant market design issue given the ongoing thermal forced outages and the summer 2026 heat wave performance. The revision request completed the following milestones prior to W37:

- **April 21, 2026**: Initial proposal filed
- **TAC & Board**: Completed stakeholder ballot, TAC, Board review cycles (first half 2026)
- **July 9, 2026**: PUCT Report filed — the most recent document in the public record as of W37

**W37 Status**: NPRR1330 is now at the PUCT action stage. The PUCT September 11, 2026 open meeting (confirmed on the ERCOT calendar) is the next candidate for PUCT action. Specific disposition — adoption, modification, or remand — is not confirmed from W37 web sources. If adopted, NPRR1330 would cap the offer prices that RMR-designated thermal units can submit in the DAM, directly affecting tight-reserve pricing during high cap-out periods.

**BESS Impact**: If NPRR1330 caps RMR offer prices, it reduces the scarcity price premium that thermal units commanded during HE18-21 peak hours in August 2026. This marginally dampens RT spike magnitude — a slight headwind to BESS RT arbitrage revenue in high-outage windows.

## 3. Generation Mix Changes

### 3-A. ERCOT BESS Fleet — ~14 GW and Growing ★

**[출처: KilowattLogic 2026; B2U GlobeNewswire Sep 9, 2026; bxenergysystems.com 2026; ERCOT Understanding BESS report Feb 2026; IndexBox Sep 2026]**

The ERCOT BESS fleet reached approximately **14 GW** of installed capacity in 2026, nearly doubling the 7.8 GW present at the start of 2025. Texas represents **53% of the 24 GW of utility-scale BESS** expected to be added to the US grid in 2026.

**W37 New COD — B2U Bexar Martinez BESS (Sep 9, 2026)**:
- Location: Near San Antonio; CPS Energy service territory
- Capacity: **28 MWh** (repurposed EV batteries — B2U's patented second-life BESS cabinet technology)
- Status: Commercial operation, NERC registration complete, ERCOT grid services active
- This brings B2U's Texas total to **50 MWh** of repurposed storage (two sites operational)
- Qualifies for **domestic content ITC bonus** — consistent with OBBBA Section 48E preservation
- Financed via B2U Infrastructure Partners; third-party financing confirmed
- Significance for fleet diversity: Demonstrates second-life EV battery commercial viability at ERCOT scale; relatively small capacity but notable for supply chain independence from FEOC-constrained Chinese-origin modules

**Near-term pipeline headwind**: FEOC restrictions continue to constrain Chinese-origin battery module procurement, moderating pace from 2025's ~40% YoY growth rate. OBBBA BESS ITC remains fully intact through 2033, but supply chain friction could keep actual COD pace below permitted capacity.

### 3-B. Solar Fleet — Seasonal Decline; Annual Milestone Confirmed ★

**[출처: EIA TodayInEnergy 2026; Yahoo Finance/Texas grid article 2026]**

- **2025 milestone confirmed**: Solar generated more electricity than coal in ERCOT for the first time on an **annual basis** in 2025 (solar 14% vs coal 13% of ERCOT generation).
- **2026 EIA forecast**: Solar ~12% of ERCOT electricity; coal ~13%. The 2025 crossover may not sustain into 2026 on an annual basis as the coal fleet dispatches more during the Sep–Dec shoulder season, but solar's structural growth trajectory is intact.
- **W37 seasonal context**: Solar peaked at 33.5 GW (Aug 31, 2026) in the heat wave final days. September brings the seasonal decline: peak time shifting from HE12 to HE13-14, daily peak ~25-30 GW vs August's 30-33 GW. Duck curve depth moderating.
- Net-load trough recovery: From August's historic 19,918 MW (Aug 30, Yes Energy) to ~35-40 GW range in mid-September — eliminates deep-negative-price midday window; compresses BESS charging arbitrage value.

### 3-C. Wind Fleet — Post-Anticyclone Recovery

**[출처: W36 structural update; daily briefing context Sep 5-6]**

The anticyclonic blocking ridge that suppressed GR_WEST wind to its series low (443 MW, Sep 3) is dissipating. September historically returns to stronger West Texas wind patterns. No W37-specific GR_WEST commissioning or curtailment event identified. Seasonal wind ramp-up through October is the expected pattern.

### 3-D. Thermal Fleet — Cap-Out Recovery Continuing, New Gas Units
