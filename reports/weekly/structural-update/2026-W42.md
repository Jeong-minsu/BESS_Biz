### 1-F. NPRR1328 GENERATION FIRMING PROGRAM — PENDING PUCT ★

**[Sources: [Modo Energy NPRR1328 analysis](https://modoenergy.com/research/ercot-generation-firming-nprr1328-solar-wind-penalty-cost); ERCOT Sep 14-15 Board Agenda; NPRR1328 issue page]**

NPRR1328 (Generation Firming Program, HB 1500 implementation) was approved by the ERCOT Board September 15, 2026, and is now pending PUCT adoption. Key mechanics:
- Applies to generators with SGIA executed on or after January 1, 2027
- Must perform at or above Seasonal Average Generation Capability during hours when reserves fall below 3,000 MW for 15+ minutes
- Compliance: own performance or Firming Transfer with another eligible resource
- First compliance season: March 1, 2028; statutory deadline: December 1, 2027

**BESS exemption posture**: BESS units are self-firming by definition (discharge when scarcity is signaled) and are expected to qualify as eligible resources for Firming Transfers. This may create a new revenue stream for BESS post-2028 as intermittent generators seek firming partners. However, per one analysis, ERCOT reserves have not fallen below 3,000 MW since September 2023 — meaning firming penalty exposure may be rare under current market conditions.

**PUCT approval timing**: Oct 15 meeting was the primary candidate; Nov 5 meeting is the backup. W43 check required.

- Require load-serving entities to purchase performance credits from generation resources

- Originally tied to RTC+B for December 31, 2026 implementation

Oct 15 PUCT meeting was the primary opportunity for a PCM scheduling decision or vote. Outcome unconfirmed. If PCM slips to 2027 legislative session, BESS units will not see this structural revenue stream before H2 2027 at the earliest.

Under current market conditions (no reserve shortfall since September 2023), the credit value would be modest — but any tightening of reserve margins post-winter or in summer 2027 would increase credit value significantly.
