### 1-B. 16 TAC §25.194 — IN EFFECT AS OF OCT 8

**[Sources: [DLA Piper Sep 2026](https://www.dlapiper.com/); [National Law Review Sep 2026](https://www.natlawreview.com/); PUCT Project 58481]**

The $50,000/MW financial security requirement for new loads ≥ 75 MW became effective October 8 (first week of W41). Combined with the ongoing Batch Zero interconnection freeze, this creates a two-layer entry barrier:

- **Layer 1** (structural): Batch Zero audit / interconnection study freeze — no study results until at least post-April 9, 2027.
- **Layer 2** (financial): 16 TAC §25.194 — any new or expanding large load ≥ 75 MW must post $50K/MW before studies begin. A 500 MW data center faces a $25 million upfront financial commitment before a single study is run.

**BESS implication**: Near-term speculative large-load additions are effectively blocked through at least Q1 2027. The existing ~7.6 GW data center baseload floor (confirmed by W40 overnight load ~46.6 GW vs ~35-38 GW pre-AI era) is intact, providing sustained elevated overnight net load that supports BESS morning charge economics.

### 1-C. NPRR1328 — GENERATION FIRMING PROGRAM; ERCOT BOARD APPROVED SEP 15; AWAITING PUCT

**[Sources: [Modo Energy NPRR1328 analysis](https://modoenergy.com/research/ercot-generation-firming-nprr1328-solar-wind-penalty-cost); [ERCOT Sep 14-15 Board Agenda](https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/0-September-14-15-2026-Board-of-Directors-Agenda.pdf)]**

The ERCOT Board of Directors approved NPRR1328 (Generation Firming Program, established under HB 1500) on September 15, 2026. The rule is now pending PUCT approval before implementation.

**Mechanics**: New generators must perform — or secure trades — at their historical seasonal capability during hours when reserves fall below 3,000 MW for at least 15 minutes within an ERCOT-defined baseline period. First compliance season: March 1, 2028. Statutory deadline: December 1, 2027.

**BESS implication**: BESS units already dispatchable during scarcity events are well-positioned relative to intermittent wind/solar generators that may face firming obligations. BESS firms itself by definition when it discharges during low-reserve windows. Watch for PUCT approval timing (possible Oct 15 or Nov 5 PUCT meeting agenda).

## 4. Market Structure Changes

### 4-A. AS PROCUREMENT — RESERVE CORRECTION FULL EFFECT; HIGHER FLOOR IN Q4

**[Source: [ERCOT Market Notice M-A081926-01](https://www.ercot.com/services/comm/mkt_notices/M-A081926-01); [ERCOT 2027 AS Methodologies Sep 2026 Board Agenda](https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/07/18-2027-ERCOT-Methodologies-for-Determining-Minimum-Ancillary-Service-Requirements.pdf)]**

Key AS procurement changes active in Q4 2026:

- **Non-Spin/ECRS correction**: ERCOT identified 3,700 MW/hour underproduction Jan-Jul 2026 due to modeling error. Correction in full effect since Sep 1, 2026. Combined Non-Spin + ECRS overnight minimum now = at least RRS + Reg-Up combined.
- **ECRS hold window (HE12-17)**: Structurally supported by deep duck curve (midday solar suppression + elevated cap-out). BESS ECRS hold during midday is a primary Q4 revenue layer.
- **RRS price trend**: Modo Energy reported RRS prices down ~50% over two years relative to DA energy as BESS fleet saturates the product. This compression is likely continuing with the Oct-Nov 14-unit commissioning wave.

### 4-B. PERFORMANCE CREDIT MECHANISM — PCM RULING APPROACHING

**[Sources: [Utility Dive PCM background](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/texas-regulators-slow-development-performance-credit-mechanism-PCM/709705/); [S&P Global PCM analysis](https://www.spglobal.com/commodityinsights/en/ci/research-analysis/texas-electric-regulators-turn-to-a-novel-solution-to-solve.html)]**

The PUCT PCM design process (E3 consulting firm engaged Feb 2026) is in a Q3/Q4 2026 ruling window. The mechanism would:
- Require load-serving entities to purchase performance credits from generation resources
- Credits priced based on performance during reliability-critical hours (reserve < 3,000 MW)
- Originally tied to RTC (Real-Time Co-optimization) Dec 31, 2026 implementation

BESS units that discharge reliably during scarcity events would earn performance credits, creating a new structural revenue stream. However, PCM implementation may slip to 2027 given Commissioner Cobos' noted concerns about stakeholder engagement. Watch Oct 15 and Nov 5 PUCT meetings for PCM votes or scheduling updates.

### 4-C. ORDC — NO CHANGES CONFIRMED W41

**[Source: W40 continuity; no new ERCOT NPRR filings identified]**

No ORDC curve modifications are confirmed for W41. The Operating Reserve Demand Curve continues at its current parameterization. Georgetown filing (per search results) criticizing E3's ORDC analysis in the PCM process suggests further academic/stakeholder debate is ongoing but no formal rule change is imminent.
