[Live fetch — Enverus blog; Training data on WESTEX/GR_WEST constraint mechanics]
The WESTEX generic transmission constraint (corresponding to GR_WEST in Yes Energy nomenclature) is the dominant structural price formation feature for West Texas generation. The constraint caps West Texas wind exports to the rest of ERCOT when wind generation approaches or exceeds the thermal capacity of the westbound transmission lines. Binding conditions reduce West Hub and North Hub prices while depressing WEST Hub prices — a congestion pattern that directly affects BESS assets located behind the constraint.

### Large Load Queue — 225+ GW and Growing

[Live fetch — Utility Dive; Latitude Media; ERCOT April 2026 Senate testimony]

The large load interconnection queue in ERCOT had reached over 225 GW as of the ERCOT Board meeting in December 2025. By early 2026, additional filings had pushed the total to approximately 233–238 GW, with 198 GW of new large load applications filed in Q1 2026 alone. More than 70% of queued large loads are data centers.

The Batch Zero framework (PGRR145, subject to June 1–2 Board vote) was designed to bring a subset of these applications through an accelerated, coordinated study process. If the framework performs as designed, it will produce confirmed interconnection agreements for the first batch of large loads by late 2026 or early 2027 — creating a more definitive long-run net load trajectory than the current queue-uncertainty scenario.

The queue-growth trajectory: ERCOT's large load queue has grown from 40–50 projects (system designed capacity) to 225+ GW in approximately three years. ERCOT contracted McKinsey to help develop a framework to address the queue. The queue cannot be fully monetized — ERCOT itself has stated that the quadrupling of demand by 2032 implied by some queue projections is likely too high and that actual COD rates will be materially lower. Nonetheless, even a 20–25% COD rate on the 225 GW queue represents 45–56 GW of new large load — transformative for the ERCOT supply-demand balance.
