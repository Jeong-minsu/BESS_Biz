## 1. Market Rule Changes (NPRR / MOTION)

### 1-A. PUCT Rulemaking 16 TAC §25.194 — Large Load Interconnection Standards (SB 6 Implementation) ★★

**[출처: DLA Piper March 2026; Greenberg Traurig March 2026; PUCT Case 58481; interchange.puc.texas.gov]**

The most significant near-term market rule development expected to conclude **in September 2026**. The PUCT published draft rule 16 TAC §25.194 in March 2026 to implement Texas Senate Bill 6 (signed June 20, 2025), which directs the Commission to establish formal interconnection standards for new loads of 75 MW or greater in ERCOT.

Key provisions of the draft rule:
- **Financial security requirement**: $50,000/MW posted at application; approximately 20% refundable upon achieving commercial operation
- **Disclosure obligations**: corporate affiliates, end-use characterization, behind-the-meter generation details, government assistance / tax incentives received
- **Backup generation**: must disclose backup generation capacity and fuel type
- **Site control**: documented site control required at application
- Comment period closed April 17, 2026. Commission expected to adopt the rule with or without modifications in **September 2026** (Project No. 58481).

**BESS/Market Structural Impact**: Once adopted, 16 TAC §25.194 creates the first formal large-load financial barrier in ERCOT history. The $50K/MW requirement would impose ~$3.75B in financial security obligations on a 75 GW pipeline (even if most projects never reach commercial operation). This is expected to materially prune the 438,000 MW (89% data center) interconnection queue over the following 12-18 months. **[INFERRED - final adoption language not yet confirmed as of Sep 7]**

## 3. Generation Mix Changes

### 3-A. Solar Fleet — Seasonal Decline Underway

**[출처: daily reports 2026-08-31 through 2026-09-03 (Yes Energy BidClose PRODUCTION)]**

The heat wave period (Aug 20-30) established new 2026 solar fleet highs, but the W36 transition shows seasonal moderation:

| Date | Solar Peak (HE, MW) | Change vs Aug 30 Record (33,396 MW) |
|---|---|---|
| Aug 31 | HE12: 33,556 MW | +160 MW (1 more day of peak season) |
| Sep 1 | HE12: 30,796 MW | -2,600 MW |
| Sep 2 | HE13: 27,081 MW | -6,315 MW |
| Sep 3 | HE14: 24,855 MW | -8,541 MW |
| Sep 4 | HE10: 26,082 MW (Enverus) | -7,314 MW |

The solar peak time shifting from HE12 (Aug) to HE13-14 (Sep) confirms the seasonal sun-angle correction. The ERCOT solar fleet remains in the 25-34 GW daily peak band through September, still structurally dominant in the noon hours. The duck curve is less extreme than August but remains the defining market structure.

**Net-load trough recovery** (HE10): From Aug 30's historic 19,918 MW (Yes Energy) to 38,775 MW by Sep 3 — a 18,857 MW trough rebound in 4 days. This eliminates the deep-negative-price regime and shifts midday DA prices from ($-15 to $-5) back to ($35-50/MWh), compressing BESS charging value in the solar belly.

### 3-B. Wind Fleet — Anticyclone Pattern Persists Through W36

**[출처: daily reports 2026-08-31 through 2026-09-03 (Yes Energy BidClose PRODUCTION)]**

The West Texas anticyclonic blocking ridge — which drove the heat wave — partially retreated after Aug 30 but did not fully clear during W36:

| Date | GR_WEST HE15 Floor (MW) | ERCOT Wind 24h Avg (MW) | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aug 30 | 6,209 | 16,519 | Ridge retreating |
| Aug 31 | 4,738 | 15,559 | Partial re-suppression |
| Sep 1 | 2,988 | 12,359 | Anticyclone re-asserting |
| Sep 2 | 1,108 | 11,170 | Strongest re-suppression |
| Sep 3 | 443 | 12,598 | GR_WEST floor hits heat-wave series low |
| Sep 4 | 5,517 (ERCOT wind floor, Enverus) | 13,943 (Enverus) | Improvement; GR_WEST not reported |

**Sep 3 GR_WEST floor of 443 MW is the lowest West Texas wind reading of the entire Aug 20 – Sep 4 event series.** This is a structurally important observation: even as the heat wave's gross load began moderating, West Texas wind suppression intensified — indicating the anticyclone pattern is not simply a function of heat wave intensity but a semi-independent synoptic blocking feature.

**GR_COASTAL sea breeze** (consistently 3.0-3.7 GW at HE18-19) provided the primary wind buffer during the evening solar cliff transition throughout W36. This is structurally the most reliable evening-hour generation source when GR_WEST is suppressed.

### 3-C. BESS Fleet — Stable; OBBBA Tax Credit Clarity Positive

**[출처: Cleanview.co August 2026; WebSearch OBBBA July 2025; daily reports]**

- **Fleet size**: ~17.0 GW / 243 projects (unchanged from W35; no new COD announcements confirmed in W36 public sources)
- **OBBBA (One Big Beautiful Bill Act, signed July 4, 2025) — key W36 structural context**:
  - BESS qualifies for full **Section 48E ITC through 2033**, with only gradual phase-down starting 2034
  - Solar and wind must have begun construction by **July 4, 2026** to retain full ITC/PTC (retroactive protection for pre-deadline projects)
  - **Implication**: The July 4, 2026 solar/wind construction deadline has now passed. New solar and wind projects beginning construction after this date face accelerated credit phase-outs. BESS projects started through 2033 retain full ITC. This structurally favors standalone BESS over new solar+BESS hybrid projects begun after the deadline, and likely accelerates BESS-only pipeline execution relative to solar.
- **FEOC (Foreign Entity of Concern)** supply chain restrictions continue to constrain Chinese-origin battery module procurement, moderating BESS growth rate from 2025's 40% YoY pace.

### 3-D. Thermal Fleet — Extended Outage Cycle Normalizing Slowly

**[출처: daily reports 2026-08-31 through 2026-09-03]**

### 5-B. OBBBA — BESS ITC Intact; Solar/Wind July 4, 2026 Construction Deadline Now Past ★

**[출처: Winston & Strawn; Latham & Watkins; Chapman & Cutler; Plante Moran — OBBBA tax credit analysis series]**

The July 4, 2026 construction commencement deadline for solar and wind (under OBBBA, signed July 4, 2025) has now passed. Key structural implications for ERCOT generation mix:

- **BESS**: Section 48E ITC at full credit rate through **December 31, 2033**. Projects begun through 2033 are fully protected. Phase-down begins 2034.
- **Solar (new projects beginning construction after July 4, 2026)**: Must place in service by December 31, 2027 for full credit; projects not meeting this timeline face reduced or no credit.
- **Wind (new projects beginning construction after July 4, 2026)**: Same constraint as solar.
- **Pre-deadline solar/wind (construction begun by July 4, 2026)**: Retain full ITC/PTC even if placed in service after 2027.

**W36 Implication**: Developers who did not have construction activities underway by July 4 now face materially less attractive economics for new solar/wind projects. This could shift the ERCOT resource pipeline toward BESS-only projects and combinations with pre-approved solar acreage. Near-term solar capacity growth (2026-2027 in-service) continues from the pre-deadline pipeline; structural slow-down may materialize in 2028-2029 vintage projects.
