What is DART Virtual Revenue? DART virtual = financial energy-only offers/bids settled against the DA-vs-RT price spread. Virtual Short (offer): (DA_LMP − RT_LMP) × Offer_MW  —  Virtual Long (bid): (RT_LMP − DA_LMP) × Bid_MW. This revenue stream is separate and additive to physical energy + AS revenue (see Energy/AS dashboard). Sign convention: spread = DA − RT; positive spread ⇒ DA expensive ⇒ virtual short is profitable.
