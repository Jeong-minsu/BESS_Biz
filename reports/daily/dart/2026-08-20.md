1. **DEC cond1 FAIL is a real risk**: The NL ramp of 18,327 MW is 1,673 MW below the historically validated DEC signal threshold. 2. **SOUTH_HOUSTON non-binding (65-78% probability)**: Even within the MEDIUM 22-35% range, the base case is the constraint does NOT bind (P(non-bind) = 65-78%). If it does not bind, the LZ_HOUSTON basis advantage disappears, and the position reverts to a weak system-level DEC with insufficient cond1 support. Half-sizing (15 MW + 12 MW vs full 25 MW) is the primary risk mitigation.

3. **DA over-pricing the scarcity (INC risk embedded in DEC positions)**: If the DA market prices in the scarcity and SOUTH_HOUSTON binding more aggressively than RT actually delivers (e.g., DA $120, RT $90), the DEC position loses. This is a real risk given ERCOT's well-developed market participant scarcity awareness.

4. **No Smartbidder P(DA>RT) confirmation**: Without Smartbidder, the 57%/55% qualitative P(win) estimates are structurally-derived with ±10 ppt uncertainty band. The applied SB-DEGRADED confidence penalty (-3 ppt in P values) may be insufficient. Real P could be 45-67% range.

5. **Record load realization risk**: If actual load at HE20-21 exceeds the 85-92 GW forecast (additional demand not captured in bidclose), NL ramp could approach or exceed the cond1 threshold, strengthening the DEC case beyond current expectations. This is an upside risk (could have justified larger sizing).

6. **Wind forecast bust (GR_COASTAL HE18)**: GR_COASTAL builds to 3,555 MW at HE18 (atypical afternoon pattern per market briefing). If GR_COASTAL underperforms in RT, NL ramp increases further → scarcity strengthens → DEC wins more. If GR_COASTAL over-performs → RT less tight → DEC softer. This is a two-sided risk.

7. **Cap-out change risk**: The 6,965 MW cap-out during HE17-21 is based on BidClose vintage (2026-08-19). Any intraday thermal trip or return could materially shift the scarcity picture before the 10:00 CT DAM bid cutoff. Monitor ERCOT Outage Scheduler.
