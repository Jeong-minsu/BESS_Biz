## Why

### HE22-24 INC at HB_WEST — Primary Signal

**Structural mechanism**: Oct 15 exhibits extreme bimodal wind structure — GR_WEST near zero during solar peak (2,090 MW at HE14) then explosive rebuild to all-time record (18,299 MW at HE24). The 16,209 MW intraday swing is the largest in Stage 0 series history. This creates the following DART alpha opportunity:

- DA bidding closes at 10:00 CT (the bid cutoff). At that point, STWPF projects GR_WEST building to 18,299 MW by HE24, but with high uncertainty: no prior October analog at this magnitude exists (prior Stage 0 HE24 record was Oct 11 at 16,859 MW). DA market participants facing unprecedented forecast magnitudes tend to bid partial congestion into the DAM clearing — i.e., DA at HB_WEST is set with only partial discounting.

- In RT, if GR_WEST materializes at forecast levels (or stronger), WEST_TO_NORTH_345 and PANHANDLE_EXPORT binding is VERY HIGH (P 38-62% per hour). RT SCED enforces the constraint through real-time price signals, pushing HB_WEST RT LMP further negative than DA had priced. Result: DA_WEST > RT_WEST → spread positive → INC wins at HB_WEST.

- This is the OPPOSITE direction from Oct 11 (which used DEC at HB_WEST). The key distinction: Oct 11 had ALL-DAY sustained high GR_WEST — DA had all morning to observe and price in the full congestion. Oct 15's surge is concentrated in HE22-24 (late evening OFFPEAK), a regime DA is less likely to fully price by the 10:00 CT cutoff.
