## DA-RT Spread Strategy

**Energy: DA > RT signal is decisive for evening hours.**
- Overall: spread DA-RT = +$2.79 (24h mean) → DA overpriced on average; commit to DA
- HE21: DA $45.83 vs RT $28.34 forecast → DA-RT spread = +$17.49; P(DA>RT) = 70.4% → DA lock correct
- HE22: P(DA>RT) = 74.8% (highest of day) → DA lock correct
- Overnight HE01-05: RT > DA signal (P(DA<RT) = 68-70%) → not relevant for physical dispatch (we have no SoC overnight)
- Midday HE09-10: Smartbidder shows mixed signal (P(DA<RT) ~60%). Charging in DA at $13-14 node-adjusted is correct regardless — solar suppression price is a structural floor, not a RT spread play

**Non-spin HE20: key strategic call.**
Holding 100 MW of Non-spin at HE20 ($8.90/MW) versus discharging 100 MW of energy ($35.24/MWh) appears suboptimal at first glance, but is correct because:
1. Non-spin HE20 ($890) + Energy HE21 100 MW ($4,598) + Energy HE22 70 MW ($2,520) = $8,008
2. Energy HE20 100 MW ($3,524) + Energy HE21 70 MW ($3,208) = $6,732
3. Net advantage of holding Non-spin at HE20: +$1,276

The 2-hour duration constraint forces a capacity allocation decision between HE20 and HE21+22. The $17.49/MWh DA-RT spread at HE21 makes it far more valuable to discharge at HE21 at full 100 MW.

## Risks

1. **Non-spin dispatch at HE20 (RT callout probability ~10-15%)**: If ERCOT calls Non-spin at HE20, we deploy 100 MW and SoC drops from 200 to 82.4 MWh. HE21 discharge becomes 70 MW (not 100 MW). Revenue impact: lose $1,359 in energy ($45.98 × 30 MW); gain ~$35 × 100 MWh RT energy = $3,500 at RT. Net is neutral to slightly positive if RT is near $35+. This risk is acceptable and symmetric.

2. **Wind forecast bust (Enverus bear: 8.5 GW avg)**: If wind actualizes near Enverus estimate rather than AG2/Yes Energy, net load at HE21 rises 2+ GW to 67-68 GW. RT HE21 could spike well above $28.34 forecast, potentially above DA $45.83. We are locked in DA ($45.83); if RT spikes to $70+, we leave $2,400+ on the table. However P(DA>RT) = 70.4% at HE21 justifies DA commitment on an EV basis.

3. **GKS MCC deeper negative during charging (HE09-10)**: Congestion Stage 0 gives -$2.5 to -$3.5 range. If MCC actualizes at -$4.0 or lower (as seen on Jul 4), effective charge cost falls further — this is a positive surprise for our P&L (cheaper charge).

4. **HE20-22 AS prices underperform Smartbidder forecast**: Smartbidder returned after 43-cycle absence; calibration confidence is lower than steady-state. Non-spin $8.90 at HE20 and $12.48 at HE21 may not fully clear in DA. Even at 50% clearing, HE20 Non-spin contributes $445 — still worth offering versus the discharge trade-off analysis.

5. **HE22 DA price uncertainty**: Estimated at $36.00/MWh (not directly stated in market-view). If actual DA HE22 clears at $30, HE22 discharge revenue drops from $2,531 to $2,100 (-$431). Stack A still wins over all alternatives at $30 HE22.
