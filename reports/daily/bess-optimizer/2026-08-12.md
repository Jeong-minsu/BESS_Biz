## 08/10 Self-Review — Why $369.97 Happened and What Changes Today

**Root cause (3 compounding failures):**

1. **DA energy bid not matched to physical operation**: Bought 50 MWh DA at HE06-07 ($24/MWh) but the unit ran in NonSpin hold mode — zero physical charging occurred. The DA purchase settled as a pure cost (-$2,418) with no offsetting energy delivery. NonSpin and DA energy are mutually exclusive in physical execution; bidding both without mode-switching created a guaranteed loss.

2. **RT charging without DA hedge**: Charged 108 MWh in RT at HE10-11 ($18.70-$20.25/MWh) with no corresponding DA buy position. On 08/10, DA prices at HE10-11 were slightly lower ($17.31-$17.09) than RT — the correct hedge was a DA buy bid, not unscheduled RT charging that settled at the higher RT price.

3. **DA sell without physical follow-through**: Sold 30.1 MWh DA at HE12-13 ($18.12-$19.41) but delivered ~0 MWh physically. Paid RT settlement prices ($22.15-$23.69) for the undelivered position — selling low in DA and buying back high in RT on the same MW block.

**Corrected protocol for 08/12:**
- DA energy bids ONLY at hours with matching physical dispatch plan (HE10-12 charge, HE20-21 discharge)
- No DA energy bids at NS-hold hours (HE01-09, HE13-19)
- Bid DA buy at HE10-11 at MOST NEGATIVE feasible price (floor bid, not $0 default)
- ECRS at HE12 (remaining capacity after topoff charge)
- No competing NS during charge/discharge hours

**Revenue with correct strategy on 08/10** (counter-factual): Duck floor on 08/10 was NL 26,477 MW — positive prices ($17-18/MWh). Optimal 08/10 strategy (charge HE10-12 w/ DA buy, discharge HE19-21 w/ DA sell, NS otherwise) would have yielded approximately $7,000-$9,000. Realized: $369.97. Opportunity cost: ~$6,600-$8,600.
