The arbitrage spread on GKS-adjusted prices is $33-38/MWh vs the raw DA spread of $20.5/MWh — nearly double. This warrants concentrated 2-cycle charge/discharge rather than spreading across more hours.

The concentrated discharge (HE19 100MW + HE20 66MW) is positioned directly in this risk-upside window. No alternative arrangement captures this better within the 2-hour SOC budget.
