## Risks

1. **GR_WEST forecast bust (upside)**: If actual GR_WEST evening is > 18,000 MW (vs forecast 16,549-17,935 MW), the W25 haircut becomes even more important. HOUSTON_IMPORT probability would fall further (wind export from West relieves South-to-Houston pressure). This is the Jun 21 solstice analog.

2. **GR_WEST forecast bust (downside)**: If actual GR_WEST evening falls to < 14,000 MW, the haircut would not apply and HOUSTON_IMPORT probability would revert to base HIGH 32-47%. Unlikely given the sustained high-wind pattern across all hours in the YE forecast.

3. **NL ramp underestimate**: Solar forecast could be optimistic (+2-3 GW possible on Saturday with cloud cover). If solar drops by 2,000 MW, net load at HE21 rises by 2,000 MW and the NL ramp intensifies — HOUSTON_IMPORT probability could increase 5-8 ppt.

4. **Non-Spin DA signal**: Smartbidder Non-Spin DA is ABSENT this cycle. If DA Non-Spin clears above $4/MWh in HE20-22 (as it did in Jun 23 at $126-$375/MWh), the 1.8x right-tail rule would apply and HOUSTON_IMPORT P(binding) would escalate to HIGH 40%+. This is unverifiable before DAM clearing.

5. **Saturday demand uncertainty**: Saturday load can be 3-5% below weekday levels. If actual load is lower than forecast (81,846 MW), NL ramp compresses and HOUSTON_IMPORT probability falls 3-5 ppt. Conversely if weekend AC load is elevated (heat wave), load surprise could push probability toward weekday levels.

6. **WEST_TO_NORTH timing**: W25 plan corrects onset to HE24-04. If actual binding is centered HE01-03 (consistent with prior cycles showing 1-hour early calls), the corrected window captures the event. This is the first Saturday cycle where the W25 timing correction is applied.
