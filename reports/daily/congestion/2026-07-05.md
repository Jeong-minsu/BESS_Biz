## Risks

1. **Load overperformance (upside for HOUSTON_IMPORT)**: Jul 5 post-holiday Saturday may see higher commercial AC load than Jul 4. If load exceeds 83-84k MW at peak, NL ramp could increase to +18.5-19k MW range, pushing HOUSTON_IMPORT to MEDIUM 25-35%. The +2,500 MW load advantage vs Jul 4 is the primary bull case.

2. **Wind cross-source divergence (overnight uncertainty)**: YE total wind HE01-06 = 15,208-18,739 MW vs Enverus = 10,247-16,545 MW (gap of 2,193-3,464 MW). If wind actualizes at Enverus levels, overnight NL is tighter than YE suggests. P(HOUSTON_IMPORT overnight) or PANHANDLE probability could move slightly upward. At the same time, if Enverus wind is correct, WEST→NORTH flow may be weaker, reducing West discount overnight.

3. **NL trough cross-source divergence (midday uncertainty)**: YE NL trough = 28,131 MW vs Enverus = 31,986 MW (3,855 MW gap). This is the largest midday divergence in the July series. Actual trough could fall anywhere in this range. If trough lands near YE level (28k MW), E[MCC] HE09-15 may approach -$4.0 or lower; if near Enverus (32k MW), midday suppression remains mild.

4. **Solar HE18 divergence (ramp uncertainty)**: YE solar HE18 = 23,382 MW vs Enverus = 25,888 MW (+2,506 MW). If Enverus is correct, NL at HE18 is 51,011 MW (vs YE 48,288 MW), which provides a 2,723 MW higher ramp base — reducing the HE18→HE21 net ramp to +17,055 MW. This actually increases confidence in the ~17.2k MW average ramp call.

5. **L1 timing offset**: Prior series evidence (L1, confirmed 4 consecutive cycles) shows HOUSTON_IMPORT binding onset can begin HE17, not HE19. If HOUSTON_IMPORT activates, GKS MCC positive window may start 2 hours earlier than the HE19-22 base call. BESS DA position window: plan for HE17-22.

6. **Smartbidder calibration uncertainty**: First cycle with Smartbidder data in 43 cycles. The P(DA>RT) = 0.71-0.75 signal for HE19-22 is strong, but model drift after 6+ weeks without data publication cannot be ruled out. Use DART signal as supplementary validation only; do not override congestion heuristics based solely on this signal.

7. **PANHANDLE overnight near-monitor (not a trigger risk)**: GR_WEST overnight peak = 12,068 MW (HE02) is 2,932 MW below the 15k trigger — safely below threshold for this cycle. Wind would need to arrive 25% above STWPF to trigger PANHANDLE activation. Low probability, included for completeness.
