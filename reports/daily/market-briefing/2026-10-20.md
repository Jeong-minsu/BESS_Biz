## Key Drivers

1. **Weekday load step-up**: The ~5 GW increase in load average vs Sunday (51,457 vs 46,510 MW) reflects commercial/industrial/data center return. October weekday peak at HE17-18 (~60 GW) occurs while solar is still generating at 18-26 GW, partially masking the net load stress. The true crunch point is HE17-19 when solar starts rolling off against still-elevated load.

2. **High outage block (26.2 GW) compresses thermal margin**: With ~26.2 GW offline, ERCOT must dispatch the remaining dispatchable thermal fleet aggressively across the day. The outage peak at HE9-10 (26,495 MW) is during solar-covered hours, but outages remain elevated through HE17-21 when they matter most. Any single unit failing to return from maintenance on schedule extends into the critical ramp window and could push RT prices significantly above the Tyba P50 view.

3. **GR_WEST wind afternoon trough (4,674 MW at HE15-16)**: Wind contribution from the dominant western zone bottoms out at exactly the same time solar starts fading and load remains elevated. The compounding of GR_WEST wind weakness + solar decline + peak load in HE15-18 is the primary driver of the RT-over-DA pattern. Refer to congestion-analyst for GR_WEST transmission constraint binding probability and any north-south flow constraint that could emerge in this window.

4. **RT > DA structure in HE17-20**: Unlike the DA-dominated pricing on 10/18, today's Tyba P50 shows persistent RT premium in the evening. This reflects intra-hour dispatch tightness that clears in real-time above DA-cleared offers. For BESS, this shifts the optimal strategy toward reserving RT charge/discharge flexibility in HE17-21 rather than locking all capacity into DA energy.

5. **Enverus net load forecasts materially higher than Yes Energy**: Enverus 24h avg 31,733 MW vs Yes Energy 29,180 MW — a 2,553 MW gap. Enverus sees a shallower solar trough (18,884 MW floor vs 13,114 MW at HE11) and consistently higher net load through the day. If Enverus is correct, the midday price floor will be higher ($22-25 range) and evening ramp prices may not reach the Tyba P50 peak of $58.40 at HE19 — the market could be less extreme in both directions.
