## Key Drivers

1. **Saturday demand with elevated outages**: Load is 58.4 GW average (YE) — typical Saturday step-down from weekday highs. However, ~13,878 MW of thermal capacity is offline (mean), approximately +991 MW more than the prior briefing's Thursday estimate of 12,887 MW. Saturday DAM tends to have thinner bidding participation. The combination of elevated outages and thin Saturday DAM increases tail risk at HE20 if load comes in above consensus.

2. **Solar-exit ramp concentration at HE19-20**: Enverus solar collapses from 17,633 MW (HE19) to 794 MW (HE21) — a -16,839 MW drop in 2 hours. This is the single largest supply-side swing of the day. The convergence of solar collapse, moderate wind (GR_WEST ~4 GW at HE18), and plateau load (~66-68 GW) forces the gas fleet to ramp steeply. This 2-hour window drives DA price to $41-$45/MWh range and concentrates BESS discharge opportunity.

3. **Wind profile — moderate with overnight improvement**: GR_WEST is the key regional contributor. It is weak in the midday (1,827-2,500 MW at HE9-12 by YE) but improves overnight (7,225 MW at HE24). This overnight ramp lowers RT prices and, combined with overnight P(DA<RT) = 60-66%, reinforces the charge window for BESS in HE01-04. GR_COASTAL sea-breeze pattern provides a mild offset at HE18-19 (STWPF 1,653-2,372 MW), slightly dampening the early solar-exit ramp — but not enough to materially shift the HE19-20 price spike.

4. **Enverus solar upside risk to price**: Enverus solar mean is +1,577 MW (18.7%) above YE BidClose. If Enverus is correct and solar generation is higher during the day, midday net load will be lower than YE implies (~35,091 MW already is the YE minimum at HE11-13). This could push midday prices toward $24-26/MWh and widen the HE20 price spike by amplifying the solar-exit drop. Conversely, if YE BidClose (lower solar) is correct, midday net-load and prices are slightly higher, and the evening spike is marginally smaller. The uncertainty range on solar is the largest single modeling risk today.

5. **Saturday DAM liquidity thin — AS clearing risk**: With lower weekday demand, fewer resources participate in Saturday DAM. Non-Spin at $12.19/MWh (HE21) and RegUp at $2.85/MWh (HE21) are at levels where clearing is highly probable (HIGH), but thin liquidity could cause clearing prices to overshoot or undershoot the Smartbidder forecast. ECRS at $1.08-2.19/MWh (HE21-22) retains MEDIUM clearing probability — DAM could pass through to RT dispatch.

## Risks and Watch

1. **Primary risk — HE20 outage/load surprise**: Any unscheduled thermal outage or load exceeding 69.4 GW (e.g., temperature surprise in Houston) compresses the already-tight reserve margin (estimated ~6-7 GW). A 1-2 GW unexpected outage at HE20 could push DA prices toward $60-80/MWh vs $44.65 base.

2. **Solar forecast divergence**: Enverus vs YE gap of +1,577 MW is large and unresolved. Watch morning satellite updates. If cloud cover is higher than Enverus forecasts, solar underperforms and midday net load rises while evening ramp is less steep (less solar to lose) — mild bullish midday, bearish evening. If solar overperforms YE, the inverse.

3. **Overnight P(DA<RT) = 60-66% — short-DA signal**: HE00-04 overnight window has consistent directional lean toward RT being higher than DA. BESS charge window (HE01-04 low prices $25.72-27.15/MWh DA) aligns with this signal. Wind ramp overnight (GR_WEST to 7,225 MW by HE24) supports lower RT prices in HE24-HE03 than the DA settles.

4. **Saturday DAM liquidity**: Fewer market participants on Saturday means price formation is more susceptible to outlier bids. AS prices especially (Non-Spin, RegUp) could be more volatile around clearing. Non-Spin $12.19/MWh at HE21 is an elevated offer — monitor if it clears or gets repriced.

5. **ECRS DAM clearing probability MEDIUM**: ECRS $1.08-2.19/MWh at HE21-22 is within the range where DAM clearing is uncertain. ECRS has historically been passed through to RT more frequently than RRS or Non-Spin when DAM procurement is thinner (Saturday). Do not count ECRS as certain DA AS revenue.

6. **D+2 forward signal (2026-06-07, Sunday)**: Weekend demand typically steps down further Sunday vs Saturday. If outage schedule holds, D+2 reserve margin improves. Preliminary assessment: lower risk day, likely sub-$35/MWh DA peak. Monitor Sunday forecast vintage once available.
