Without AG2 Windcast IQ or Enverus STPF meteorological cross-validation, the duration of this low-wind regime cannot be assessed. 3. **Solar Cliff at -14,771 MW — Steepest of Series**: Each day the solar exit at HE19→HE20 has deepened by ~200-900 MW. On 08/16 it reaches -14,771 MW (the largest cliff so far). The corresponding HE19→HE20 net load ramp of +11,746 MW requires ~11.7 GW of thermal to come online or reduce load in a single hour. Generator commitment errors at this cliff are almost guaranteed.
