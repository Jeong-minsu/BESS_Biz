SCED re-dispatch flexibility in RT is limited when load ramps against wind-heavy supply that created cheap DA bids → RT clears above DA systematically.

**Peak conviction hours HE05-07**: Tyba P(RT>DA) 70-75%, spread −$7.99 to −$10.58. HE06 is the strongest DEC signal of the overnight block (raw 75.1%, spread −$10.58). **Congestion note**: congestion-analyst finds WEST_TO_NORTH_345 blended P(binding) 8-15% at HE01 under cross-source conflict (YE SCI 0.274 LOW vs Enverus-implied SCI ~0.37 MEDIUM).
