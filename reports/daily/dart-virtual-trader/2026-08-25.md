This is a directional override signal analogous to prior cycles where market-analyst explicit language adds +2 ppt.

Sizing conservatively at 10 MW (half of standard 15–20 MW MEDIUM weekday size) to calibrate the MILD tier WDPEAK pattern before scaling.
