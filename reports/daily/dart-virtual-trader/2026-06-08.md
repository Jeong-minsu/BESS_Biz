## Spread Derivation Methodology

### HB_WEST Hours (HE09-15)
Spread estimate = System DA-RT spread (Smartbidder) + E[MCC HB_WEST]

- System DA-RT spread at solar trough hours (HE09-15): Smartbidder shows -0.7 to +0.4 (system is flat to negative)
- E[MCC HB_WEST] HE09-17: -$8.0/MWh (congestion-analyst Stage 0 heuristic; range -$2 to -$22)
- At HB_WEST, the convention: SHORT DA means selling DA at HB_WEST price (which is high due to import constraint) and buying RT at HB_WEST (which is depressed by West surplus). spread(HB_WEST) = DA(HB_WEST) - RT(HB_WEST) = [system_DA + MCC_DA_WEST] - [system_RT + MCC_RT_WEST]
- When WEST_TO_NORTH binds: DA at HB_WEST is elevated relative to system; RT at HB_WEST is depressed (surplus trapped). The net effect is that SHORT DA at HB_WEST captures both the system-level DA premium AND the nodal premium from constraint binding.
- P(win) for HB_WEST uplift: computed as Bayesian uplift from system P using P(binding)=35-55% and conditional spread estimates. Formula: P(win) = P(system_win) + P(binding) × incremental_win_probability_from_binding.
- Confidence factor: 0.80 (Stage 0 heuristic, not Smartbidder-confirmed nodal)

### HB_HOUSTON Hours (HE17-22)
Spread estimate = System DA-RT spread (Smartbidder) + E[MCC HB_HOUSTON]

## Risks

1. **Monday load return-to-work overshoot — positive for evening, ambiguous for midday**: If realized load exceeds 79,000 MW (P~20%), HOUSTON_IMPORT P(binding) escalates to 45-65% and HE19-21 spreads at HB_HOUSTON could exceed +$15/MWh (P90 range per congestion-analyst). However, higher load also means more imports into Houston which could increase RT at HB_HOUSTON if the constraint binds hard — but in that scenario DA was priced even higher, so the SHORT DA position still wins. Net effect: load upside is bullish for evening positions.

2. **Wind outperformance compressing midday West surplus**: If GR_WEST realizes above YE forecast (Yes Energy mean: 22,595 MW system; but AG2/Enverus are -900 to -1,425 MW BELOW YE, not above) — counterintuitively, the two external sources forecast LOWER wind than YE, making the YE forecast the most bullish for the West surplus thesis. Actual wind below YE (the external consensus) means LESS West surplus, LESS constraint pressure, and LOWER MCC. Risk is that if wind comes in well above all three forecasts (3,000+ MW above YE), the SCI is higher but the constraint may already be binding hard — this is a second-order effect. Primary risk: wind undershoots all sources, NL rises, West surplus DECREASES — this is the tail risk for HE09-15 positions. Probability: LOW given YE is already the bullish outlier.

3. **Evening wind mutes HE21-22 scarcity**: GR_WEST at HE21 = 14,497 MW (YE) is substantial — higher than the 2,372-7,225 MW range seen on weekends. This partially offsets the solar exit at HE21. The congestion-analyst specifically notes that "high evening wind from West partially mutes HOUSTON_IMPORT vs 2026-06-06." This is why HE22 is sized MED (25 MW) not HIGH, and why HE21 spread ($6.5 est) is conservative vs the system +$3.0 Smartbidder (relying on MCC component only — not assuming energy spread expansion from extreme scarcity). If GR_WEST evening wind delivers 16,000+ MW, the HOUSTON_IMPORT constraint may ease in the second half of HE21 and HE22 could be a loss.
