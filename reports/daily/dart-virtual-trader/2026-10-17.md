## Why

### 1. Overnight DEC (HE01-09) — Saturday Pricing Anomaly

DA for Saturday Oct 17 was set in the Friday DAM (~10:00 CT Oct 16). With AG2 forecasting 16.7 GW ERCOT wind overnight and Saturday demand ~10% below weekday average, market participants cleared overnight hours at $3-15/MWh — the lowest overnight prices in the Oct 2026 Stage 0 series. Tyba RT P50 for these same hours is $16-32/MWh. The P_RT_gt_DA = 83-93% for HE01-07 reflects a structural floor in RT pricing that DA cannot reach:

- ERCOT minimum-gen requirements (~4-5 GW thermal floor) maintain some RT energy price even when VRE oversupply is severe
- Balancing energy costs (regulatory obligations, reserves) add a premium to RT that DA's energy-only clearing does not capture on a per-hour basis
- Saturday occupies a unique low-demand regime where DA under-prices not because of extreme events, but because of weekend demand suppression

This is a different mechanism from prior overnight DEC signals (Oct 11 and Oct 16 overnight DEC required D+3 carry-in western congestion story at SCI ≥ 0.350). No western congestion story exists for Oct 17 overnight (SCI 0.308-0.364 at HE01, LOW conviction). The Oct 17 overnight DEC is a pure probability-driven signal from Tyba GKS.

Adj P range 63-88% across HE01-09 surpasses any prior overnight DEC signal in Stage 0 series (prior max adj P for overnight DEC: Oct 11 HE01-06 at 55-56%). This is likely due to the extreme DA/RT differential ($3 DA vs $23 RT) rather than an extrapolation concern — RT would need to go negative to lose the DEC at these DA clearing levels.

### 2. Evening INC (HE19-20) — Peak Hour Conviction

Highest conviction evening INC signal in Oct 2026 cycle. DA $82.46/MWh reflects DA market pricing in scarcity from: solar-off (31,580 MW → ~0 by HE20), wind trough (2,660 MW HE19), and net load surge (~35,984 MW HE19). Tyba RT $60.94/MWh implies thermal response is sufficient and/or Saturday demand reduces the realized scarcity below what DA priced. The +$21.51/MWh spread at HE19 is the widest positive spread in the Oct 2026 forecast cycle.

SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT binding (P 15-45% HE19-21, MEDIUM-HIGH per congestion-analyst with Saturday discount) would increase RT at South nodes. Positions placed at GKS (North zone, MCC ≈ −$0.5 to +$0.5 at HE19-21) to avoid HB_HOUSTON basis risk. If constraint binds, GKS remains at hub-level pricing.

## Risks

1. **Overnight wind materially below forecast (primary downside for DEC HE01-09)**: If ERCOT wind falls below 14 GW by HE01 (vs 16.7 GW forecast), RT prices could drop lower than Tyba P50 → smaller DEC profit, but direction (RT > DA at $3-4) still likely correct. RT would need to go BELOW $3-4/MWh to flip direction → requires actual sustained negative SCED prices, which is unusual in overnight ERCOT.

2. **Wind materially above forecast (secondary risk for DEC HE01-09)**: Very high wind could push RT prices near zero or negative → DEC could lose (RT < DA $3-4). This is the primary loss scenario for overnight DEC. A 10% upside surprise in wind from 16.7 GW → ~18.4 GW would increase this risk. The congestion analyst notes SCI 0.308-0.364 at HE01 (LOW WEST_TO_NORTH) — if actual GR_WEST is higher, WEST_TO_NORTH could bind and suppress RT at West nodes, but GKS (North zone) would remain premium.

3. **Saturday liquidity / bid clearance**: DEC bids at $10-22/MWh against expected DA $3-15/MWh should fill comfortably. Liquidity in Saturday DART market is lower than weekday — positions at 10-15 MW are within typical weekend volume. No clearance concern at these sizes.

4. **Evening INC: RT spike risk at HE19 (primary downside for INC)**: If wind underperforms further below 2,660 MW forecast + thermal cold-start delays, RT could spike well above DA $82/MWh → INC loses. P_DA_gt_RT = 69.9% (raw) implicitly assigns 30.1% probability to RT > DA. Tail risk: RT > $120/MWh would create a large loss. Position sized at 15 MW (Saturday full-size) limits exposure to $15 × ($82 DA clearing − actual RT settlement).

5. **SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT HE19-21 binding (risk for INC at GKS)**: Constraint P(binding) 15-45% at HE19-21. When bound, RT_HOUSTON rises but GKS (North zone) is isolated from this effect (MCC ≈ 0 at HE19-21). Positions at GKS correctly avoid this basis risk. If positions were at HB_SOUTH, this constraint would narrow/flip the spread.

6. **Stage 0 magnitude uncertainty (systemic)**: All spread magnitude estimates are Tyba P50 and may not reflect realized spreads. For DEC overnight, spreads could be 30-50% different from P50 ($23 RT vs possibly $15 or $30). Direction confidence HIGH (adj P 64-88%); magnitude confidence MEDIUM (Tyba-sourced, single-source).

7. **DEGRADED state (ongoing)**: Smartbidder MSAL expired/terminated Day ~84. −5 ppt penalty eliminates HE10-18 DEC and HE17-18 watchlist positions (adj P 47-52%, all sub-floor). Duck curve hours (HE10-13) specifically blocked by penalty — without penalty, HE10 adj P 55.6% would be AT FLOOR for min-size entry.
