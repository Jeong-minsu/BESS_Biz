In this scenario, HE20 RT might actually fall below DA — making the DEC position lose. 4. **WEST_TO_NORTH overnight binding (HE01-03)**: P(binding) 20-32% with SCI 0.382-0.429. If W2N binds in DA, West node DA prices will be structurally different from North. For a HB_NORTH DEC position at HE20, this has no direct effect (different time window). But it signals active constraint pressure for the flowday, which can affect overall RT settlement patterns.

5. **Sep 14 HE14-style RT spike in HE13-15**: Sep 14 showed a brief RT > DA at HE14. Midday-afternoon hours on high-load days can generate short-lived RT spikes if solar-to-load transition creates brief reserve tightness. No position recommended in HE13-15, so no direct exposure.
