## Risks

1. **HE20 ORDC/scarcity risk (primary)**: The +9,260 MW single-hour NL ramp is the mechanically largest stress event of the day. If ORDC activates in RT (reserve margin falls below ACRC during the ramp) or if SCED dispatches an unexpected generator trip, RT at HE20 could significantly exceed DA. Holiday thin market amplifies pricing volatility. This is the highest probability loss scenario for the INC positions.

2. **Enverus upside at HE21**: Enverus NL = 59,002 MW vs YE = 57,598 MW (+1,404 MW). If Enverus is the more accurate forecast, RT at HE21 will be under more demand pressure than DA anticipated → P(RT > DA) increases. This partially explains the conservative P(win) estimate of 63% for HE21 despite it being the NL peak hour.

3. **Holiday thin market (Independence Day Saturday)**: Fewer active participants in both DA and RT auctions means wider bid-ask spreads, more idiosyncratic price movements, and higher variance on spread outcomes in either direction. All P(win) and E[spread] estimates carry wider confidence intervals than on a weekday.

4. **GR_WEST afternoon forecast accuracy**: GR_WEST at 3,393 MW at HE15 is an Enverus STWPF estimate. If actual wind is higher (say 5,000+ MW), NL at HE19-21 would be lower → smaller INC spread → E[spread] overestimated by ~$2-3/MWh. Conversely, if wind is even lower, RT spike risk at HE15-17 intensifies.

5. **Basis (HOUSTON_IMPORT) holiday discount**: The congestion-analyst has discounted P(binding) by 8-12 percentage points from the weekday base. If the discount is understated (holiday actually behaves more like a weekday evening), basis positions upside increases. If binding doesn't occur, basis P&L = ~$0.
