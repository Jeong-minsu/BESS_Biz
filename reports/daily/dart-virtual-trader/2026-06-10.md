## Spread Derivation

### HB_WEST Hours (HE10-12) — Basis Trade

The HB_WEST positions are a NODAL BASIS trade, not a pure system-level DA-RT trade.

Spread formula: **E[DA_WEST - RT_WEST] = System DA + E[DA MCC premium_WEST] - System RT - E[RT MCC depression_WEST]**

Key inputs (HE10 example):
- System DA HE10: $15.92 (Smartbidder, PRODUCTION)
- System RT HE10: ~$13.25 (Smartbidder LMP5)
- System spread: +$2.67 (positive — this is more favorable than 2026-06-09 where midday system spread was NEGATIVE)
- E[MCC HB_WEST | unconditional]: -$9.0/MWh (congestion-analyst Stage 0; range -$2.5 to -$25.0). MCC sign negative means RT at HB_WEST is depressed below system RT.
- RT(HB_WEST) estimate: $13.25 - $9.0 = $4.25
- Spread HB_WEST: $15.92 - $4.25 = **+$11.67 ~ +$11.9** (conservative)

When WEST_TO_NORTH binds (P=22-38%): MCC conditional -$18 to -$45/MWh; spread expands to $20-35/MWh.
When WEST_TO_NORTH does not bind (P=62-78%): E[spread] = system spread ~+$2.67; still positive (small win).

P(win) = P(bind)×P(win|bind) + P(no bind)×P(system DA>RT)
= 0.30×0.95 + 0.70×0.60 = 0.285 + 0.420 = **~62-63%** (consistent with model)

Today, the system spread is POSITIVE at midday (+$2.67-3.10 per market briefing), adding a second layer of support. This makes HB_WEST slightly more favorable in the non-binding scenario than yesterday.
