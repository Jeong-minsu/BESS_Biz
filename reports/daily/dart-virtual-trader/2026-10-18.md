## Why

### 1. Overnight INC (HE01-09) — Sunday DA Overnight Premium

Tyba GKS DA prices for Sunday Oct 18 overnight ($26-45/MWh) are fundamentally different from Saturday Oct 17 overnight ($4-15/MWh). The structural cause is not clear: GR_WEST overnight HE01-08 is only 2,469-3,047 MW (LOW, SCI 0.08-0.12), so wind is not suppressing DA the way it did on Oct 17. Sunday demand is low but so is wind. The congestion-analyst labels this as "possible Sunday market microstructure, Tyba model seasonality, or a shift in the bid-close clearing pattern."

Despite the anomaly flag, the signal direction is unambiguous: DA $35-45 vs RT $4-12 → P_DA_gt_RT = 93-100% → adj P 88-95% → INC wins overwhelmingly if the Tyba forecast materializes. The only loss scenario is if actual DA clears materially lower than forecast (below RT delivery). Given that a 100% probability Tyba signal implies virtually no cases where RT > DA, the risk is model error rather than fundamental uncertainty.

The Tyba threshold solver (2026-09-21) showed that for P_DA_gt_RT ≥ 0.98, historical OOS (Jun-Sep 2026) win rate was only 48.6% — statistically unreliable. This is the primary reason for the 0.70 confidence weight and 10 MW size cap. Direction is filed but with explicit caveat: if actual overnight ERCOT Sunday DA clears at $15-20 rather than $35-45, the INC spread narrows but positions at $22-40 offers may not even clear (benefit: automatic loss limitation).

### 2. Duck Curve INC (HE10-12) — Solar Overgeneration

The most mechanically straightforward block. With 31.8 GW solar (Yes Energy) or 29.5 GW (Enverus), net load drops to 7,655-9,316 MW (EXTREME tier, cross-source validated). RT prices near-zero (HE10 $0.14, HE11 −$0.41, HE12 $1.86) reflect overgeneration and curtailment management in real-time. DA prices stay at $18-22 because the energy-only DAM cannot price in zero-MT dispatch — AS clearing and bid-stack shape maintain a positive floor.

INC offers at $14-18 clear DA at $18-22, settle RT at $0-2. Net spread $16-22/MWh. Adj P 76-90%. This is the cleanest signal of the day. October duck is SEVERE and confirmed by two independent solar sources; DUCK_CURVE_MIDDAY P(binding/hr) = 55-72%.

Historical analog: Oct 9 INC duck (NL 24,251 MW SEVERE, adj 60%, 20 MW HE11). Oct 11 INC duck (NL 979 MW UNPRECEDENTED, adj 60-63%, 20 MW). Oct 18 NL 7,655 MW is EXTREME tier, between those data points. Adj P 76-90% reflects higher confidence than prior duck entries because Oct 18 has no concurrent WEST_TO_NORTH complication (GR_WEST SCI 0.09-0.10 during duck = LOW).

1. **Overnight model error (HIGH risk for Block 1)**: If Tyba's overnight DA forecast ($35-45/MWh) is a model artifact rather than a real market signal, actual DA clearing could be materially lower. 4. **WEST_TO_NORTH HE22-24 — tail watch, not adverse**: GR_WEST HE24 = 9,984 MW (SCI 0.335, threshold 0.350). 7. **Duck curve depth — solar forecast divergence**: Yes Energy 31,788 MW vs Enverus 29,486 MW (+2,302 MW gap). Both confirm EXTREME duck. Lambda magnitude uncertain ±30%. Direction of INC (DA > RT during duck) is robust across both forecasts. Spread magnitude may be 30% smaller if Enverus is correct, reducing duck block E[net] from ~$592 to ~$415.
