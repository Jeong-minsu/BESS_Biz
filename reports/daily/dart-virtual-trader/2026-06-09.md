Spread = System DA-RT + E[MCC HB_WEST]

- At HB_WEST specifically: WEST_TO_NORTH HIGH (25-40%) binding creates a local basis differential. When binding: DA at HB_WEST is elevated above system DA (constraint premium in the DA market); RT at HB_WEST is depressed below system RT (surplus energy trapped west of the constraint). Net effect: DA(HB_WEST) - RT(HB_WEST) = POSITIVE even when system-level spread is negative.

- Spread formula: DA(HB_WEST) - RT(HB_WEST) = [system_DA_HE10 ~$15.56] - [system_RT + MCC_component]. If system_RT ~$15 (flat to system DA) and RT(HB_WEST) is reduced by MCC $10 → RT(HB_WEST) ~$5. Spread ~$10.5. This is the central estimate.
- GR_WEST trough: 11,083 MW at HE08 (below 11,500 MW absolute trigger). Recovering to 11,319 MW HE09 but still in trigger zone at HE10. This is the hardest West wind trough in the June 2026 tracked series, providing the strongest SCI-based WEST_TO_NORTH signal to date.
