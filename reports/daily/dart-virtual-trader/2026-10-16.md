## Why

### 1. D+3 Carry-in — DEC at HB_WEST HE01-06 (onset_early logic)

Oct 15 HE24 GR_WEST = 18,299 MW (SCI 0.614 — all-time Stage 0 record at time of Oct 15 filing). DAM for Oct 16 closes at 10:00 CT Oct 15, when the overnight wind surge was fully visible. DA for Oct 16 HE01-06 has complete information about expected GR_WEST ≥ 18,544 MW → DA fully prices in WEST_TO_NORTH congestion → DA_WEST is heavily discounted (very negative). In RT, ERCOT proactive curtailment management prevents prices from going as negative as DA priced → RT_WEST > DA_WEST → spread < 0 → DEC at HB_WEST wins.

This is the `onset_early → DEC` rule documented in learnings/2026-10-14.md §8 (where D+3 carry-in for Oct 16 was explicitly flagged as "STRONGLY ACTIVATED"). Oct 16 HE01 GR_WEST confirmed at 18,544 MW — the central estimate from that filing (14-17k MW) was exceeded on the upside.

### 2. Overnight North Premium — INC at GKS HE01-05

WEST_TO_NORTH binding creates DA premium at NORTH nodes (HB_WEST cheaper than HB_NORTH). Tyba P_DA_gt_RT = 63.9-69.7% for HE01-05 at GKS confirms DA_GKS > RT_GKS. These two legs (DEC HB_WEST + INC GKS) together capture the WEST-to-NORTH basis differential — the same structure as Oct 11 filed positions for HE01-06.

### 3. Dual-Stressor Midday — INC at HB_WEST HE10-12

October 16 is the first flowday with negative net load in Stage 0 series history (NL = −345 MW at HE14). The combined EXTREME duck curve (solar peak 25 GW per Yes Energy) + VERY HIGH WEST_TO_NORTH (SCI 0.622-0.629 at HE10-12) creates a congestion severity that DA could not fully anticipate at 10:00 CT cutoff (solar had not yet peaked; GR_WEST already high and included in DA pricing but duck depth unknown). In RT, SCED executes near-zero or below-zero dispatch with no historical analog → RT_WEST pushed to extreme negative prices beyond DA pricing → DA_WEST > RT_WEST → spread positive → INC at HB_WEST wins.

Congestion analyst rates this as HIGHEST CONVICTION signal in Stage 0 series history (dual-stressor). MEDIUM decision weight (Stage 0 provisional). Tyba independently confirms INC at GKS with P_DA_gt_RT = 65-69% for HE09-11 — cross-node corroboration.

Key distinction from Oct 11 DEC at HB_WEST: on Oct 11, GR_WEST was sustained all-day (onset_early for the entire daytime block → DEC wins). On Oct 16, the DUAL-STRESSOR (solar + wind together) creates a midday overgeneration depth that DA cannot price at 10:00 CT cutoff → onset_late-equivalent for the DUCK COMPONENT even though wind onset is early. The wind alone is onset_early (DEC), but the duck combined with wind is onset_late (INC). INC wins at HB_WEST for HE10-12 based on the duck component exceeding DA pricing.
