## Why

### 1. HE07-08 INC — Morning Ramp DA Premium

**Mechanism**: The classic ERCOT weekday morning ramp. Load accelerates from ~41,230 MW (HE01 overnight) to ~48,788 MW at HE08 — a +7,558 MW buildup over 8 hours — while GR_WEST wind declines from 10,541 MW at HE01 to 6,184 MW at HE08 and solar remains effectively zero until HE09 (8,515 MW ramp begins). The combined load surge + wind decline forces DA to clear thermal ramp-up capacity at premium prices ($40.87 at HE07, $39.24 at HE08). RT, however, benefits from more flexible dispatch (SCED can re-dispatch in real-time as solar comes online early, thermal ramp uncertainty resolves, and load forecast errors wash out) → RT clears at $36.04/$33.35, the spread of $4.82/$5.90 represents this DA forward-loading premium.

Tyba P_DA_gt_RT: HE07 = 60.3% (raw), HE08 = 63.3% (raw). The HE08 signal is the highest probability for the INC direction across all 24 hours. Adj P (after −5 ppt): 55.3% / 58.3% — both clear the Medium threshold.

Congestion-analyst note: "Morning Ramp HE7-8 — DA Premium Flag" — brief constraint possible on North/Houston import paths. This congestion would be additive to INC direction (higher DA vs RT) rather than adverse.

Offer prices: $38 (HE07, ~7% below DA P50 $40.87) and $36 (HE08, ~8% below DA P50 $39.24). These ensure scheduling if DA clears at or above offer level. Buffer: HE07 offer fills if DA ≥ $38 vs P50 $40.87 (2.1 standard deviation buffer given ~$2 typical DA deviation); HE08 offer fills if DA ≥ $36 vs P50 $39.24.
