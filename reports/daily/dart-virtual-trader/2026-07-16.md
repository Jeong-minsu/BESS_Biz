## Why — Top 3 Advisory Hours (Analytical Deep-Dive)

### 1. HE02-04 Overnight Cluster (P=70%, E[|spread|] $3.5, Advisory E[P50] $46.2 for 33 MW)

**Thesis**: Overnight hours (01:00-04:00 CT) carry the strongest DEC signal of the day. Three compounding factors:

- **Wind 8,432-10,056 MW** (Enverus) / **9,200-11,400 MW** (AG2): Overnight wind is at the peak of its daily cycle. Higher wind reduces marginal cost of real-time power but does NOT reduce DA clearing prices (which are set before 10:00 CT). Result: RT < DA or, in this environment, DA is pinned high overnight ($20-21) while RT is under marginal wind cost pressure. However, the full-system load is 43-44 GW while wind supplies 9-10 GW — net remaining thermal/etc load 34-35 GW. This is manageable, but the RT clearing includes any imbalance from load forecast error.

- **High humidity, dew point 68-70°F**: Overnight temperature 76°F (vs normal 78-80°F in July — the model shows dew point 70-71°F on 7/16 at midnight). High humidity means A/C units continue running past midnight. This creates sticky overnight load that the DA model may under-forecast → RT slightly above DA. Note: on 7/15, overnight DFW dew point was 64-66°F, which is more moderate. The +4-5°F dew point jump overnight on 7/16 increases P(RT>DA) at HE02-04 relative to 7/15.

- **P(DA<RT) = ~70% (Smartbidder, market-analyst)**: This is the strongest directional signal available. At 70%, the DEC direction passes the "High conviction" threshold (≥ 60%) in the W:L matrix. Floor failure ($2.10 vs $4.00) is the sole barrier.
