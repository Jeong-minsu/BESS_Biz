Note on E[gross] formula: The formula used is E = [P × spread − (1−P) × 0.5 × spread] × MW = [P − 0.5(1−P)] × spread × MW. This is a conservative formulation assuming the loss scenario realizes half the stated spread in the adverse direction. ## Risks

### Risk 1: Evening Wind Underperformance → RT Price Spike
GR_WEST forecast at HE19-21: 4,529–6,635 MW STWPF. If realized wind at HE19-21 is 20-30% below forecast (e.g., 3,200–4,700 MW instead of 4,500–6,600 MW), RT prices at HE20-21 could spike above the DA clearing price → SHORT DA positions lose. This is the primary tail risk for the HE19-20 HIGH positions. Monitoring signal: AG2 vs YE wind agreement at HE20. Both show 10,757–11,875 MW ERCOT system total — high agreement reduces tail risk but does not eliminate it.

### Risk 2: YE Load 72,316 MW vs Enverus/AG2 70,180 MW Mis-Calibration
If actual load exceeds 72,316 MW (e.g., extreme AC demand at HE18-20), RT could run above $49/MWh at peak hours, inverting HE19-20 spread. Conversely, if Enverus/AG2's lower load (~70,180 MW) materializes, DA bid at ~$47-49 may clear but RT settles near $33-37 — spreads compress but remain positive (model's "Enverus NL" scenario above, still profitable).

### Risk 3: ORDC RT Scarcity → RT > DA at HE20-22
Non-Spin $11.93 at HE20 and $12.74 at HE21 are the highest AS forecasts in the tracked cycle. If ORDC scarcity adder fires in RT (e.g., operating reserves drop below 2,000 MW during HE20-21 transition), RT prices could spike to $60-$100+ → SHORT DA position loses on HE20-21. Probability: ~10-15% conditional on AS scarcity signal. GKS physical discharge at these hours (bess-optimizer domain) would hedge this risk from a portfolio perspective, but the virtual position itself would lose.

### Risk 4: HE18 Spread Compression
Spread at HE18 is +$8.80 but solar is still 15,262 MW at HE18. If solar outperforms Enverus (+3,897 MW gap vs YE), HE18 net load is lower than expected → DA may clear softer → spread narrows. At $8.80 base, there is buffer before hitting zero spread, but a HE18 skip (as tested on 2026-06-01) remains debatable at the margin. Sized at 25 MW MED (not 50 MW) to limit exposure.

### Risk 5: GKS Node vs HB_HOUSTON Basis
Trading at HB_HOUSTON (hub) rather than GKS node directly. GKS confirmed on Houston load side (2026-05-31). Stage 0 heuristic MCC for HB_HOUSTON +$3.0-$5.0/MWh at HE19-22. Hub trading introduces basis uncertainty: if GKS MCC diverges significantly from hub average, P&L may differ from HB_HOUSTON-based forecast. This risk is LOW given load-side confirmation.
