1. **HE21 NL series-high RT spike spillover into HE20/22**: The 53,670 MW NL at HE21 is the largest thermal demand recorded in the tracked series. If ORDC scarcity fires at HE21 and lags into HE20 or HE22 (real-time re-dispatch), RT could clear above DA in the adjacent hours. The 0.6x HOUSTON spread discount partially offsets this but does not fully hedge tail scarcity exposure at HE20.

2. **HOUSTON_IMPORT_345 right-tail**: Congestion-analyst assigns P90 MCC = +$22/MWh and P99 = $155+. If this constraint binds hard, HB_HOUSTON DA prices are set with the constraint in mind but RT can clear at a different (potentially higher) nodal price if the constraint wasn't fully cleared in DAM. Monitor ERCOT constraint clearing status.

3. **HE10 GR_WEST WEST_TO_NORTH floor proximity**: GR_WEST trough at HE16 is 11,768 MW — only 268 MW from the 11,500 MW absolute trigger (per congestion-analyst). If wind underperforms and the floor fires, WEST_TO_NORTH escalates to HIGH (35-55%) and West prices could go negative (very favorable for HB_WEST SHORT DA positions). However, during HE10 the floor has not yet been approached (15,773 MW) — this risk materializes later in the day, not at HE10.

4. **HE15 HB_WEST tail inversion**: If GR_WEST drops faster than forecast toward the floor at HE16, RT prices at WEST could spike upward (paradox: floor trigger can cause large price swings as the market clears the constraint). This would hurt the HE15 SHORT DA position. Half-size (25 MW) is the mitigation.

5. **Overnight HE01-09 Long DA omission**: P(DA>RT) is 43-56% in overnight hours — never crosses 58% threshold. The market-analyst explicitly notes overnight RT upside risk (high wind, demand surprises). Standing overnight suspension rule confirmed for this cycle.

6. **Model limitation — Stage 0 congestion**: All congestion-analyst inputs are Stage 0 heuristic (no ML model, no PTDF projection, 20th consecutive blocked cycle). E[MCC] figures are directional only. The nodal basis trade at HB_WEST rests on this provisional foundation.
