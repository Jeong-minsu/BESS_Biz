## Risks

### Risk 1: RT spike from ORDC scarcity at HE20-21 (MODERATE probability, HIGH impact on SHORT DA)

- **Trigger**: Unscheduled forced outage at HE20-21 coinciding with Saturday thin reserves. Thermal cap-out already at 13,878 MW avg; an additional 1,500-2,000 MW unscheduled outage at HE20 (scenario table: 5% tail) could push ORDC and lift RT above DA.
- **Impact**: If RT > DA at HE20-21, the SHORT DA positions at those hours lose (we sold DA and buy RT at a higher price). At 50 MW each and worst-case RT spike of +$20/MWh above DA, exposure is −$2,000 per hour. Combined HE20-21 tail exposure: −$4,000 gross.
- **Stop-loss trigger**: If any real-time outage alert for a 1,500+ MW unit posts before 10:00 CT (DAM bid cutoff), consider reducing HE20-21 sizes to 25 MW or skipping HE21 entirely.
- **Mitigation**: P=63-67% already implies the model assigns 33-37% to a loss outcome, some fraction of which is this ORDC scenario. The Non-Spin DA at $12.19 is itself a partial hedge signal — it means the forward AS market has priced scarcity, which often (but not always) captures the outage premium in DA.
- **Probability of adverse outcome (RT > DA at HE21)**: ~31% per Smartbidder model.

### Risk 2: Wind overperformance at HE19-22 (LOW-MODERATE probability, MANAGEABLE impact)

- **Trigger**: If ERCOT evening wind at HE19-21 realizes 2,500-3,000 MW above forecast (GR_WEST forecasts ~3,500-5,000 MW in this window), RT prices could be suppressed below $30/MWh while DA cleared at $34-44/MWh. This would actually widen the spread and HELP the SHORT DA positions — wind overperformance is only adverse if it compresses DA ex post by creating a surplus that the forward market didn't expect.
- **Note**: Wind overperformance at HE19-21 is directionally FAVORABLE for SHORT DA (cheaper RT), not adverse. The adverse wind scenario is wind UNDERPERFORMANCE, which would tighten supply, lift RT above the DA, and create losses. See Risk 3.

### Risk 3: Evening wind underperformance below forecast (MODERATE probability, ADVERSE impact)

- **Trigger**: GR_WEST falls 2,000-3,000 MW below forecast at HE19-21 (e.g., STWPF forecast is for ~3,500-5,000 MW; underperformance to 1,500-2,000 MW). Combined with 10,630 MW NL ramp already the sharpest in the cycle, this would push HOUSTON_IMPORT binding probability toward 35-50%, potentially spiking RT.
- **Impact**: P(RT > DA) increases. At the HOUSTON_IMPORT binding tail, E[MCC | binding] = +$8 to +$20/MWh at HB_HOUSTON — this is a positive DA premium that stays in DA, not a loss. However, if wind underperformance is severe enough to trigger RT scarcity (RT >> DA), the position loses.
- **Probability of this adverse chain**: ~15-20% (from congestion-analyst risk table: "evening wind underperformance: ~20%").

### Risk 4: Saturday thin DAM — DA price significantly below Smartbidder forecast (LOW probability, MANAGEABLE impact)

- **Trigger**: Saturday DAM liquidity is structurally lower than weekday. If fewer buyers participate in the HE20-21 DA auction, DA clearing prices may undershoot the Smartbidder forecast by $5-10/MWh (e.g., HE21 clears at $36 instead of $44.65). If RT also clears at $34 (forecast), the spread narrows from $10.19 to $2.
- **Impact**: Realized spread below $4 floor means what would have been a skip in hindsight. P&L would be marginally positive but far below model EV.
- **Probability**: ~10-15% for Saturday (vs ~5% for weekday).
- **Stop-loss**: No pre-trade stop-loss available once DAM positions are submitted. This is a post-settlement tracking item.

- HE23 at spread $3.80 (P=63.0%) was skipped per the $4 hard floor rule. If realized HE23 spread > $4 (actual spread > Smartbidder forecast), this is an opportunity missed. Track in self-review.
