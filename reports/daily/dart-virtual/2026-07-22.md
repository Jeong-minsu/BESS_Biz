**Conviction: HIGH.** Highest single-hour Smartbidder P in the current Stage 0 calibration history. Three convergent signals:

2. **Structural driver: DA prices in the scarcity premium / RT unlikely to fully match**: Duck curve NL ramp HE18→HE21 = +19,170 MW (YE), +15,205 MW (Enverus). Solar collapses from 25,863 MW at HE18 to 20,140 MW at HE19 to 741 MW at HE21 — a 25 GW collapse in 3 hours. The DAM must price for the ramp uncertainty. Once the ramp completes and thermal units are online, RT typically clears below DA's ex-ante scarcity premium. 3. **Node selection (HB_NORTH) insulates from SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT**: Congestion-analyst gives P(binding) = 42-58% at HE19-21. If binding, RT at HB_HOUSTON spikes (bad for INC at Houston). For the INC position at HB_NORTH, the binding scenario creates additional basis support (HB_NORTH RT higher than system average is offset by HB_NORTH DA also being higher). The net effect on the HB_NORTH INC spread is ambiguous but less adverse than at HB_HOUSTON.

**DA-conditional veto override justification at HE19**: At DA=$100.47, an INC loss requires RT > $100.47. For RT to reach $100+ with system wind at 6,465 MW (HE18) and forecast thermal dispatch at full commitment, either (a) SOUTH_HOUSTON_IMPORT binds at extreme severity AND system-wide scarcity activates, or (b) a major thermal forced outage triggers ORDC. P(both a AND b) is embedded in the 25% INC loss probability. The standard veto (designed for DA=$30-50) would have blocked this trade despite DA already pricing the scarcity scenario. At $100+ DA, the trade is fundamentally "short the ex-ante scarcity premium" — a well-understood DART alpha source that should not be blanket-blocked by a rule calibrated for lower-price environments.

6. **Advisory-only status and veto override precedent**: The DA-conditional override at HE19-21 is the first formal application of the W29 pending rule.
