## Why

### Core Thesis — INC at HE19-21 (Short DA / Long RT)

**spread = DA − RT; positive → DA expensive → INC signal.**

The 2026-07-15 market structure creates a clear INC bias at evening peak hours (HE19-21) driven by three independent moderators of RT price relative to DA:

**1. Wind supply abundance at peak net load hours**
ERCOT total wind rises from 12,622 MW at HE19 to a cycle peak of 13,547 MW at HE21 — the wind is STRONGEST precisely when net load is highest. GR_WEST 5,608–6,806 MW at HE19-21 is well below the WEST_TO_NORTH_345 threshold (>12k MW below at all hours). The real-time market clears with this supply, suppressing RT relative to the DA clearing price that was set with some uncertainty about wind delivery.

**2. Temperature 7-9°F below seasonal normal**
AG2 DFW forecast 86-87°F vs. 94-95°F typical for mid-July. Actual load tends to come in below DA bid-close forecast on below-normal temperature days. If load at HE19-21 underruns by even 2,000-3,000 MW, RT clears with less scarcity than the DA bid-committed market anticipated → DA over-committed relative to RT → positive spread.

**3. NEGLIGIBLE HOUSTON_IMPORT (series lowest ramp ever recorded)**
YE NL ramp HE18→HE21 = +8,096 MW (series minimum, 6,765 MW below the confirmed non-binding floor at +14,861 MW). No transmission congestion premium is available to RT in the Houston corridor. Historical pattern: when HOUSTON_IMPORT binds, RT spikes well above DA in Houston; when it does NOT bind, DA commitment pricing tends to exceed RT clearing. Today's NEGLIGIBLE probability all but eliminates the RT spike scenario.

**4. No-Smartbidder regime probability model**

- Wind-to-net-load ratio: ratio of 0.26 (13.5 GW / 52.2 GW) at HE21 is high → INC-favorable

- Below-normal temperature: load-side underrun risk → RT moderation
